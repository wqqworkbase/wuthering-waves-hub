This weapon draws its design inspiration from the play—Waltz in Masquerade. A waltz is a dance of spectacle and masquerade, where the grand symphony drowns whispered secrets and swirling dresses conceal the exchange of letters and promises. The dancers speak a silent language, as smooth and precise as the perfect resonance of a rectifier.
Waltz in Masquerade is a Rectifier weapon in Wuthering Waves.
