This weapon draws its design inspiration from the play—Legend of Drunken Hero. From the cup flows a liquid that grants courage and strength, empowering the hero to defy fate and turn the tide with his gauntlets. Yet in this intoxicating delight lies peril-dulled senses become the hero's undoing as enemies close in. Thus, in those wine-stained pages, one always read about triumphs followed by defeats.
Legend of Drunken Hero are Gauntlet weapons in Wuthering Waves.
