A supplement storing a small amount of Tacetite Energy. It can be used in the core modules of Tacetite Weapons to increase power output. WEapons are the allies of Resonators and Tacetites serve as their food. Only by nourischind weapons well can they be handier and more powerful in battles.
Medium Energy Core is an Upgrade Material for Weapons that provides 8,000 Weapon EXP.
