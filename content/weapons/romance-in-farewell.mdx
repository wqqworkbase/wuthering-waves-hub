This weapon draws its design inspiration from the play—Romance in Farewell. This pistol was once the symbol of the aspiration for repute, the weight of unrequited love, and the tremors of unspoken words. When the trigger is pulled, and the curtain drops, it turns into a long farewell and a sign of lingering sorrow in the heart of the bereaved.
Romance in Farewell are Pistol weapons in Wuthering Waves.
