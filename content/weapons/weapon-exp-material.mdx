Description[]

Weapon EXP Materials are an item type belonging to the Weapons Backpack Category.
There are currently four items  of this type:
&#91;Notes 1&#93;

 1,000 EXP Basic Energy Core
 3,000 EXP Medium Energy Core
 8,000 EXP Advanced Energy Core
 20,000 EXP Premium Energy Core
How To Obtain[]

Simulation Training[]

Main articles: Simulation Training&#32;and&#32;Simulation Training: Energy Core 
Simulation Training is a set of 3 Challenges located in the Northern part of Jinzhou. Weapon EXP Materials can be obtained from the challenge named Energy Core. The amount and rarity of the materials rewarded for completing the challenge depend on the player's SOL3 Phase. Collecting the rewards costs  Waveplate ×40

Synthesis[]

Basic Energy Core and Medium Energy Core can be Synthesized from Floramber and  Shell Credits. The Energy Cores can be found in the Construction tab in the Synthesizer menu. Advanced and Premium Energy Cores are not craftable. 

Shops[]

Some Shops sell Weapon EXP Materials. These include Shilang who sells Medium, Advanced and Premium Energy Cores, and Souvenir Shops that will exchange Advanced Energy Cores for Wood-textured Shards. A more detailed and up-to-date list of shops can be found on each Weapon EXP Material's page.

Store[]

Weapon EXP Materials can be redeemed for free as well as bought in the Store.

- In the Level Bundles the ones rewarded for reaching Union Levels 10, 20, 60 and 70 contain Weapon EXP Materials.

- In Item Exchange Advanced Energy Cores can be bought with Oscillated Coral

- In Points Exchange Advanced Energy Cores can be bought with Hazard Records.

- There are also various paid Bundles in the Store and some of them contain Weapon EXP Materials. These Bundles can be bought with Lunites.
