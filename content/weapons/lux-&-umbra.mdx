Trivia[]

- The name of these weapons is a reference to a pair of pistols from the Devil May Cry game franchise named "Luce & Ombra", which are Italian words also meaning "light" and "shadow" respectively.
Etymology[]

- Both "lux" and "umbra" are Latin words meaning "light" and "darkness" respectively.

- The word "lux" is further derived from Latin's ancestral languages Proto-Indo-European léwks > Proto-Italic louks > Latin lux
