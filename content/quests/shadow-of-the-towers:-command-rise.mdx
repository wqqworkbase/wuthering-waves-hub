Steps[]

- Head to the camp to find Insula and Itzel

- Enter the Command Rise

- Go deeper into the Command Rise

- Pursue the Abyssal Patricius

- Enter the top of the tower

- (Optional) Talk to Itzel

- (Optional) Talk to Insula

- Leave the Command Rise

- Talk to your two companions

- Talk to Insula

- Talk to Itzel
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest awards the Footprints in Rinascita I trophy Shadow of the Towers
