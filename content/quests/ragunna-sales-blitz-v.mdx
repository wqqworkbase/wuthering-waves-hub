Steps[]

- Head back to Alannah and see what she thinks

- Ask Alannah about the reviews on the Echo plushies
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionHmm? Alannah isn't in town today?

(Head to the designated location)
Alannah: S-Stay back!

(After defeating the TDs)
Alannah: Thought I was a goner...
 How did you get surrounded by TDs?
Alannah: Well, as a member of the Pioneer Association, I should be out in the field doing actual research.
Alannah: So I came all the way out here for some inspiration. But I didn't expect this...
Alannah: Wait, there's a TD right next to that person over there. He might be in trouble. We need to help him!

(Talk to the man)
 I'm here to save you!
Alannah: Look out! There's a TD right next to you!
Lazy Teen: Oh? You mean Slowly? Slowly won't hurt anyone.
Alannah: You gave a TD a name?
Lazy Teen: That's right. Slowly, it strolls. Slowly, it lives.
 But it's a TD.
 Aren't you scared of it?
Lazy Teen: I'm here, just watching Slowly, chilling. Slowly's here, chilling, watching the scenery. We both take each other slowly.
Alannah: Y-You sure?
Lazy Teen: Why don't you join us and enjoy some sunshine?
Alannah: It's kind of scary...
By the young man's invitation, the three of you begin sunbathing with Slowy.
After getting plenty of sunshine, you see Alannah in deep thought. She then says goodbye and returns to Jinzhou on her own.
Lazy Teen: What do you think? Felt good, didn't it?
 It doesn't mean us any harm.
 I have achieved inner peace.
Lazy Teen: Quite nice living life this way, don't you think? Slowly, we stroll. Slowly, we live.

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)!
 Found any inspirations?
Alannah: I did! The way that guy treated the TD made me think about the relationship between humans, TDs, and Echoes.
Alannah: In Jinzhou, people fear and despise Tacet Discords because they have decimated countless homelands in the past.
Alannah: As for Echoes, they're mostly for utility purposes. Resonators defeat TDs and transform them into Echoes to enhance their capabilities.
Alannah: But in Ragunna, Echoes aren't just tools of warfare.
Alannah: Children buy Echo toys, and you see Echo marching bands in the streets. There are even octopus Echoes that strike the bells in bell towers.
 That's incredible.
 Sounds completely different from Jinzhou.
Alannah: Then it dawns on me that since the Ragunnesi love Echoes so much, making plushies of Jinzhou Echoes would definitely work!
Alannah: They'll sell like hotcakes!
 One vote for Gulpuff plushies.
 Hoartoise plushies, please.
After some hard work, Alannah manages to send a shipment of Jinzhou-styled Echo plushies to Ragunna.
Alannah: Ohhh, I have a good feeling about this one! I can't wait for the results!

(Talk to Alannah)
Survey Feedback: "What a cute Gulpuff stuffed toy!" "Why are they so spiky?" "I'd say our plushie Echoes are a lot cuter."...
Alannah: Hmm... The reviews are kinda mixed.
Alannah: There are all kinds of Echoes over in Ragunna, so it's understandable they can get pretty picky about them.
 It went better than it could've been.
 We're making good progress.
Alannah: Yep! Can't expect any product design to be a banger right out of the bat. We need to come up with even more and better ideas to attract the Ragunnesi's attention.
Alannah: I'll message you when something hits me again! Thanks for dropping by today, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)!
(Quest Complete)
