Steps[]

- Go to the place where you agreed to meet Fulmine

- Deliver Sonance Caskets to Teensy Weensy

Fulmine: This is exactly what I wanted to talk to you about! Could you try turning in a Sonance Casket? We need to confirm if the ones from Septimont can power the Morph Paintings too.

Fulmine: Mmm-hmm. It's confirmed. Septimont Caskets can be used to fill the Morph Painting's Sonoro Sphere.
