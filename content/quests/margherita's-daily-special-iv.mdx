Variant 1[]

Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionNot one to dwell on past failures, Lelia has thrown herself into her next attempt.

Lelia: Sorry, I'm afraid there's no taste test today.
 Didn't try anything new?
Lelia: I did! Today was supposed to be Margherita Pizza, but...
 Why so mysterious about it?
 It's alright. Let's see it.
Lelia: Are you sure? Okay... don't say I didn't warn you.
What lies before you can barely be called a pizza. Most of its surface is charred, with only a few spots that look remotely edible.
Carefully, you break off a piece that looks safe and take a bite.
Lelia: ...You're really trying it?
 It's... not too bad?
Lelia: I know you're just being kind. It's all right. I won't let this stop me from trying again.
 I've had better.
Lelia: It's all my fault. All these good ingredients have gone to waste.
Lelia: Guess I still need more practice with Bakie. I left it in for too long this time.
 Timing is everything.
Lelia: Got it. I'll take note of the cooking time for every single dish.
 Maybe set a timer next time?
Lelia: A timer sounds like a good idea. I always get distracted watching the fire and overcook the food.
Lelia: From now on, my goal is to master the art of not burning the food!
 Shouldn't you try to aim a little higher?
(Quest Complete)

Variant 2[]

Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionNot one to dwell on past failures, Lelia has thrown herself into her next attempt.

Lelia: You're here! I can't wait for you to taste what I cooked today!
 I've never seen you this excited.
 Was it a success?
Lelia: I've tasted it, and I'd say the seasoning is just right. Please enjoy, Egla Mushroom Soup.
 Alright, here goes.
You take a small sip. The warm soup has a rich, creamy flavor that makes you want to go back for more. You eat a few more spoonfuls.
Lelia: What do you think? It's made with some special ingredients.
 Not bad at all.
Lelia: To capture true Egla flavor, I got up early and went to the Gondola Market to buy wild mushrooms from Whisperwind Haven.
 Something about these mushrooms fells... off.
Just as you finish speaking, your vision darkens.
Lelia: Hey, are you okay?
 What the... who are all these people?!
 I think I must be hallucinating.
Lelia: Huh? Now that you mention it, I'm feeling a bit dizzy too, and my heart won't stop racing.
 Maybe skip the special ingredients next time?
Lelia: I'm terribly sorry! It won't happen again!
(Quest Complete)

Variant 3[]

Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionNot one to dwell on past failures, Lelia has thrown herself into her next attempt.

Lelia: Good to see you! I tried out a family recipe today.
 Oh?
 What do you have in store for me today?
Lelia: Papa used to make this for me when I was little, and I wanted to recreate those flavors. It's called Sweet and Tangy Risotto. Let me know what you think.
You take a bite of the steaming risotto, feeling like you've swallowed a ball of flames. A searing, intense heat ignites your mouth.
Lelia: Um... your face is turning red. Are you alright?
 Why is it so spicy?
Lelia: That's strange... Let me try.
Lelia: Oh no! I mixed up the gemberry jam with chili sauce!
 Be careful about mixing up your sugar and salt next time, alright?
Lelia: I'll fix it right now!
 Wait, water first...
(Quest Complete)
