Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYhoran, a member of the Black Shores Emergency Response Team, is currently testing new strategies against Tacet Discords. Defeat the targets and meet the special challenge goals to earn extra rewards prepared by Yhoran.
"When you're prepared, the Reaper will not come."

(After completing the challenge)
An emergency response team member runs toward you in haste.
Yhoran: Got a notification saying someone just activated the tactical trial... Phew, turns out it was you (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)!
Yhoran: I can't believe you took down those digital Tacet Discords so fast. Truly incredible speed.
Yhoran: Your combat records have met the special objectives. This will be a great help to my research. Please, take these as a token of my gratitude.
 Sorry, but who are you again?
 Combat challenge?
Yhoran: Ah, my apologies. I got carried away. I'm Yhoran from the Black Shores' emergency response team.
Yhoran: I'm researching combat strategies against Tacet Discords. The challenge you completed just now is part of my research.
Yhoran: As you can see, I've set specific challenge objectives with corresponding rewards for completion.
Yhoran: But it's probably too difficult... I've only gathered a few valid records so far.
Yhoran: Many gave up after a few rounds, complaining it was impossible to complete...
Yhoran: Given the situation, could you please complete the rest of these challenges for me?
 No problem.
Yhoran: Excellent. I've sent you the coordinates for the other challenges.
Yhoran: I'm looking forward to witnessing your combat skills.

(Talk to Yhoran)
Yhoran: Good to see you again, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Yhoran: Congratulations on completing all the combat challenges with perfection! Those legends about you certainly do you justice—you're an incredible combat master.
Yhoran: So how would you rate the difficulty of these challenges?
 Piece of cake!
Yhoran: Aha, just as I expected.
 A bit tricky.
Yhoran: Such modesty...
Yhoran: Anyway, thank you so much. Without your records, it would be a real headache to proceed with the follow-up study.
 Follow-up study?
Yhoran: Yes, it's focused on optimizing our teams' combat training.
Yhoran: By analyzing your records, we can better understand teh Tacet Discords' combat patterns, body structure, and preferred techniques.
Yhoran: With this data, we can design specialized training to improve efficiency and reduce casualties.
Yhoran: I used to ignore my mentor's advice about the importance of combat efficiency and strategy... You can't really understand it until you've been through it yourself.
Yhoran: Now that I'm in a position to mentor others, I'm finding it challenging to teach newcomers.
Yhoran: I'll be sure to study your combat records throughout.
Yhoran: Thank you so much for your help!
(Quest complete)
