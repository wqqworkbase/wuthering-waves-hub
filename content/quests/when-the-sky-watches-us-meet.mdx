Dialogue[]

After a while, you receive a message from Tulia. The two Scourgewings you rescued have appeared near Border Mountains. She's already there... Better not make them wait.

(Arrive in Septimont City)
Tulia: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), I just found traces of Iaen and Isth.
Tulia: If you're free, would you like to come and see them?

(Talk to Tulia)
Tulia: Ah, took you long enough to get here.
 Where are the Scourgewings?
 Where are they? Where are the dragons I saved?
Tulia: Isth and Iaen... they circled Capitoline Hill a few times before returning to their old trial grounds.
Tulia: Maybe they wanted to see how Septimont looks now, after all these centuries.
Rover: I wonder if they were pleased or disappointed with what they saw.
Tulia: Only they would know. Although judging from their roars, I'd say they were happy.
Tulia: After all, the night sky they soar freely beneath is just as beautiful as ever.
Tulia: Look&#8212;the echoes from the Ashen Dragon's Crypt are spilling out, painting everything around us.
Tulia: This wasteland, once swallowed by the Dark Tide, is blooming with new life.
Tulia: There they are.
Tulia: I saw something like this when I was a child. Back then, I lived with my family at the Gladiator's encampment night during the onset of a High Tide season.
Tulia: When the Tacet Discords came... everyone shouted for me to run. Don't stop. Don't look back. I ran. But I tripped on a hillside.
Tulia: Hundreds of them were closing in. I was so scared I shut my eyes. And then, I heard it. A deafening roar right above me.
Tulia: When I opened my eyes... the monsters were gone. All that remained was a glimpse of brilliant wings disappearing into the clouds.
Tulia: ...Seeing that Scourgewing was the first time I felt true wonder.
 Where will they go this time?
Tulia: Back to some ancient roost? Or to where the next tragedy waits to unfold?
Tulia: They've stood beside Gladiators in their final moments. Now, they carry those memories forward.
Tulia: I have a feeling... As long as we share the same sky, we'll meet again.
(Quest Complete)

(Optional - Talk to Tulia)
Tulia: I'm heading home for a while. To rest. To write. Isth and Iaen have given me more data than I ever dreamed of.
 Safe travels.
 I can't wait to see what you discover.
Tulia: ...(&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), meeting you on this journey has been a gift.
Tulia: To you, this might be routine. Just another rescue along the road.
Tulia: But to me, it means everything. I want you to know...
Tulia: ...I will always... remember you.
 Then add my name in your acknowledgments.
Tulia: Yes, I wouldn't dream of leaving you out.
