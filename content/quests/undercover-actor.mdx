Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou received a message. Talk to Langsheng.

(Talk to Langsheng)
 Hello, I received your message earlier.
Langsheng: So, you're the famous Rover, huh?
Langsheng: I'm contacting you on behalf of the Patrol Station. Sorry for the abrupt request.
 How may I help you?
Langsheng: Have you crossed paths with someone named Yihu lately?
Langsheng: He's the guy standing outside the teahouse, trying to reel in customers. Tall, thin, loves doing Guankoustand-up show.
 Yes, I know him. What happened?
Langsheng: Well...
 Did he get into something?
Langsheng: No, not like that.
Langsheng: Don't be fooled by his modest disposition. He's actually my informant from the streets.
 Your informant?
Langsheng: I've been working with him for years. Smart, gutsy, sharp-eyed, good with words, and reads situations well. I always thought he'd make a good Patroller.
Langsheng:  But all he's ever wanted was to make people laugh. He dreams of being a Xiangsheng Master and claims it's a tradition to perform Xiangsheng at teahouses, so he won't leave there.
Langsheng: You know, a teahouse is a place where people of all sorts gather. Lots of chatter and gossip. For I've helped him once a few years back, now he's doing this for me to return the favor.
Langsheng: He's my eyes and ears, telling me all the street rumors, which has been a big help in solving cases.
Langsheng: I'm currently on a case, and I need his info. However, every time I visited the teahouse, he signaled me to leave.
Langsheng: So I think something might have happened or someone's got their eyes on him. For his safety, I can't approach him directly.
Langsheng: I saw you shooting the breeze with him a few times before, so I figured maybe you could get close without blowing his cover?
 I see. I'll go talk to him for you.
Langsheng: Thank you. Just be careful, for both Yihu and yourself, not to blow your covers.
 Got it.
(Quest Complete)
