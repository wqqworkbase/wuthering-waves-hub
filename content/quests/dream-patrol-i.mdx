Steps[]

- Talk to the strange white cat in Ragunna City
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest unlocks the following Nightmare Echoes: Nightmare: Feilian Beringal, Nightmare: Impermanence Heron, Nightmare: Thundering Mephis, Nightmare: Inferno Rider, and Nightmare: Mourning Aix.
