Xieyang: For the last riddle, how about we change things up a bit? You two can pick one from any of the lanterns.

Yangyang: Umm... I don't really see any riddles...
Xieyang: Oh, the riddles here are written in special ink. You'll have to hold them close to a lantern in order to see.

Yangyang: Made up of many, but can also be one...

Xieyang: Ooh. Close, but not close enough!
Yangyang: ...I know. It's "Family", right?
 Is it... "Family"?
Xieyang: Correct!
Xieyang: Bravo. You've completed all 3 riddles. The special prize is yours!

Yangyang: It's so pretty...!

Xieyang: Well, it's just not any lantern.
 Yangyang, you seem to like it a lot.
Yangyang: I was little when I last received a lantern like this... I really miss those times.
Xieyang: We were lucky to get this lantern from a renowned collector for our special occasion.
Xieyang: It's said that Sentinel "Hsin" handcrafted it and hid many more in the woodlands, making it a prized possession. Finding one is like getting a year's worth of blessings.
