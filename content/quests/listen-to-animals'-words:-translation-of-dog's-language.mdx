Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou meet Xuanyin again, who is having fun with some puppies...

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: Hi, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), how's it going?
 I'm great. Rare to meet you in Jinzhou.
Xuanyin: Haha, true. After all, most of my research subjects are out in the wilderness.
Xuanyin: I feel a bit strange to rejoin a civilized society after roughing it out in the field. I prefer to play with dogs rather than deal with other people.
 So it's the Dog Translation Module this time?
Xuanyin: Yes, they're all my old friends, so it won't take too long to collect data as before.
Xuanyin: See this pit bull? Its white eyebrows are pretty cute, huh? That's why I call it "Heroplay".
 Is there a cause-and-effect relationship in this?
Xuanyin: Hahaha, actually, it's because it loves to hang out near the entrance of the Jinzhou Theater.
"Hero Play": Woof, woof, woof.
Xuanyin: Seems like it has something to say. Let's install the translation module and check it out.
Xuanyin loads the Canine Language Translation Module onto your Pangu Terminal.
"Hero Play (Translation)": Woof, woof, assemble, assemble!
Xuanyin: Why did it run away? Slow down! Slow down! I can't catch up with you!
Xuanyin: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). Use your Sensor to scan for its whereabouts.

(Follow the Traces to find the puppies)
Bulldogs (Translation): Let's play! Let's play!
Speedydogs (Translation): Happy! Happy!
Zhuxi: Where have the kittens gone? Can't find any lately. Days without fluffy foot pads are so grueling... lucky the puppies are here.
Yingqi: Dogs are not a "substitute" for cats.
Zhuxi: I know! I love them both!
Yingqi: Fine, as long as you're happy.

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: Seems like the thing the dogs are most concerned about is their two-legged friends.
 Like they've been trained.
Xuanyin: Some dogs, after training, can take on complex tasks. That's the original purpose of humans domesticating them.
Xuanyin: It's surprising that dogs and wolves share the same origin but are different. Dogs lack the ferocity of wolves, you know.
Xuanyin: They've established a deeper connection with humans.
Xuanyin: Wolves hunt in packs in the wilderness, whereas dogs find a home in human households, viewing their two-legged friends as lifelong companions.
Xuanyin: That's why they conceal their sharp teeth and claws.
 Is this your insight or...
Xuanyin: So touching... I can't help crying!

Xuanyin: The translation module...
 Already uninstalled. Need more tissues?
Xuanyin: You're so considerate, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). I'll go back and analyze the data. Goodbye, friend. Sobbing.
