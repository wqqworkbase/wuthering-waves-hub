Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYongxin's first attempt ended tragically. Will a miracle arise on his second try?
(Head to Spring's Taste)
Yongxin: There you are, Rover
 Your face is glowing red.
 And that's a lot of sweat
Yongxin: It... it's too hot here for sure. That's hot springs for you. Even the air feels hot...
Yongxin: Hello... I am...
Xinyang: Here you are, finally. Tongchuan Pharmacy sent you, right? Do you have any idea how busy I am today, and you just got here? Where's my shipment?
Yongxin: Shipment...?
Xinyang: The medicine I ordered for spa therapy. Wait, why are you empty-handed?
Xinyang: And Rover... when did you start working for Tongchuan Pharmacy?
 We're here to make sales.
 You got us mixed up with someone else.
Yongxin quickly explains his intent.
Xinyang: Oops, my mistake. He didn't look familiar to me so I thought he was from Tongchuan Pharmacy.
Xinyang: But I don't have time for that right now. As you can see, I'm swamped here... And the fact that the medicine from Tongchuan Pharmacy still hasn't arrived yet makes it even worse.
Xinyang: Oh, right! Can you check on the delivery for me, Rover? If I don't have the spa ready soon, I'll get complaints.
 No problem.
Xinyang: Yes! I knew I could count on you!
(Obtain herbs from Tongchuan Pharmacy)
Zhonglin: You're a rare sight, Rover.
Zhonglin: Here for some herbs? I just got a fine batch in from Jinzhou. They work wonders for both internal and external use.
 I'm here for the herbs for Spring's Taste.
Zhonglin: Oh my, can't believe that slipped my mind.
Zhonglin: I was going to send someone over this morning, but the guy called in sick. Xinyang must be getting impatient by now...
Zhonglin: Let me see... Yeah, should be these. Thanks for delivering them, Rover.
(Hand the herbs to Xinyang)
Xinyang: You're back, Rover?
Xinyang: Yes, this is it. I spent a lot of money on this new recipe. Now, all I have to do is put it in the hot spring.
Xinyang: Dip in the hot spring just for fun, twice to dodge the flu and run, thrice for muscles feeling fine, four times to make sickness decline!
Xinyang: Oh, the fellow over there... What did you want to see me for again?
Yongxin: Excuse me, but... do you need stones for your hot spring?
Xinyang: Stones? You mean for renovating the pool? No, because we're in peak season so it will cost me a lot of money to renovate now.
Xinyang: How about rockery, got any of that?
Yongxin: Yes, yes! We... do...
 Tell me about your best product.
Yongxin: ...Well, our rockery comes in a variety of styles, such as the Jinzhou border style, the Mengzhou garden style, and the newest luxury style from Huanglong capital. We also offer customization based on your own design, dear customer!
Yongxin: Dear customer, you can order now for free shipping to Hongzhen, plus a three-year warranty! If you have any questions or need more information, please leave a message and I will...
 Ahem. You can stop right there.
Xinyang: ...Is this how people from Jinzhou sell stuff nowadays? Sounds a bit weird... but it's actually kind of appealing?
 The best bang for your buck, dear customer.
 Your hot spring deserves the enticing look, dear customer.
Xinyang: Hm... Indeed, I do think the spring is missing something, so if I added this...
Yongxin: Phew, I guess that's one deal closed. Now I can face the boss.
 You seem to be under a lot of stress.
Yongxin: Well, my boss was never optimistic about Honghzhen, or he would've never sent a low-man-on-the-totem-pole like me here.
Yongxin: But I did it... I've taken the first step, and that's because I have your encouragement and help...
 So, what's up with the "dear customer" thing?
Yongxin: Didn't you want me to say what I say best? That's all I know...
Yongxin: I still came off a little nervous, but much better than before.
Yongxin: Then I'll have to keep working at it. Thank you, Rover.
(Quest Complete)
