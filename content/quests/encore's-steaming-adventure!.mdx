Steps[]

- Head to the "Fireworks Shop"

- Find the ingredients needed

- Collect "Flarepowder"

- Find fire around

- Collect "Low Freq Charcoal"

- Deliver the ingredients to Xisha

- Find a place suitable for firework testing

- Use Utility - Skyshaker to blast the boxes

- Defeat the TDs

- Return to the stall and talk to Xisha
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is time limited.

- Completing the "Find a place suitable for firework testing" step awards the player Skyshaker*30.

- Completing the quest unlocks Encore as a Lodge Wishes companion in the By Moon's Grace limited time event.
