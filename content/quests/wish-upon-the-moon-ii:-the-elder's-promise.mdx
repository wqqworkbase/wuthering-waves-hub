Steps[]

- Go to the Wishing Stall and continue to prepare for the Moon-Chasing Festival

- Build "Yumyum Haven"

- Build "Fan Drawing Stall"

- Build "Moon Shooter"

- Decode the second Wish Tag with Patty

- Figure out the meaning of the second Wish Tag

- Visit Mrs. Heying at Xiehua Village

- Take Mrs. Heying to the Yumyum Haven

- Take Mrs. Heying to the Fan Drawing Stall

- Take Mrs. Heying to the Moon Shooter

- Complete the Moon Shooter challenge
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is limited.

- When completing the "Go to the Wishing Stall and continue to prepare for the Moon-Chasing Festival" step, the quest name changes from Festival Festival Preparations II to Decode corrupted wishes: II.

- When completing the "Decode the third Wish Tag with Patty" step, the quest name changes to Wish Upon the Moon II: The Elder's Promise.

- Completing the quest awards the player the Rover's Journey trophy Token of Promise.
