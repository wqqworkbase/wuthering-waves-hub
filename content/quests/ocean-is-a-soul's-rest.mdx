(Talk to Levito)
Levito: Sails of the departed drift on and on, souls above the clouds, hearts claimed by the sea...
Levito: Oh, hello, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
 Is that one of your new songs?
Levito: It is... this one came from a rather unusual experience.
Levito: Tell me, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). What do you think of the sea?
 Why do you ask?
Levito: Someone once said the sea is a city of the dead. You can hear the endless cries of the wind and waves, but never the voice of anything living.
Levito: When you spend days, weeks, maybe even years adrift, loneliness creeps in and twists despair into something monstrous. And yet, when you see a flicker of light out there—something you dare call "hope"...
Levito: That's when people stop thinking straight.
Rover: Is this from someone from the Fool's Troupe?
Levito: No, it was someone who's long gone now.
Levito: He died ten years ago in a shipwreck. I wasn't there to see it happen, and his face has almost faded from my memory. But...
 But?
Levito: On my last voyage, I saw him. He was on another ship. A lively one if I might add. I could hear the laughter and clink of glasses.
Rover: Hold on. Didn't you just say it's rare to see anything living on the sea?
Levito: Exactly. That's what makes it unusual. When I saw him, he looked just as he was all those years ago, not a single day older. And in that moment, everything I'd forgotten about him came rushing back.
Rover: Are you saying... he might still be alive?
Levito: Surviving ten years on the sea, without even a scratch? What do you think, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)? How likely is that?
 ...
Levito: Almost impossible, right? My thoughts exactly.
Levito: Yet when the light of "hope" is right in front of you, you can't help but chase after it. No matter how violent the waves or impossible the odds, you have to see it for yourself.
Levito: Sadly when we got closer, Battier and Brant reported back the waters were teeming with Tacet Discords.
 If another ship were to stumble across that area...
Levito: It would end badly. No doubt about it.
Levito: That's why I need your help, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Levito: I'll admit, this seems more of a personal request from my side. But when you're out there dealing with those Tacet Discords or trying to find the man, regardless of the results your safety comes above all else.
 What does this man look like?
Levito: He had the same blue hair and eyes as me. He used to call it the color of the sea, a blessing from the ocean itself.
Levito: After he died, I stopped believing in nonsense like these. Most sailors still prefer to hold onto their superstitions though, as a way to boost their spirits before heading off to sea.
Levito: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), I wish you safety... and the best of luck.

(Talk to Levito)
Levito: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), welcome back. How did it go out there?
 The place was overrun with Tacet Discords.
Levito: So it's already...
 I didn't see the man you mentioned.
Levito: Ah, I see... Thank you for trying, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). Can you tell me more about what you saw?
You describe what you saw to Levito.
Levito: Sea fog, never-ending tides of TDs, distant voices on the wind... (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), it reminds me of an old story I heard as a child.
Levito: Sailors, weary and broken after battling storms and endless waves, find their minds playing tricks on them. As I said before, loneliness can evolve into something unbearable.
Levito: In those moments, if a door appeared before them. Bright, warm, and inviting. Even if it led straight to death, many would still choose to walk through it. All for a glimpse of happiness that feels so real they'd gladly give everything for it.
 So these waters... is a trap. The TDs are luring people in.
Levito: I believe so. And the longer those monsters are left unchecked, the more lives they'll claim.
Levito: I'd like to ask another favor of you, though I understand you've got plenty of other tasks to tend to...
 I do have a lot going on.
Levito: I've learnt about your reputation of being quite a busy person. So don't feel pressured in taking on the task. Feel free to come by whenever you have the time to spare. I'll think of other ways to handle it in the meantime.
 But it's not like I can't make time.
Levito: That said... I do prefer that you make use of that time on some relaxation.
Levito: However, if you do lend me a hand, I'll make sure you're rewarded properly.
 Are you planning on a reward for me?
Levito: Of course, if that's what you hope for. I do not want any one else to have to experience the loss of hope any longer.
 ...
Levito: Don't worry. I've made my peace with the idea that he did die ten years ago. Maybe that's not such a bad thing after all.
Levito: Ten years have passed for me, but for him? He'll stay forever young. I think there's something beautiful about that—it's the kind of ending that belongs in a song.
Levito: Thank you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). I'll leave the rest in your capable hands.
(Quest Complete)
