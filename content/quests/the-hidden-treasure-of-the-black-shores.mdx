Steps[]

- Investigate the mysterious room

- Leave the "Coastal Room"
Gameplay Notes[]

- To unlock this quest, the player needs to interact multiple times with Black Shores Archipelago doors in a set order. You can see the order in this video guide

- Completing the step "Investigate the mysterious room" and claiming the chest in the room awards the player the Footprints of the Shores I trophy Old Family Squab Recipe.
