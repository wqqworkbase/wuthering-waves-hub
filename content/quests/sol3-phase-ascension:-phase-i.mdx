Steps[]

- Head to the Nexus Beacon in Jinzhou

- Complete the Phase Ascension Trial
Results[]

Completing this quest unlocks the following progression:

- Allows ascending Resonators and levelling them to level 50 as well as their weapons. It also unlocks the possibility to increase Forte (skills) levels.

- Allows further increasing the players Union Level to level 40

- Allows the increase of the Data Bank Level to 14. Databank level increase allows for higher tier Echo to be found in the world.

Note that on completion enemy levels are increased to level 40-50 in all areas. If you are having trouble defeating enemies after this and are unable to level up Resonators and weapons you can lower SOL3 phase by going to the menu and pressing the "i' next to your SOL rank.

Resonator and weapon ascension material can be obtained for this rank at the synthesis machine in Jinzhou, at the weapons store as well as the Souvenir store. Core materials are obtained through defeating Overlord class enemies. Note that completing a character's skill tutorial (found int he Guidebook) also grants some ascension materials.

Not completing this quest does not prevent gaining experience for Union or Databank level. Appropriate levels are automatically granted upon completion of this quest.
