Glossary[]

- Mingting - The capital of Huanglong. The nation's political, economic, and cultural center.

- Ministry of Sentinel Affairs - A special agency responsible for monitoring Sentinels, astronomy, and calendars across Huanglong's various cities. Led by the Minister of Sentinel Affairs in Mingting, it has subordinate bureaus in the other six cities, each headed by a Supervisor. While they hold independent authority on Sentinel-related matters, they are limited to advising and supervising the administrative bodies of each city and Mingting.

- Internal Security Agency - An institution that operates independently of the administrative, judicial, and military branches of Huanglong, with the highest official titled "Chief Commander." Reporting directly to the Emperor of Huanglong, it holds the authority to investigate any case, official, or criminal without approval from other agencies, with the power to "act first, report later." Tasked with handling intricate cases year-round, the agency has developed specialized expertise in managing Resonators.

- Grand Marshal - As the highest official in Huanglong's Ministry of War, the Minister oversees all military affairs for the six cities in Huanglong. The honorific title "Grand Marshal" is used to signify the rank and authority of this position.

- Censorate - One of Huanglong's three judicial bodies. It holds the authority to jointly try major cases and wields significant supervisory power, a duty summarized as "to probe evil and maintain discipline." Its officials are generally titled Censors, while those stationed or assigned temporarily in the six cities are called Investigating Censors.

- Fenrico the First - Just as the name enjoyed by Napoli the Second, it is a title Primus Fenrico should have rightfully possessed. However, due to unknown reasons, Fenrico forbade Acolytes from referring to him as such, until he met his end at the bottom of the sea. In the history of the Order of the Deep, he is the only one who bears the name "Fenrico."
