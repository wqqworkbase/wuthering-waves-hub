 Quest DescriptionLollo Logistics has partnered with the Pioneed Association to launch a new event with generous rewards. Go to the courier truck to learn more.

A Lollo Logistics vehicle with a strange paint job. Wonder who parked it in front of the Pioneer Association?
 Examine the vehicle.
The loudspeaker on the vehicle is playing an advertisement with funny music.
Lollo Logistics Truck: "Take out your Terminal&#8212;your trusty wish-granter! It's time to dive into some juicy perks again with Lollo Logistics' 10-billion-worth subsidies. Available to all Pioneers and couriers!"
