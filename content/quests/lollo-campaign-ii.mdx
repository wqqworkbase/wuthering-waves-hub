Steps[]

- Onboard yourself with Maji's business idea today

- Repair the control panel of the logistics courier

Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou have a new "Lollo Fixit" order. Please fulfill it in time.

(Onboard yourself with Maji's business idea today)
 What is today's pilot business?
Maji: A new business that suits just your capability: "Lollo Fixit."
 Repairperson Rover at your service.
Maji: I heard you fixed a lot of puzzles in the wild alone, including even those left by CSC. You should find no problem doing this job.
Maji: One of the worst nightmares during travels is the sudden breakdown of devices. For example, courier truck failures, short circuits of electric locks on courier boxes...
Maji: What would you do as an experienced repairperson to address these issues?
 Knock it hard.
 Reboot.
Maji: Ha, in that case, I can make an expert myself.
Maji's Terminal: Ding! You have a new repair order. Please fulfill the order in time.
Maji: A new order! An automatic courier truck went missing in the depths of Whining Aix's Mire. I'll send you the coordinates.

(Find the malfunctioning automated logistics vehicle)
 Punch it.
Broken-down Cargo Truck: Impact detected, impact detected. Ejecting copilot in 3141.5926 seconds...
 Reboot the control panel.
Broken-down Cargo Truck: Traveling to... Rinascita.
 Start repairing.

(Solve Signals Console puzzle)
Repaired, the courier truck shakily makes its way to Port Gunchao.

(Recap today's pilot business with Maji)
Maji: You're here just in time. The cargo truck made it to Port Gunchao in one piece, but it broke down completely shortly afterwards.
Maji: Here's your pay for today. You keep that momentum rolling for tomorrow, all right?
