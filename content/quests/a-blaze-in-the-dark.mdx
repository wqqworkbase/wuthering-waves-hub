Summary[]

In the streets of Ragunna, you come across a group of thieves who claim to be from the "Black Alley." Before you catch up to them, Zani appears and stops them in their tracks. She proposes escorting the thieves to the Chamber of Discipline with the patrolling Acolytes and making a statement. Meanwhile, you decide to stay behind to investigate a mysterious person calling for help.
You uncover some clues, but none lead you to the mysterious help-seeker. You decide to wait for Zani to return before continuing. After her return, she shares stories about a lone hero who single-handedly wiped out the Black Alley, a known criminal gang. This makes you suspect that the person might be connected to the Black Alley.

You and Zani reach the entrance of the Black Alley, and attempt to unlock the trapdoor, but it won't budge. Right at the moment, two masked thugs confront you and announces their plans to rebuild the Black Alley before they attack you.
After defeating the masked thugs, they reveal that the entranceto the Black Alley was destroyed by a ruthless woman weeks ago. Zani escorts them to the Chamber of Discipline and will reach out to you in the following day.

You receive contact from Zani, informing you that the thugs are led by a woman named Talos, who was believed to have vanished with the Black Alley years ago. Zani also mentions that the rumored hero behind Black Alley's takedown is referred to as the "Blazing Nightwalker." She then suggests you meet her in the city. You can sense her hesitation when mentioning that name.
You meet Zani near the ice cream cart in Ragunna. She shares with you that many of the Montelli family's staff have previously run into the same kind of trouble as that night. Zani asks for your assistance in locating the help-seeker, which is crucial to resolving the deadlock in this complicated case.
Before getting some rest in, you decide to inquire Zani for more information about the case. Seeing that she has experienced past events, she might help you piece together some parts of the puzzle.

You wake up in the middle of the night and find it difficult to fall back to sleep, so you decide to take a walk. To your surprise, you run into Zani, who also struggles to fall asleep. As you chat away, you uncover her identity as the Blazing Nightwalker. Zani was the one who once defeated that formidable gang.
After defeating the gang members, one manages to escape and takes Bakie from Trattoria Margherita as hostage. Just when the situation seems dire, Zani arrives in time and takes him out. And the mysterious help-seeker finally reveals themselves.
The mysterious help-seeker, Colleen, admits she is from the Black Alley, a place that was once home to the homeless but is now seized by Talos and her gang. Colleen warns that Talos possesses a Terminal of unknown origin. You and Zani are determined to stop Talos from exploiting its power.
Following the runner's route, you locate the hidden entrance to the Black Alley and confront Talos to put an end to this once and for all.

While in the Black Alley, you meet a drunken thug, but he ignores you completely. Colleen explains that Talos has been extremely cruel to her men. Talos has fixed up a device in the tunnel and uses it as a barricade to stop intruders and punish anyone who opposes her. Zani raises her shield as protection as you navigate through the tunnel.

Upon arriving at the living quarters of the Black Alley, you discover that it is empty. You begin searching the nearby areas for the residents. After gathering clues from the area, you realize that the residents of the living quarters were likely taken away against their will, to which you suspect by Talos. Out of nowhere, several gang members show up, and you decide to confront them for answers. You defeat the gang members and extract Talos's current location from them. However, they refuse to disclose the whereabouts of the residents and even go as far as to knock themselves out to prevent giving you further information. Sensing there might be danger, Colleen decides to search for the residents despite Zani's objections.

At the end of the tunnel, you and Zani discover the residents taken by Talos. Though unconscious, none of them are seriously injured. Hubert explains Talos's motives for taking the residents: She was planning on escaping Black Alley before you could reach her. Hubert is convinced that Talos is unstoppable: the miserable fate of the Black Alley residents has been sealed, and this place will continue to be a haven for criminals like Talos. On the contrary, Colleen believes that you and Zani hold the key to bringing about change. Determined to avenge its residents and eradicate the criminals dwelling within once and for all, you head out towards the depths of the Black Alley.

Zani defeats Talos, yet she attempts to justify her actions by asserting that she was merely adhering to the rules of the Black Alley. Using Talos's self-proclaimed rules against her, Zani then announces Talos's final judgment. With everything settled, you and Zani leave the Black Alley behind.

What made an ordinary Montelli employee become a hero with a dramatic alias? Was it for fame, a commitment to justice, or a desire for excitement in an ordinary life? Perhaps the truth lies deeper beyond...
