Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionAs the project nears its deadline, Alannah proposes one final plan...

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: I'm at the end of my rope, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Alannah: The project's deadline is coming up, and if I can't get it right this time... I suppose I'm not really fit for the job.
 Anything I can do?
 Let's give it one last effort.
Alannah: Thank you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Alannah: As I'm slowly reduced to ashes among the flames of my career, you are my saving light at the end of the tunnel...
Alannah: For our final topic of merch, I'd like to approach Jinzhou's culture and people's daily lives.
Alannah: Jinzhou's distinctive culture and traditional values are completely novel to the Ragunnesi.
Alannah: Let's go chat around with some Jinzhou residents!

(Talk to Uncle Dong)
 About Jinzhou's cultural life...
Uncle Dong: Jinzhou has plenty of plays, but it's people who truly understand them that are lacking.
Uncle Dong: Back in my day, those actors were real gluttons for hard work. And that made them great performers.
Uncle Dong: Nowadays, people would cheer for just about anything. None of 'em knows what makes for a good play!
Alannah: So you're saying the plays aren't as good as they were before, sir?
Uncle Dong: Not even close. Those youngsters are way off on their deliveries!
Uncle Dong: You young people need to have your eyes wide open to enjoy the really good stuff!
 You seem very knowledgeable about plays.
Alannah: If foreigners were to visit Jinzhou, would you recommend that they watch plays here?
Uncle Dong: More than happy to!
Uncle Dong: Jinzhou's hero plays have been around for so many years that they've formed a style of their own!
Uncle Dong: This old theater buff may sound picky sometimes, but I'll say the plays are more than enough to feast the foreigners' eyes on!
Alannah: Thank you for sharing these with us, sir.
Alannah: I think I got another idea, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! Let's head back!

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: That mister was pretty critical of those actors. However, I do have a feeling that deep down, he's really proud of them.
Alannah: He genuinely believes that Jinzhou's play culture is something the whole world should witness.
Alannah: Ragunna also has its own unique culture and traditions.
Alannah: For example, during the Carnevale, people will wear traditional clothing and masks with designs that represent their ancestry.
Alannah: It's a way of commemorating their homes and loved ones lost during the Lament.
 A very meaningful festival.
 I'd like to see it if I get the chance.
Alannah: See? Just as the mister said, one place's unique culture will definitely impress someone from a different land.
Alannah: For example, you got really hooked when I told you about the Carnevale!
Alannah: Now let's snap some hero play highlights and send them over to Ragunna!
A while after the hero play merch was sent to Ragunna...
Alannah: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! I have the reviews! Moment of truth!

(Talk to Alannah)
Survey Feedback: "Interesting stories." "I hope I get to see a live performance one day." "I want to be like Fiamma."...
Alannah: I suppose... we did it?
 Everyone seems to like it.
Alannah: Finally, our efforts paid off, and that's the end of my work!
 So we hit the three-million-copy sale?
Alannah: Hahaha. Not even close. The salespeople say the hero play merch will sell 30,000 copies at most.
Alannah: Actually, I was just informed that the manager who gave me the sales target was removed.
Alannah: The main office put someone new in charge, and she's really satisfied with what I achieved.
 Congrats.
 You earned it.
Alannah: Thank you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). I wouldn't have done it without you.
Alannah: If you hadn't been there for me till the end, I would've been in total meltdown a long time ago...
Alannah: Here, I got you a little something. Please take it!
Alannah: I'll be counting on you again if I land on another big project next time!
(Quest Complete)
