Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionAll of Yongxin's business plans ended in failure. Could there be a turning point waiting for him?
(Talk to Yongxin)
Yongxin: Hey, it's you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). Come, grab a drink.
Yongxin: I totally screwed everything up. These past few days have been a complete waste of time. I barely made any money, and I haven't been able to get a foothold in the market at all.
Yongxin: My transfer order should come through any day now. After that I'll be back in Jinzhou explaining to my boss how I messed everything up.
Brrring brrring...
Yongxin: Excuse me, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), I have to take this.
Yongxin has a brief exchange with a client through his Terminal. Though a bit nervous, you can see a true salesman emerging right before you.
 ...So, did you just land a big deal?
Yongxin: Yeah... I really didn't expect that.
Yongxin: He said he's a merchant from Mengzhou looking to invest in tourism around Mt. Firmament.
Yongxin: The mountain passes around Hongzhen have long fallen into disrepair and are no longer suitable for travel, so he plans to buy some stones and pave them up.
Yongxin: He actually did not consider buying from us at first, but he happened to meet that researcher in Hongzhen on a business trip.
Yongxin: After learning we supplied a batch of stones at a low price to support the scientific effort in Hongzhen, he seemed to think that demonstrated... a certain quality of our company.
 A blessing in disguise.
Yongxin: True. Good things tend to happen to good people, perhaps?
Yongxin: I'm glad I made the right call that day, all thanks to you, Rover.
 Nah, it was all you.
Yongxin: For as long as I can remember, whether it was my family, teachers or colleagues, no one believed I could achieve anything.
Yongxin: They would leave the most menial, insignificant jobs to me and never ask for my opinions.
Yongxin: If it weren't for you, who were willing to put faith in me and believe that even someone like me could do it...
Yongxin: I don't think... I would have never had the courage to make a decision.
 Why are you getting so emotional all of a sudden?
Yongxin: I'm probably just too excited because I finally got a big deal.
 It's not that big of a deal.
Yongxin: But I meant everything I just said.
Yongxin: Okay, things are going to get busy from now. Thanks for helping me out, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Yongxin: I learned a lot over the past few days. I feel like I'm ready to handle any sales opportunity that comes my way.
 Confidence is the key, after all.
 The beginning is always the hardest.
Yongxin: That's right. But once you succeed, it's much easier the second time around...
Yongxin: Oh, I gotta run back to Jinzhou. There's this big deal waiting for me to take care of.
Yongxin: Thank you, Rover. I'll definitely be the greatest stone salesman in all of Jinzhou when we meet again!
(Quest Complete)
