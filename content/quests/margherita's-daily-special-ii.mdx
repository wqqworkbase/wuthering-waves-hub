Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionLelia has been working at the restaurant for a while now. If you're hungry, why not stop by and see how she's doing?

(Talk to Lelia)
Lelia: Thank you for your patience! Here's your meal, please enjoy.
 How are you liking this job so far?
Lelia: It's been lovely. There's no better feeling than seeing a customer's smile after a good meal.
Lelia: Just last night, a lady who works at the bank tried our new pizza, and it worked its magic on her.
 Did she shake off the smell of the office?
Lelia: Exactly! It was like her weariness melted away. Amazing!
 I think I know who you're talking about.
Lelia: I see. This place is a go-to for bank employees. I'm also starting to recognize a few faces.
Lelia: Sometimes, I can't help but think... if only I could cook dishes that work the same magic.
 Maybe you should give it a try.
Lelia: Huh? Me, cooking? But I've never done it before. I thought that ship already sailed...
 Anyone can lean to cook.
 It's still at the dock if you're ready to board.
Lelia: Hmm... you are right. I always overthink but never act. I guess I need to just go for it.
Lelia: If I manage to cook something, could I ask you to taste it?
 Of course.
Lelia: Thank you! I'll keep your words in mind and do my best!
 It'll be free, right?.
Lelia: Oh, don't worry about that! Your feedback would be payment enough for me.
(Quest Complete)
