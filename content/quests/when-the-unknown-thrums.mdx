Steps[]

- Leave Honami City and wait for the Shorekeeper's reply

- Head to the rift in the Black Shores and enter Honami City

- Talk to Shorekeeper

- Follow Shorekeeper to the spatial tunnel

- Next Stop: Lahai-Roi

- Shake off the pursuing Voidworm
Summary[]

You finally receive a reply from Shorekeeper regarding the stabilization of the spatial tunnel to Lahai-Roi and tells you to meet with her at Shasho Cafe once ready.

Shorekeeper briefs you on Lahai-Roi. It's a region without a Sentinel to guide its civilization, and your frequency loss may be tied to a Threnodian-induced Waveworn Phenomenon known as the Void Storm. She explains that the Spacetrek Collective leads the world in frequency-stabilization research, and you might find a solution to your problem there. Before you depart, she hands you an acceptance letter. This time around, you'll be entering Startorch Academy as a student.

The spatial tunnel to Lahai-Roi allows only one person to pass through at a time. After thanking Namipon and Shorekeeper, Chisa goes first. You bid Shorekeeper farewell and leave under her watchful gaze.

You enter the rift, wandering through the dark void to suddenly have a body resembling you fall from above onto the ground. It suddenly unravels and grabs ahold of you, absorbing your frequency as you are faced against a giant black hole resembling an eye. You are then saved by a flash of pink light, revealing a girl doned in a white suit. After she playfully tap your nose and leaves, you watch the dark void become filled with stars.

An unknown girl shoots a researcher in red, walking away from the scene to her motorcycle. Falling through the rift, you finally make it out safely to only be struck by the girl earlier. She apologizes and offers to take you to the infirmary to check for injury, however, your meeting was interrupted by a storm, led by a giant Void Worm that gives chase.
Working together, you bring down falling rocks to slow the creature and make your escape.

Together on the motorcycle, the two of you bursts out of the cave into the vast, open view of Lahai-Roi. The girl introduces herself as Lynae, and together, you continue toward Startorch Academy.
