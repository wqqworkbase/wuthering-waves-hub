Steps[]

- Go to the designated location

- Find Fulmine

- Find the plushies

- Take a group photo of the plushies

- Follow the Wingray

- Gather the plushies for a group photo

- Take a group photo of the plushies

- Ride the Gondola

- Head to the next photo spot

- Take a group photo of the plushies

- Head to the next photo spot

- Defeat the Echoes

- Enter the painting

- Take a photo of the starry sky

- Return to the Hall of Swordsmanship

- Return to the Plushie Village
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest awards the player  Above the Stars andthe Exploration: Rinascita trophy Plushie Story.
