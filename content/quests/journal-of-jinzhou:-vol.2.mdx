Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is available 24 hours after completing Journal of Jinzhou: Vo.1.

- Only one of either Xingzhu's or Yingzhu's quiz can be attempted.

- The correct answer for Yingzhu's quiz are:

- Vanguards

- Pangu Terminal
