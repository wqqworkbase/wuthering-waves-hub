Amidst the Stars (Quest) is a Side Quest that occurs in Black Shores Archipelago, The Black Shores, [[{{{nation}}}]].

Steps[]

- Talk to KU-Cass

- Head to the area indicated by the clue

- Search for clues

- Take a photo of the Black Shores' main complex for KU-Cass

- Go to the Tethys' Deep

- Head to the Eidoscope to search for clues

- Use the Sensor to search the Greenhouse&#32;&#91;sic&#93; for clues

- Head to the Modulation Hall to search for clues

- Use the Sensor to search around the Modulation Hall for clues

- Inspect the ancient Terminal

- Head to the Bank of Life to find the last clue

- Inspect the Shorekeeper's illusion

- Record the last location for KU-Cass
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is limited.

- Completing this quest will award the player the Rover's Journey trophy Pale Blue Dot.
