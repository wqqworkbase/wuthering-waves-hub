 Quest DescriptionYou meet Maji from Lollo Logistics in Ragunna. What new campaign is he planning?

(Talk to Maji)
Maji: A real-time exchange rate, plus a 0.02% variable fee, plus 10% GST, and a courier fee... What the actual... Oh! If it isn't my old friend, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! Unbelievable, we meet again!
 Well, if it isn't good old Maji!
 Why the sudden change in tone?
Maji: Haha, call it the magic of Ragunna! The allure of classical aesthetics and culture is simply too tempting to resist!
Maji: So, do you still remember the trial "Lollo Navigator"? I navigated my way to Ragunna with it, and it turned out to be incredibly useful. It even sparked a new idea! Care to hear about it?
 I'm all ears.
Maji: Alright, picture this: You see something you want, but it's unavailable where you live. Or maybe you find it, but the price is inflated beyond reason. Or maybe the price is acceptable, but you don't have the luxury of time to get it yourself…
Maji: Where there's a need, there's a market! That's why I came up with a new campaign called "Lollo Errand"!
 Sounds very nice.
 Sounds rather profitable.
Maji: Haha, exactly! The time I've spent on it has already paid off. I've already completed several orders!
Maji: Right now, I'm handling an order for some flour from Ragunna. An astute client discovered the price here is half of what they pay in Jinzhou, so they've been buying flour from Ragunna for quite a while!
 Really?!
Maji: Amazing, right! They used to get the flour from a mysterious man wearing sunglasses, but he told them his ship was lent to someone else, so no errands for now…
 Your astute friend right here knew that a long time ago.
Maji: Aha! As expected for someone as sharp as you!
Maji: This client said they used to get the flour from a mysterious man wearing sunglasses, but he told them his ship was lent to someone else, so no errands for now…
Maji: But later, they found out about Lollo Logistics's new service and reached out to me for help. That's how this all came together! Interested in joining me this time, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)? Buy 10 bags of flour, and you'll earn a nice errand fee in return!
 I never say no to bounties.
Maji: Wonderful! The customer has already made their payment, and I'll transfer it to you right away.
 If you pay upfront, I'll handle it.
Maji: Haha, of course! The customer has already made their payment, and I'll transfer it to you right away.
Maji: By the way, you might want to check out the Gondola Market. I hear they've got a special fortified flour. I'll be waiting for you here!
