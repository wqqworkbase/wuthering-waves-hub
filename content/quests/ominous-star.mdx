Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the "Investigate the Tattered Wooden Plaque" step will award the player Wooden Plaque: Left.

- Completing the "Head to the direction Yangyang pointed to" step will award the player Wooden Plaque: Right, fusing it immediately with Wooden Plaque: Left to create Restored Wooden Plaque.

- Completing the "Investigate the diary pages under the tree" step will award the player A Diary and the Rover's Journey trophy "Buried under the Peach Blossom Tree...". It will also remove Restored Wooden Plaque from the player's inventory.

- Completing the quest will award the player the Rover's Journey trophy Shepherd Game.
