Summary[]

You unexpectedly overhear two Exiles discussing a lost package during transit, with one of them intent on confronting Lollo Logistics about it.

Upon arriving at Lollo Logistics, you notice the Exile departing after receiving the promise that his package will be retrieved.

You engage in conversation with the Lollo member, named Lumi, who reveals that strange events have been occurring in the Whining Aix's Mire, where her team member lost the package. She asks for your help in recovering it, and to investigate the anomalies, you agree to assist her.

In the dream, you come across Lumi, but it appears she can't hear you.
