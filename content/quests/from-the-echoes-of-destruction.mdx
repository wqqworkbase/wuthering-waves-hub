Summary[]

Yangyang has sent word that a Bloom Bearer from the Black Shores is currently in the Port City of Guixu and may have important information about your identity. Upon your arrival in the city, you happen to run into Chenpi's apprentice, Youhu...

In the Port City of Guixu, you meet Niya, a Bloom Bearer who quickly recognizes you. She warns of an abnormal frequency detected in Guixu that must be urgently brought back to the Black Shores. If left unresolved, it might trigger another Lament...

Upon arriving at the coordinated provided by Niya, you find the bodies of several Black Shores members who have died under mysterious circumstances. There, you encounter a mysterious Bloom Bearer named Hoda, who claims to know about your past but cannot divulge the details. Before disappearing, Hoda directs you to Youhu for further clues...

Youhu reveals that the clue Honda mentioned is an artifact from Mt. Firmament, discovered by her and Master Chenpi. Now, it seems connected with the Black Shores. The frequency tied to Hoda is hidden within this artifact, and it seems linked to the deaths of the Bloom Bearers. Reporting this matter to Niya and the Black Shores is now a matter of urgency.

As you traverse the Sonoro Sphere, you finally arrive at your true beginnings. Yet, the person who waits here remains beyond your grasp...
