Summary[]

The Lioness of Glory Arsinosa gives you and Lupa her blessing. As your hand touches the Guardian, you once again feel that strange energy emanate from her. With Arsinosa's recognition, you follow her to the Vale of Glory, the resting place of Septimont's heroes&#8212;the source of the abnormal energy you felt.

You begin to piece together everything you've learned about Septimont, Lupa, Cartethyia, Mya, the Dark Tide, and Arsinosa. As Mya comes from Ragunna, you believe she was under the influence of Leviathan, and that It aims to corrupt Lupa, if it is not already done. No matter how this carefully crafted story ends, you will act to correct its course.

When you touch the sword of Tercules, a Septimontian hero, Cartethyia's voice abruptly rings throught the air. Just as suddenly, the world around you falls once again into anomaly.

As you follow traces of the Dark Tide, Lupa loses control to Leviathan. After making her regain consciousness, you tell her to read the script and adapt as needed, as she is the protagonist of this story.

Lupa wakes near the Central Hub. All at once, she remembers everything. Cartethyia, the fall of Septimont, and the Realm of Plays. The story is starting again. Lupa steels herself and heads toward the monstrosity possessed by the Threnodian.

Lupa arrives at the flooded Old District, the waters stirring memories of when she once walked these streets with Cartethyia. Lupa and Cartethyia's journey together comes to an end when Lupa is "erased" from the story. But before she vanished, Cartethyia imparts a frequency that binds Lupa's fate with yours. Cartethyia confirms that the creature she's hunting is hiding deep within Septimont's underground waterways.

Lupa resolves to drain the Old District's flooded streets to reach the underground waterway where the monster waits. With you and Cartethyia's hopes resting on her, she seeks the truth behind Mya's disappearance decades ago. Lupa decides to use the Giant of Glory to drain the water from the Old District and enter the underground waterway.

Inside the underground waterway, the copper mirror reveals Julia Silva striking a deal with the Playwright to recover Mya's remains, the same Mya who fell into the sea decades ago. It also reveals Phrolova speaking with the Playwright. Mya was the Threnodian's creation who resisted Its control by throwing herself into the sea two decades ago. After the events at the Inverted Tower, Leviathan's lingering power sought refuge in Mya's corpse, allowing the Playwright to make contact with the Threnodian and begin their machinations. The copper mirror reveals the Lady of the Sea, Mya's Threnodian-corrupted remains, speaking with a lingering Reverberation of Mya's soul. Seeing Its "daughter" continue to stubbornly reject Its control, the Lady of the Seal grows displeased and silences her.

The confusion that has churned in Lupa's heart begins to ease, thanks to you, Cartethyia, and Mya. The light of clarity strengthens her resolve into a keen edge. She raises her spear at the awakened Lady of the Sea.

After defeating the Lady of the Sea, you, Cartethyia, and Lupa reunite and exchange the truths you've learned. The three of you arrive at the Central Hub where you learn Imperator left behind a message before merging with Leviathan. The message points to the hunting grounds in Septimont. A storm brews on the horizon, but after a long journey, it's time for a moment of rest.

Mya's sword lies among the countless weapons in the Vale of Glory, just like every hero who once fought for Septimont. They stand guard over a city drenched in both victory and defeat, holding both disappointment and hope.

Glossary[]

- Blightcloud - An omen of the coming Dark Tide, one that has lingered over Septimont for years. The region it shrouds is known to Septimontians as the "hunting grounds."

- Gryphon's Fortress - A majestic name for the city of Septimont, perched atop Capitoline Hill. Its people are renowned as master sculptors, skilled hunters, and awe-inspiring gladiators who celebrate heroism.

- High Tide - One of the signs that heralds the coming of a Dark Tide. It's when countless Tacet Discords swarm beneath the Blightcloud, descending upon Septimont in waves of destruction.

- Vale of Glory - The final resting place of Septimont's heroes and legendary gladiators. Stone monuments, shaped after each fallen hero's signature weapon, stand tall in tribute to ensure their names and deeds endure through the ages.
