Steps[]

- Head to "Moon Shooter"

- Head to the first challenge

- Complete the first challenge

- Head to the second challenge

- Complete the second challenge

- Return to the stall and talk to Qingxi
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is time limited.

- Completing the quest unlocks Chixia as a Lodge Wishes companion in the By Moon's Grace limited time event.
