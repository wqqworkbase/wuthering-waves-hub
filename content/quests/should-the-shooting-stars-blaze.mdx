Steps[]

- Follow the Nimbus Wraith

- Complete the trial

- Summon and follow the Wingray

- Enter the Path of Bestowal

- Talk to the Nimbus Wraith

- Take the elevator down

- Use the Gravity Calibrator

- Embrace the challenge

- Defeat Capitaneus

- Defeat Capitaneus once more

- Use the "Door of Unity"

- Search for the light spark at night

- Talk to Cantarella

- The light spark appears again

- Fly with the light spark

- Find the light spark again

- Find the light spark

- Catch up to the light spark

- Meet the light spark again

- Rescue the light spark

- Teleport to the flying light spark at night

- Ride the elevator

- Talk to Cantarella

- Board the Gondola to enjoy the performance
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest awards the Exploration: Rinascita trophy Wish Upon the Stars.
