Steps[]

- Investigate the radio on the crate to start the quest

- Investigate the robot

- Use Sensor to investigate surroundings

- Repair the robot

- Find the spare parts

- Follow the trail

- Find the supply pack

- Defeat the Exiles at the base, and pick it up.

- Find the motor

- Complete the Shooting Party Quest
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the Shooting Party Quest is required.
