Steps[]

- Head to the "Wishing Stall" and create even more festival vibe (Raise the Popularity to above 20,000)

- Tour around Taoyuan Vale
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is time limited.

- Rewards will be received after the "Tour around Taoyuan Vale" step.

- When completing the "Tour around Taoyuan Vale" step, the quests Chasing the Moon, Here and Now and Moon Shall Be My Guest will be available on the map.

- You can choose which route for Chasing the Moon's Glory to complete; you will be able to do the other later.

- Completing the quest awards the player the Moments with You trophy Paint Me As Your Friend.
