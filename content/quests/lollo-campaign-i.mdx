Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou have a new "Lollo Hitman" order. Please fulfill it in time.

(Talk to Maji)
 What is today's pilot business?
Maji: I worked all last night to launch our first applet, "Lollo Hitman"! This app helps protecting people from the Exiles.
 Is this legal?
 I'll call Patrollers.
Maji: Let me explain. I know not all the Exiles are bad guys.
Maji: "Lollo Hitman" helps protect people from the Exiles while traveling alone.
 That sounds nice.
 Hopefully it's legal.
Maji's Terminal: Ding! You have a new hitman order. Please go beat up the bad guy in time.
Maji: An order has been placed. Let me see the details...
Maji: "I was robbed by a group of Exiles in Wuming Bay, and they threatened to destroy all my stuff if I dared to call the Patrollers. Please, help me!"
Maji: Darn! Yashaa, do you want to be an ally of justice?
 I will make them pay.

(Talk to Maji)
Maji: A five-star review! Look, the buyer just posted a review.
Maji: "Rover looked so cool! 'Lollo Hitman' will be my go-to app when I run into the Exiles again!"
Maji: A good start! Let's see the next order...
Maji: Strange... Why are the orders all canceled? Anything wrong with the system?
Maji inspects the codebase and contacts the buyers who canceled the orders.
Maji: It's not a problem with our code. The buyers canceled their order.
 Why?
Maji: The reason they canceled the orders are the Exiles who picked the quarrel with them came and apologized...
Maji: All their apologies read like: "We return all your possessions and accept liability for your mental damage, lost wages and nutrition costs, but please, could you cancel that order?"
Maji: Some even said, "Scold me or hit me if you like, but please don't call 'Lollo Hitman'... Not that Rover, please!"
 ...
Maji: Didn't know you were so famous in Jinzhou.
 Sorry, it seems I scared off your potential clients.
Maji: Don't be. I've seen a lot of cases when I was a courier in remote regions, in which Exiles just took advantage of outnumbered travelers and picked on them.
Maji: However, little can be done to fight back when one suffers their raid. That's why I had this idea about "Lollo Hitman."
Maji: If everyone could just stay in their own place, there would be no Exiles who deserve lessons.
 You had quite an experience...
Maji: Ha, that's years ago.
Maji: With you here, I think the Exiles will stay put for a while. We can remove "Lollo Hitman" from the app store for the time being.
Maji: This is your pay for today, and remember, there is a new business to test out tomorrow.
(Quest Complete)
