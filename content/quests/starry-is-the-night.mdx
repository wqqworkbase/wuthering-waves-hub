Summary[]

The always calm Roccia reaches out to you with an urgent request. Go to the Strand and check on the banished "pilgrims".

You repel the Tacet Discords, saving both the pilgrims and the troupe members. Roccia thanks you and explains the origins of the pilgrims. Together, you and Roccia rescue the pilgrims. Some are badly injured, others are starving, and none are in good shape. Pero discovers a dying little girl in the cabin. You return to the troupe, where Tina is preparing pasta. Meanwhile, Roccia is busy patching up the little girl. You decide to go lend a hand. Roccia carefully tends to the little girl. She then asks about the condition of the other pilgrims. Tina informs you two that the others have been fed and are recovering from their injuries, though they remain mentally drained.

You encourage the introverted Roccia to ask the pilgrims directly about their well-being and explore how else she can help. Filippo’s arm has mostly recovered, but he remains deeply depressed. He recounts how Ragunna, under the Order's rule, strictly regulated theatrical performances. He and others were exiled for performing the banned opera, "Pegasus". During their exile, many of their companions perished in perilous encounters, leaving the survivors unable to continue the beloved opera. Giulio, another exile, shares his longing for the freedom celebrated in "Pegasus" and his deep nostalgia for his departed partner.

After speaking with the pilgrims, Roccia concludes that their biggest wish is to perform "Pegasus" with their companions once again. As a stage prop maker, Roccia resolves to collect and repair the objects left behind by her partners so she can participate in the welcoming opera alongside them. Roccia begins setting up the stage and preparing props for the welcoming opera. You help her inventory the items Pero brought back to determine how they can best be used. Roccia reflects on a lunachord left behind by the Pilgrim’s Sail. Its owner sought to ease everyone’s hearts through music, and they performed till the last second of their life. Though the lunachord has lost its strings, Roccia replaces them with something new, transforming it into a stage prop to inspire others.

Roccia reflects on the ship debris. These fragments once belonged to a Pilgrim's Sail—a vessel that carried everyone’s fear and confusion, while also recording their resilience and defiance. The debris is large and well-suited for use as scene props for the stage. Roccia tells you about a petal—a fragment of a wreath that once belonged to a little boy who didn’t survive the shipwreck. The petals are soft and fragile, and Roccia carefully considers how they might be incorporated into the welcoming opera. Roccia shares with you the significance of Luceanites. Legend has it that each Luceanite is a soul trapped on the Strand. If left exposed in the wild, the stone loses its light and must be collected in time. Bright Luceanites are ideal for creating star clusters to decorate stage ceilings.

The initial stage setup is complete, but Roccia notices that the stars aren't shining brightly enough. The North Star, a symbol of hope in "Pegasus," should shine even brighter. Roccia recalls Filippo mentioning that people sang under a star on the boat and decides to ask him for more information. Filippo reveals that the star belonged to Luca. Pero, sensing that Luca may have woken up, goes to investigate. Luca cries upon realizing her star is lost. She believes it has always been the star that blessed everyone and that misfortune strikes when the star is gone. You and Roccia comfort the weak Luca and primose to help her find the star. You and Roccia search the ship but find no star-like objects. Objects imbued with emotional frequencies resonate with their users, so Roccia decides to use familar items to help the pilgrims. She resolves to create a new "star" to perfect the "Pegasus" ensemble. For Roccia, "possibility" means "hope".

You find a Luceanite, which Roccia carefully stores away. Meanwhile, Pero suddenly perks up, as if it's made an important discovery. Pero finds footprints that emit star frequencies, though they're jumbled and hard to decipher. You volunteer to investigate futher. As you venture deeper, the rising mist surrounds you, forming a maze. The open door seem to invite you in. Roccia realizes the misty maze resembles a scene from "Pegasus." Together, you conclude that the maze must be man-made and decide to explore what lies within. At the end of the maze, you find a cavern shrouded in mist. Inside, the pitch-black cavern is filled with stars and something you can't recognize. You venture into the cavern, and Roccia—who is usually calm—shows fear of the dark. With you by her side, she steadies herself. Inside, the cavern opens into a wide space where a group of Pluhie Echoes are busy at work. Could this so-called "Demon King" really just be a bunch of Plushies?

The Plushies are defeated, but they seem to have something they want to say. It turns out the Plushies are abandoned Echoes, and all they want is to reunite with their owners. Even after reaching this desolate island, the Plushies couldn't find their owners. Instead, they take comfort in the emotional frequencies lingering on objects and begin piecing together "homes" from these fragments of memory. That's how they discovered the "star". Roccia believes both people and Echoes need a place to return to. You offer to bring the Plushies back to the Fool's Troupe with you. Lottie Lost's owner used to sing "Pegasus" to inspire those who suffered injustice, and Lottie learned it too. The maze you encountered earlier was their attempt to recreate scenes from the play, blending the foggy seaside environment with its imagery. You return to the troupe with the Plushies. Roccia suggests letting them to help set up the stage. You tell Luca that her star has been found. Luca, overjoyed, lets you lend her star for the welcoming opera.

Back at the stage, the Plushies reveal a startling truth: their owners never abandoned them—they were exiled. To protect their Echoes from the Order, which sought to erase their frequencies entirely, the owners made the painful decision to leave them behind. At the theater, some Plushies are joyfully reunited with their owners, while others discover cherished items their owners once held dear. After the show, Roccia returns the borrowed stage props to their rightful owners. The audience leaves, satisfied with their reclaimed belongings. As the stage empties, only one Luceanite remains. You recognize it as Roccia's Luceanite. Roccia, sensing something different in you, invites you to join the Fool's Troupe. Together, you performed the perfect welcoming opera, giving everyone the freedom to bring laughter back to Ragunna. Your ideals and the troupe's ideals aligne, making them your steadfast allies and support. Thanks to your efforts, Roccia's first orientation and return-to-sail event was a success. In addition to paying you for lending a hand, Roccia has a special gift for you.
