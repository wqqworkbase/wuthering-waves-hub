Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou receive an urgent message from Huaxu Academy for emergency support. Head to the designated location for more information.
Head to the designated location[]

(Approach Sanhua)
Sanhua (Contacting): Yes, My Lady, everything has been taken care of. Don't worry.
Sanhua (Contacting): We ran into an unexpected situation... Yes... They sent us a message, and I happened to be in the vicinity.
Sanhua (Contacting): I need to escort this researcher to the Tower. I'll be back shortly.

Talk to Sanhua[]

(Talk to Sanhua)
 Sanhua? Fancy seeing you here.
 Looks like you've already got the situation dealt with.
Sanhua: Rover?
Sanhua: I was assisting Madam Magistrate with certain matters, and as I happened to be close to that place, I arrived slightly ahead of you.
Sanhua: I resolved the issue.
 Since it's already been dealt with, I'll be heading back.
Sanhua: Yes, there is.
 Is there anything else I can help you with?
Sanhua: Actually...
Sanhua: I was instructed by Madam Magistrate to discuss a confidential project. Would you mind if I ask about it now, since we 're here?
Sanhua: While the project is still confidential, if you are interested to join, it can be arranged with Madam Magistrate's authorization. Are you interested?
 Confidential?
 Authorization?
Sanhua: I am currently escorting this injured researcher to that place.
Sanhua: If you could spare a moment, would you mind coming along? Explaining on-site might be more efficient.
 Sure, let's go together then.

Meet up with Sanhua[]

(Talk to Sanhua)
Young Soldier: Hey, what happened? How did you get injured?
Researcher: Can you believe it? I was just doing my routine specimen collection, and a Howler-type TD just pounced at me out of nowhere! What a lucky encounter!
 You think getting injured is lucky?
Researcher: Well, it didn't hurt that much. More importantly, it was an unidentified special variant! This discovery will surely enrich our database.
Researcher: My little injury today might save countless lives in the future!
Sanhua: As you can see, the Tower Project, formally known as the Tower of Adversity, is an artificial mirage simulation training ground we're currently developing in Huanglong.
 Artificial... what?
Researcher: Simply put, it's a man-made Sonora Sphere.
Researcher: We renovated a large experimental site left behind by the Court of Savantae  to build this place.
Researcher: This is a surreal space that can record specific information from a specific time and situation through our intervention.
Sanhua: Regarding the Court of Savantae... There is a lot of information we need you to know. We shall discuss it at a later time.
Researcher: Within this simulated environment, adversaries can materialize from the subject's recollections.
Researcher: As you delve deeper, tougher opponents will emerge, because of the difference in energy distribution pattern.
Sanhua: Our esteemed Magistrate intends to integrate this technology into military training for a more authentic experience.
Sanhua: This could potentially reduce casualties during real-life combat scenarios.
Sanhua: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), would you be willing to participate in our preliminary tests?
 Sure, why not?
Sanhua: Thank you.
 Why me?
Researcher: As I mentioned, enemies in the simulation are based on the tester's memories and their strength depends on their perception.
Researcher: For example, if I were to enter, my fear of Tacet Discords might cause me to imagine them as stronger than they actually are, which would impact the accuracy of data.
Researcher: This is why we rely on testers like you and Lady Sanhua, who possess both mental and physical fortitude, to provide realistic data for our research.
Researcher: The data we gathered from Lady Sanhua's past simulated battle against an Elite Class Tranquilite greatly aided us in our recent fight.
Sanhua: Madam Magistrate values this project greatly. As we discussed, our soldiers' lives are at stake.
Sanhua: I have faith that you will not disappoint her if entrusted with this task.
 Let's get started.
(Tower of Adversity Unlocked)

Complete the test once[]

(Enter the Tower of Adversity and complete at least one challenge)
