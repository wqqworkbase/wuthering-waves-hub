Dialogue[]

(Talk to Platya)
Platya: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), I've got the intel sorted. Is now a good time to meet?
Platya: Same place, near Rabelle College.
Platya: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), there you are. So I've asked around, and it turns out the initial sightings of the Ghost Miko didn't happen on campus.
Platya: They were in the Roya Frostlands. Quite a few researchers mentioned being saved by a figure dressed in plain white robes.
Platya: I'm convinced that the figure in the frostlands, this Ghost Miko, is the Lady Miko I'm looking for.
Platya: Some of them tried talking to her, you know? Shouting into the blizzard. But she never answered.
Platya: Only one researcher accidentally found an ID card where she'd fought a few Tacet Discords. Translated from the Lahai-Roi language, it read—"Special Response Force."
Platya: No name. No photo. Aside from that, the card was blank.
 I've never heard of that force before.
Platya: But since it translates from the Lahai-Roi language, that proves the Ghost Miko has ties to the Spacetrek Collective.
Platya: I remember there's an AI in the Academy that can access Spacetrek Collective data. Maybe we can ask her?
 You mean I.R.I.S.?
Platya: Exactly! So you've already met her. I'll leave talking with I.R.I.S. to you, then.
Platya: I'll keep poking around campus. See what else I can dig up.
(Find I.R.I.S.)

(Ask I.R.I.S.)
I.R.I.S.: Spacetrek Archive Retrieval AI, I.R.I.S. (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), long time no see. What brings you to see me today?
 Search "Special Response Force."
I.R.I.S.: Searching... 13 results match your query.
 Summarize key information from all results.
I.R.I.S.: Affirmative! Executing operation. The "Special Response Force" was an emergency response team established by the Spacetrek Collective in the past.
I.R.I.S.: Unlike Startorch Academy, they didn't care about research or philosophy. They only had one job.
I.R.I.S.: Eliminate anything that threatened the Collective. Members operated in five-person squads, with year-round deployment in the most dangerous zones imaginable.
I.R.I.S.: Over time, the casualty rate increased annually, reaching as high as 85%.
I.R.I.S.: Then there was an incident. During one operation, all members of the Special Response Force were confirmed KIA.
I.R.I.S.: Now, they technically still exist. But the system shows no new members have joined since.
I.R.I.S.: That's all the key information. Anything else I can help you with?
 Is there information on the members?
I.R.I.S.: I'm sorry. The system contains no records regarding specific members.
 Could there have been survivors from that operation?
I.R.I.S.: Answering hypotheticals isn't really my hobby, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). But if that's your guess... Gotta say, it is possible.
I.R.I.S.: If someone did survive, they never reported back. Or maybe the Collective never updated the data. Either way, the system still shows 100% casualties in that last operation.
 Understood. Thank you.
I.R.I.S.: Sure thing. You know where to find me if you have any more questions.

(Talk to Platya)
Platya: So? How'd it go? Did I.R.I.S. point you in the right direction?
 (Relay the information I.R.I.S. provided.)
Platya: So just the basics about the task force's history. No actual member records at all...
Platya: But... I think I.R.I.S. made a good point at the end. What if the Lady Miko survived, but the Collective's system never recorded her?
Platya: Or what if she made some kind of pact with the Collective? For instance... she didn't want anyone else to get hurt, so she decided the Special Response Force needed only her.
Platya: After all, the Lady Miko I knew... was exactly that kind of person.
Platya: Thanks for your help today, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). Let's call it a day. Tomorrow we can brainstorm other angles to try.
(Quest Complete)
