Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest unlocks Tactical Hologram: Phantom Pain.
Steps[]

- Chat with Botim

- Investigate the mysterious Terminal

- Tell Botim what you discovered
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionThe Fisalia family's heir seems to have a request.
In Thessaleo Fells, a mysterious Terminal is flickering with ominous light. What secrets of the past might it hold?

(Talk to Botim)
Botim: Honored guest from afar, I am relieved to finally see you.
Botim: I'm afraid it's not right to burden you with such a troublesome matter, but I can't think of anyone else capable of handling it.
 So what happened?
Botim: This isn't as complicated as it seems.
 Heh, lucky for you, you came to the right person.
Botim: I knew you would be willing to help.
Botim: Recently, my family entrusted me with managing an ancestral site, where a handed-down "Terminal" still remains.
Botim: This thing... it has a certain malevolence to it, you understand?
Botim: Shadowy monsters frequently emerge from within, causing chaos before disappearing on their own.
Botim: I'm asking for your assistance in dealing with these shadows, and rest assured, you'll be well compensated.
 Leave it to me.
Botim: I entrust this task to you, and I shall await your triumph here.

(Approach the designated location)
Something's off here. Traces of the Dark Tide's power linger...)
 Investigate the corrupted Terminal
Phantom: Hello, one who has passed the trial.
Phantom: I don't know what kind of person you are, but your strength is undeniable, among the world's finest.
Phantom: Drawing on the mechanics of the Thorncrown Rises and the Remnant Energy within Nimbus Sanctum, I have recreated powerful frequencies from various dimensions.
Phantom: To amplify these frequencies to the necessary strength, we even risked harnessing the power of the Dark Tide.
Phantom: We have taken on the job from Fisalia, exerting great effort and even collaborating with those from the Order, all to harness these "phantoms of salvation" and bring forth a true "savior."
Phantom: Savior, no matter which family you hail from, we have prepared a generous welcome gift for you!
Phantom: The "Savior's Proof," obtainable only here and forged into crest coins from the wealth of Fisalia, will bring you endless riches!
Phantom: To obtain them, you must become stronger and embrace continuous challenges!
Phantom: I await here, eagerly anticipating the emergence of the "Savior"!

(Talk to Botim)
Botim: You've returned, Victor! How did it go?
Botim: I'm not going to lie, they've entrusted this place to me, but I truly don't know how to handle that Terminal.
 Tell him honestly.
Rover: (He genuinely seems unaware... Should I tell him the truth?)
You told Botim everything the phantom revealed...
Botim: It all makes sense now... So that's what it is... Yes, I understand.
Botim: Esteemed Savior, thank you for sharing the truth with me.
Botim: Certainly, anything you gain from the Terminal is well-deserved. If you wish, you may proceed to the Terminal at any time to undertake the trials.
Botim: I have personal matters to attend to, so I'll take my leave for now.
Botim left, weighed down by his worries.
 It's better not to tell him.
Rover: (He genuinely seems unaware... It's probably best not to tell him.)
Rover: I'm on it. Just a few dangerous monsters left to handle, but I'll see it through.
Botim: I'm... I'm not sure how to express my gratitude... but truly, thank you so much!
(Quest Complete)
