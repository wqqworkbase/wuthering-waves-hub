Getting there[]

From the Wenye Beach resonance beacon, instead of heading east towards the quest marker, turn south and jump down the hole next to the camp with Sanshi and Lazing Around Researcher. Once you're inside the underground cave, follow the path and turn left at the crossroad. Quiz is under the awning.
