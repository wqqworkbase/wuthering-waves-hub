Steps[]

- Head to the "Fan Drawing Stall"

- Take a photo of the waterfall in Bell-Borne Ravine

- Take a photo of the Jinzhou City

- Find Zhezhi

- Deliver the photos to Dianxiao
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is time limited.

- Completing the quest unlocks Zhezhi as a Lodge Wishes companion in the By Moon's Grace limited time event.
