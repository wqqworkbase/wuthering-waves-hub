Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou have a new "Lollo Petter" order. Please fulfill it in time.

(Ask Maji about today's pilot business)
 What is today's pilot business?
Maji: The business today is: "Lollo Petter."
 You want me to pet you?
Maji: That would be nice... Just kidding. You'll be petting animals for other people. The demand has been growing lately.
Maji: There are many animals roaming in the city. People living in other places would like you to pet and feed the animals in their place.
Maji: Actually, I once dealt with a similar order as a courier. The sender was a human, but the recipient was a hound named "Mimi"...
Maji: And the customer told me... "I bought some jerky in Rinascita. Please help me feed the jerky to Mimi the dog in Jinzhou city!"
Maji: At that time, I was totally lost because offering that service wasn't something we did, but it really got me thinking about a whole new business opportunity.
Maji's Terminal: Ding! You have a new petting order.Please fulfill the order in time.
Maji: And this time we're looking for... a tabby cat named "Wangcai"? Oh my, I know this cat. I even have an emoji of it on my Terminal!
 I must have the emoji. Send it to me!
Maji: Let's see here... The emoji, and Wangcai's location... There, I've forwarded them to you! Remember to bring Wangcai's favorite fish fillet!

(Check what happened)
Onlookers: We have too many people trying to feed it at once. It looks scared. Step back, everyone!
Onlookers: Got it! Wangcai is hiding in the braces behind the light box, but his primordial pouch is too fat. He's stuck.
Wangcai: Meow meow meow...
Xuanyin: Let me try the cat language translation module!
Xuanyin: Wangcai said: "Numb. Something's zapping me inside the light box. My tummy feels numb. Please get me out quickly."
Xuanyin: Quick. Turn off the circuit module, and I'll go save Wangcai.

(Turn off the circuit module)
You and Xuanyin join forces to save Wangcai, prompting cheers from the crowd. After thanking you, Xuanyin takes Wangcai to the Academy's medical department. It seems you'll have to wait until next time to feed Wangcai.

(Recap today's pilot business with Maji)
You tell Maji about the event, leaving him speechless for a moment.
 What's the matter?
Maji: Being a cat is way better than being a human.
 I don't get it...
Maji: I can't write today's report in this case, but if I were a cat, I would never have to write any report. Sob sob...
 You can try another of your business ideas tomorrow.
Maji: You're right. Let me cope with this now. Everything will be fine tomorrow.
(Quest Complete)
