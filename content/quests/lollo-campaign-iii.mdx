Steps[]

- Chat with Maji about today's pilot business

- Deliver the following ingredients to Xiehua Village

- 3 bags of flour

- 2 bottles of cooking oil
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou have a new "Lollo Grocery Shopping" order. Please fulfill it in time.

(Chat with Maji about today's pilot business)
 What is today's pilot business?
Maji: The business today is "Lollo Grocery Shopping". Let me brief you on the background first.
Maji: The Tacet Field in the Port City of Guixu is expanding, and the nearby Xiehua Village has been included in the emergency evacuation area. The Ministry of Development and the Patrollers are already very busy.
Maji: Merchants willing to go to Xiehua Village are few already because of the short distance to the Port City of Guixu. Now with the trouble in the Tacet Field...
Maji: The purpose of piloting this business is to help the villagers of Xiehua Village with "Lollo Grocery Shopping."
 I understand your idea.
Maji's Terminal: Ding! You have ten new grocery orders. Please handle them on time.
Maji: Let me see... 3 bags of flour and 2 bottles of cooking oil. You can buy them from Mahe's Grocery.
Maji: I will do the purchasing with you. You can find the shopping list on your Terminal now.
Maji: When you've got everything done, meet me at Xiehua Village.

(Deliver the ingredients to Xiehua Village)
 Who are you...?
Maji: You don't recognize me just because I changed the outfit?
Maji: Maggie! It's me, Maji!
Maji: I still feel nostalgic about this outfit. It fits me well.
 Indeed, it suits you.
Maji: I have already handed over the ingredients to my Patroller friends. They will distribute them!
Maji: Oh, by the way, I will transfer the expense and pay to you through the terminal.
Maji: See you tomorrow! Don't be late!
