Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionSee what comes after the stand-up show

 You didn't finish the story last time, did you?
Yihu: No, there's much more to tell.
Yihu: It's getting more and more intriguing.
 Did you come up with this yourself?
Yihu: Yeah. I pulled an all-nighter to write this bit. Hear it.
Yihu: So, after that day, the oil thefts at the boss's house just stopped.
Yihu: Now the rats weren't stealing oil no more. The young cat could catch a mouse every day. The boss was overjoyed, treating it to meat and fish.
Yihu: But then the meat started disappearing from the kitchen, and the boss was all confused.
Yihu: He thought to himself, if he's treating this cat like royalty, there's no need for him to steal the meat.
Yihu: Little did he know, the young cat was secretly feeding the old one with the meat.
Yihu: As time went by, those two cats got brazen. Not satisfied with just filling their bellies, they even started selling the stolen meat to stray cats.
 Cats selling meat?
Yihu: Yeah. What do you know? The world's full of peculiar things.
 Tell me about it.
Yihu: I ran into them the other day.
Yihu: I overheard some strange muttering like, "Clouds cover the moon, people hide in shadows, and cats meow three times?"
Yihu: Didn't catch him all though. The young cat, unlike his father, was on high alert. When I approached, he hissed at me, so I didn't dare to get any closer.
Yihu: And that's why I couldn't find an end to the story.
 Indeed... There's no punchline and the delivery was bland.
Yihu: Well, you certainly don't sugarcoat your words, huh? If you've got the time, maybe share this story with others. Who knows, someone might find it amusing.
(Quest Complete)
