Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the "Take a seat" step will award the player Black Fruit, Leaf, Small Device and Sugar Pearl.

- Completing the "Ask the kids nearby" optional step will award the player Candy and the Rover's Journey trophy Candy Spells Riddle.

- Completing the "..◼️#.◼️..Lea#ve.◼️.◼️.." step will unlock Convene and grant the Resonator Baizhi.

- Completing the "Deliver the parts to Mortefi" step will award the player Sundial.

- Completing the "Look for clues about the tokens" step will award the player Archive: "The First Resonator" and Archive: Sugar Pearl Vaccine and Tacet Discord Outbreak.

- Completing the quest will grant the Rover's Journey trophy Thus Spoke the Magistrate: Wordless Message.
