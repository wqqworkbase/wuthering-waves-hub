Summary[]

Following Captain Ahab's instructions, you and Phoebe manage to find his old First Mate, Starbuck, but the man seems to have fallen under some kind of enchantment. To help Starbuck come to his senses, you decide to locate his Gondola for clues.
You and Phoebe find Starbuck's lost Gondola. Having theorized that Starbuck's enchantment has something to do with the fish he was holding, the two of you begin brainstorming a plan to get it off his hands.
You and Phoebe finally manage to grab the fish from Starbuck, and the First Mate's sanity is restored. Starbuck claims he once saw Cetus the Tidebreaker at a place called Serene Bay, with a large fish swimming alongside it. He hands you the lighthouse key Captain Ahab entrusted in his care before you departed.

Steps[]

- Head to the dock and get ready to depart

- Complete Requests to increase your Fame to Level 3 or above

- Search for Ahab near the dock in Riccioli Port

- Find a shipwright to learn more about the large ship

- Collect Palm Tree Logs and Riccioli Fishery Coins for Queequeg

- Gather 50 Palm Logs

- Earn 500 Riccioli Fishery Coins

- Go to the dock, prepare for departure, and travel to Helm Village

- Travel to Helm Village

- Search for Starbuck

- Set Sail from the dock and find the abandoned gondola

- Go to the location where the Gondola is trapped

- Clear the rubble and free the Gondola

- Complete Requests or update the Index to increase your Fame to level 6 or above

- Wait for the evening (or adjust the time to 19:00-05:00)

- Fish for large catches at designated Fishing Spot

- Head back to Helm Village and locate Starbuck

- Use the key to light up the lighthouse in Helm Village
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing "Search for Starbuck" step awards  "Sweet Puff".

- Completing "Fish for large catches at designated Fishing Spot" step awards Familiar-looking Large FishFamiliar-looking Large Fish Familiar-looking Large Fish.

- Completing "Head back to Helm Village and locate Starbuck" step awards  Helm Village Lighthouse Key.

- Completing the quest awards  Pequod Blueprint: Basic.
