Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionButler the Steward seems troubled about taking care of the Acolytes in the Fabricatorium of the Deep. Talk to him and learn what's weighing on his mind.

(Talk to the Acolyte)
Butler: Wait, no... How do I open it...
 What's going on?
Butler: I'm collecting Sacred Salt for future needs.
Butler: There is no room for errors in the work of a Steward. I must take great care of the Acolytes' welfare.
Butler: The martyrs sacrificed everything for the cause of saving Ragunna. Even in failure, they are entitled to live out their days in peace...
 So the Sacred Salt is for them?
Butler: Yes. For they hold onto the belief that anything untouched by the Sentinel's grace on this blessed land could be potentially tainted or cursed by the Threnodian.
Butler: That's why I would perform cleansing and consecration rites with Sacred Salt on all provisions before their eyes.
Butler: To lead them into thinking they're taking the Divinity's nurturing meal, drinking holy blood that grants enlightenment. So they have no reason to refuse.
Butler: But lately, we are running short on Sacred Salt. As an Acolyte of the Ministry of Holy Rituals, I...
Rover: (He seems a bit unsettled too... Best not to push him. We should figure out a way to help.)
 Did the Order anticipate this situation?
Butler: Ah, yes. We've set aside some Sacred Salt in the Fabricatorium for times like this. It's kept in the Triptych Chests and other places...
Butler: If you happen upon any, bring it to me. I'll offer you plenty in return. What do you think?
 It's a deal.
(Quest Complete)
