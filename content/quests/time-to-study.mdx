Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionJiu is supposed to be at the academy, but he appears troubled by something. Check out what happened to him.

Jiu: Oh, I wish I could grow up faster, but just going to school isn't enough...
 What are you doing?
Jiu: I'm trying my best to grow up quickly!
Jiu: All we do in school is read textbooks, but that doesn't really help. I want to learn something useful, so I can help my dad. That's why I'm here!
 Does your family know?
Jiu: I don't know... I made this decision on my own. I... I really want to help my family!

Variant 1[]

Rover: Jiu? That name sounds familiar.
Rover: Oh, he could be Sixi's son.
Rover: Let's go find him and talk.
(Talk to Jiu)
Jiu: Oh, what's wrong? Are you trying to tell me how to grow up quickly?
 About Sixi...
Jiu: Oh! You saw my dad?! How is he?! I heard something happened at the mine recently, and he's been really busy...
Jiu: Dad said the mine had some trouble lately, so he's been swamped and doesn't have time to play with me...
Jiu: I really want to grow up quickly and find a job, so I can help my dad! By the way, did he mention me?
 He wants you to stay in school.
 He wants to spend time with you, too.
Jiu: Oh, I see... Well, my dad always says as long as I study hard, he'll be happy!
Jiu: Your dad loves to spend time with you!
Jiu: Okay, I'll go back to class now!
(Quest Complete)

Variant 2[]

Rover: His family must not know he's skipping class.
Rover: Go talk to Wulü. Maybe he knows something.
(Talk to Jiu again)
Jiu: What are you doing?
(Talk to Wulü)
Wulü: Hello, do you need something?
 About Jiu...
Wulü: Ah, Jiu, he's Sixi's son. That man works at the mine and probably doesn't have much time to take care of him.
Wulü: Lately, I've seen Jiu wandering around looking for a job to do. I was thinking of having a little chat with him.
Wulü: You might want to tell Sixi about this if you meet him.
(Talk to Sixi)
Sixi: What's wrong? Do you need any help?
 You are Sixi, right?
Sixi: Yes, what is it?
 About Jiu...
You tell Sixi about Jiu's situation.
Sixi: Hmm? Jiu is skipping school?
Sixi: Thank you for the updates. I'll go back and check on him right away!
Sixi quickly ran towards the city.
(Return to the Resonance Nexus)
Sixi: What do I always tell you, hmm? Why are you skipping school?
Jiu: I, I...
Jiu: I'm not skippin school! I just want to learn something not taught in the classroom...
Sixi: What do you mean by that? Explain yourself.
Jiu: It's just... something not in a textbook, something that'll teach me how I can help you, Dad!
Jiu: You're always so busy and don't have time for me, so I want to help you...
Sixi: You can help me by attending your classes, you know!
Sixi: They're supposed to be teaching you practical life skills. If you learn something interesting, you can even teach your old man something new!
Sixi: Anyway... there really are issues at the mine recently. I promise you, once they are settled, we'll spend more time together, okay?
Jiu: Really? You really mean it?
Sixi: Yes. I'll keep my word. But before that, you need to keep yours and stay in school!
Jiu: Deal! I promise I'll stay in school!
After the conversation, Sixi took Jiu and left.
(Quest Complete)
