Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou meet Xuanyin again. This time, she is observing the doves on the rooftop...

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: Player, I've got good news for you! The Gray Crested Gull language samples we collected last time were a big help!
Xuanyin: The Bird Language Translation Module is almost complete. I'm planning to test it with other birds to see if there are any areas for improvement. By the way, I have something serious to tell you.
Dove (Translation): Coo coo coo.
Xuanyin: Hold on! We have volunteers here!
 Do we need to attract them with food?
Xuanyin: Well, let me think... There are plenty of grains on the ground, but they don't seem interested. Seems like we need to find another way to attract their attention.
Xuanyin: Let's hear what they're talking first.
Xuanyin loads the Pigeon Language Translation Module onto your Pangu Terminal.
Dove (Translation): Human, coo, stupid, coo, bye.
The pigeons fly away after saying that.
Xuanyin: Hey! How dare you fly away after name-calling! Are you trying to pick a fight?
Xuanyin: Those impolite pigeons! I won't let them get away with it! Quickly scan to see where they flew off to.

(Find the traces of doves with Sensor)
Feathers are scattered around the box, but the pigeons are nowhere to be found.

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: Looks like the pigeons stood us up.
Xuanyin: Is having a condescending attitude towards humans a common trait shared by birds?
 Why should we humans care about the opinions of birds? What's the business?
Xuanyin: Come on! I'm full of fight now! I've got to get an explanation from them!
Xuanyin: Next time, I must catch them and find it out!
(Quest Complete)
