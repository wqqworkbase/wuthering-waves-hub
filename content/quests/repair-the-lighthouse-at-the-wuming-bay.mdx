Steps[]

- Place the battery in the base to launch the pulse

- Connect the pulse circuit

- Hack into the lighthouse terminal system

- Search for treasures
Gameplay Notes[]

- This is one of five lighthouses required to be repaired for Lost History.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionThe lighthouse at the Wuming Bay has lost power. Try to restore its power!

(Talk to the person)
Infrastructure Division Member: Ugh... Still can't do it...
 Why the long face?
Infrastructure Division Member: I was assigned to this power station to restore power of that lighthouse on the hill.
Infrastructure Division Member: Everything else was swiftly taken care of for a genius such as myself.
Infrastructure Division Member: Little did I expect to get stuck at the final step of turning on the power...
 What's the problem?
Infrastructure Division Member: This power station utilizes a pulsed power supply system. After the battery is installed, the circuit must be fully connected within a certain time frame.
Infrastructure Division Member: In order to complete the connection, the pylons along the circuit must be activated using physical force in sequential order within the time limit.
Infrastructure Division Member: Unfortunately, I was only bestowed with a genius brain but not strong physiques. I've always been at the bottom in terms of fitness, and I simply can't complete the route in time.
 I can offer you some help.
Infrastructure Division Member: Really?! You would do that? Thank you!
(Optional - talk to the person again)
Infrastructure Division Member: There's just no way I can activate all the pylons leading up to the lighthouse within such a short time period...

(If time runs out)
Infrastructure Division Member: H-Hello? Rover, can you hear me?
Infrastructure Division Member: The electromagnetic pulse just went out... I guess you didn't make it in time. You should come back for another run.
(If pulse circuit was connected successfully)
(Made it. That should restore the lighthouse's power.)
(Head back to the lighthouse and hack into the console.)
