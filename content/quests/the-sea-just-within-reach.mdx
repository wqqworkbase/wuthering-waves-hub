The Sea Just Within Reach is a Side Quest that occurs in Bjartr Woods, Roya Frostlands, [[{{{nation}}}]].

Steps[]

- Check the Submerged Tunnel Memorial

- Check WavesLine

- Meet up with Mornye

- Ride the motorbike through the Submerged Tunnel

- Say goodbye to Mornye
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest awards World in All Its Variety II trophy Do Submarines Dream of Data Sets?.
