Summary[]

With the Sovereign slain, you attend the victory ceremony by invitation of Ceremonial Officer Bruno. The celebration honors the new Hero of Heroes and those who stood by their side amidst the crackling bonfire and joyous cheers of the crowd... Now that the countless petals are to be thrown into the sky to become the rain of glory and triumph, you might finally reunite with the girl you have never forgotten in a way even beyond fate's grasp.

After distributing some petals, a Priestess's oration catches your attention. Surprisingly, for a ceremony of such significance, Iuno isn't the one hosting. In fact, she isn't mentioned at all. Dispelling the Dark Tide's miasma, her role in the hunt, everything... The Priestess's oration of the battle attributes all of Iuno's contributions to you. No one seems to find this strange. It's as if all memory of Iuno has been erased from the world.

Your mind swirls with questions, but no answers come. An elder at the camp notices your unease and gently explains that what you're experiencing may be a side-effect of prolonged Dark Tide exposure—hallucinations, confusion, delusions. He reassures you that these symptoms aren't permanent, and simply avoiding the Dark Tide should let you recover. He suggests speaking with Augusta, who went through the Dark Tide alongside you and might offer some clarity.

Knowing how close Augusta and Iuno are, you look to her for confirmation. But what she says shocks you. She believes your memory of "Iuno" is a product of the Whisper's deceptions, a false memory to be resisted. You try to prove Iuno is real by showing her Iuno's Lunarum bracelet. During the hunt, you had tried to return it to Iuno, but she insisted you keep it as a memento. Did she somehow already know she would be forgotten? Just then, light spills from the bracelet to form a specter of Iuno. But Augusta sees nothing at all.

You're unable to accept Iuno's disappearance, but doubt flickers through your mind... Was it all an illusion? Now that even Iuno's last sliver of moonlight fades, your memories are the only proof she ever existed. Abby reassures you that it also remembers everything about Iuno and all the moments you shared. As the only exception left, you refuse to accept this ending. You decide to search Tetragon Temple for any remaining traces of her.

Lillibet reveals two important truths. First, Septimont's future is now veiled behind a white barrier. For the first time, none of the Priestesses can see any of Septimont's Auguries. Second, after hearing from Augusta about your unwavering belief in Iuno's existence, she uncovered a single record: a Priestess named Iuno who died three years ago. But death and disappearance cannot both be true. Your lone memory of Iuno has disrupted the causal chain, creating a reality suspended in limbo. Refusing to accept this ending, you resolve to bring Iuno back from the Chaos between life and death, between existence and non-existence, by using the same method that once gave form to Avidius.

The realm of Chaos isn't entered by location, but through a causal "knot," a specific intersection of circumstance and action. You and Lillibet travel to the site of Iuno's recorded death three years ago, but find nothing unusual. Just as doubt begins to creep in, you notice the corrupted moon above. Once again, a trail of moonlight appears, guiding you toward a specter of Iuno—and deeper into the Dark Tide. You don't hesitate. Though uncertain of what you're seeing, Lillibet knows she can no longer stop you. She offers one final piece of advice: "If you find what remains of her in the Chaos, follow her guidance."

Iuno's specter takes shape before you in the Chaos. You don't know how long she's been lost here, but it's clear she's no longer whole. She calls you an "unwritten blank" and reaches out her hand. You're unsure how to anchor Iuno back into existence, but the moment your hands meet, you know it has begun. You've found a trace of Iuno, and now she's guiding you to find the rest.

You and Iuno's specter journey deeper into the Chaos, seeing memories of her past unfold around you. From whispers among the Priestesses, you learn of a prophecy tied to her birth. Lillibet once remarked that Iuno, born during a lunar eclipse, was destined to be a prodigy among Priestesses. Whether she would rise as the next great champion or follow the fate written for her, one thing was clear: the bold, willful Iuno was never bound to a single path.

Your anchoring with Iuno continues. In another memory, you witness a female Gladiator charging into a battlefield corrupted by the Dark Tide. From her name and title, you sense her connection to Iuno, but when you suggest approaching her, Iuno's specter refuses. These visions in the Chaos aren't entirely real—they're shaped by Iuno's own memories and perceptions. For someone who once kept her eyes closed to the truth, she can neither see things as they truly were nor change what has already passed. You see deep pain in her eyes.

You continue forward and meet a blacksmith who mistakes you for Iuno, perhaps a result of the anchoring. He asks whether you still plan to become a Priestess or choose another path, but offers no advice. Instead, he reminds you of the bow you once asked him to craft. Like anything else, creation takes knowledge and the right steps to take form. He tells you that if you can gather enough Lunarum Bough, he'll forge the bow for you, and then opens a path deeper into the Chaos.

You reach a shallow pool and sit down to rest beside Iuno's specter. Her bracelet begins to glow, releasing memories of the past: A young Iuno celebrates a victory gladiating and runs into her mother's arms, only to see her consumed by the Dark Tide. She wonders why fate chose her, and what the cost truly was. The memory ends suddenly. From the pool, golden Lunarum Bough begins to grow, and Iuno's specter becomes a little more whole. Anchoring her back into existence is working.

The light from the bracelet leads you to two doors. To accept the fate that was seen, or turn away from it. To face a predetermined path, or hide from it. The possibilities are endless. But like Iuno once did, you know you must make a choice.

The moment you sit at the divination altar, everything around you shifts. You find yourself in what appears to be a moment of the past, face-to-face with a living Iuno who's caught in a looping memory. She reaches for your hand to read your fate, but the instant your fingers touch, her lost memories come rushing back. Why are you here? And why hasn't she vanished from existence?

Realizing you entered Chaos to help her, Iuno chooses to go along with your attempt to rewrite her ending. She promises to meet you again at the very end. As her voice fades into the air, your vision shifts once more. This time, it feels like you have truly become Iuno.

As Priestess Iuno of the past, you sit on the altar offering prophecies to those who seek guidance.

The first to come is Harelius, a Gladiator in search of strategic insight for his squad and their mission to confront a monstrous Tacet Discord never before seen in the Dark Tide.

The second to come is Samara, a lovestruck woman holding a bracelet. She...Transcription missing

The third to come is Nikolas, a noble youth desperate to outwit fate. Another Priestess has prophesied that his twin brother will die at the hands of a beast this summer. But you foresee Nikolas living to old age. He wonders: if he takes his brother's place in battle, can he cheat death? Can one prophecy cancel another?

The fourth to come is Maia, a young, innocent girl. She...Transcription missing

Light streams from the bracelet. You've learned how to draw out Iuno's memories, and you wait as they reveal more of her hidden past. The girl who should have watched her mother's death unfold instinctively chose to shut her eyes. After losing the chance to change anything, she vowed to see everything from then on, no matter the cost. That vow led her to become a Priestess. But after countless prophecies, she began to question whether "seeing" truly meant understanding. The Lunarum Bough continues to grow, and the Iuno beside you slowly becomes more whole.

You encounter Augusta in the Chaos who mistakes you for Iuno, like the others you've met. She poses a difficult question: when fate offers no solution and only promises annihilation, what then is the role of a Priestess? Would you dare, with no direction or clues, gaze upon Septimont's destruction and trace its cause? You understand your answer doesn't matter. What matters is how Iuno would answer that question.

After countless days and nights of divination, not a single prophecy showed Septimont surviving. Still, Iuno refused to accept that fate. To her, human dignity lay in defying what was predetermined. When Harelius returned seeking guidance, she cast her candelabrum into the Dark Tide and walked toward the unknown beyond Tetragon Temple. By joining Harelius's squad in battle, if she could prove fate could be changed, even once, then Septimont's fated destruction would no longer be absolute.

You return to the now fully grown Lunarum Bough and talk with the blacksmith again. While there's clearly more to him than meets the eye, what matters now is that the Bough's growth has strengthened the anchoring of Iuno's memories and connections. Even though Iuno's specter you've been trying to make whole was taken by the Dark Tide, as long as the Lunarum Bough remains, there is still hope to find another trace of her in the Chaos.

Remembering your promise to Iuno to meet at the very end, you decide to gamble everything on the chance of finding her again in the Dark Tide. You ask the blacksmith to fulfill his promise of making the Moon Bow to give to Iuno.

After being taken by the Dark Tide, Iuno awakens alone at the edge of Chaos. She finds herself standing on the battlefield from three years ago. Having foreseen Harelius and his squad wiped out, she had decided to stop being a passive observer of fate and instead fight in their battle against the Dark Tide.

The dust settles after that final moment of the battle. No more Chaos. No more Dark Tide. All you see is Iuno standing before you, framed by an infinite white void. Is this the changed ending? Did you really unlock infinite possibility? You can't be sure, but within the vastness of this blank void, Iuno tells you it's time to leave.

Upon returning from Chaos, you discover some records in Lillibet's Book of Prophecies have been overwritten, proof that what you did made a difference. After hearing the Priestesses in discussion, you're certain that things have changed. They mention a girl with two blue ponytails. Could it be Iuno? Following their suggestion, you step into the celebrating crowd to search for her.
