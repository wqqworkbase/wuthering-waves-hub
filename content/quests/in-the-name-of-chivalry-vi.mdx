Steps[]

- Reach the designated area

- Ask those who claim to have seen the knight

- Speak with the "knight" everyone has been talking about
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionLately, rumors have been spreading around Egla Town of a selfless knight helping those in need. You think you know who that is.
(Talk to Carmilla)
Carmilla: Oh, dear Imperator, I'll always remember that knight's bravery...
 You met a knight?
Carmilla: Indeed. On that fateful day, I ran into a fierce Tacet Discord outside the town. A brave young man rushed to help me, fighting the monster with something like a pitchfork.
Carmilla: Once we were safe, I asked for his name, and he told me that he was the squire of that "Roving Knight who wanders between the realms of day and night."
 ...
Carmilla: It appears that knights still exist in this era...
 Well, I think I've met him before too.
Carmilla: Is that so? He left in such a hurry, and all I could do was watch him go.
Carmilla: This brief encounter brought comfort to my broken heart. Perhaps our paths will cross again someday?

(Go talk with the "knight" everyone has been talking about)
Faro: I'm ready, so what do we have for today's training?
 It's you! Squire of the Roving Knight!
Faro: Oh... Haha, how did you know that?
 Why did you leave my title when you were the one doing good deeds?
Faro: I'm sorry for doing that without asking. It's just that your guidance has brought me closer to my dream.
Faro: Just as the tales of knights inspired me, I hoped that by spreading the name of the Roving Knight, others might also find motivation.
Faro: People would see that knights who uphold justice and righteousness are still active, in Whisperwind Haven and even in other places in Rinascita, not lost to time.
 You'll earn your own title one day.
 Leave your own title next time.
Faro: I will! But there's still so much to learn, so I hope you'll keep training me!
 Absolutely.
Under your guidance, Faro begins a new level of real combat training.
