Steps[]

- Ask Alannah about the plan for day 4

- Ask Lollo Logistics staff for information about the vehicle

- Repair the courier truck

- Talk to Alannah

- Ask Alannah about the reviews on the model courier truck
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionAlannah seems to be interested in Lollo Logistics vehicles

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), do you like cars? Or automobiles in general?
 I do.
Alannah: I do, too!
 Not really.
Alannah: Come on, nothing beats the sound of the engine roaring as you zoom down the open road with the wind in your hair. It's a feeling you don't get to enjoy anywhere else.
Alannah: You should give it a try when you get the chance. You'll love it!
 Yes, but I can't afford one.
Alannah: Same here. But, yeah, they aren't cheap to get. Hahaha.
Alannah: Sorry, I digress. During my time in Ragunna, I noticed their road infrastructure isn't the finest.
Alannah: You won't find many roads in the countryside, and even the city roads aren't friendly for driving.
Alannah: Why don't we explore the market for scale model cars in Ragunna? We might discover some potential automobile enthusiasts there.
 Thinking ahead. I like it.
 If there's no demand, we'll create one.
Alannah: How about we go to Lollo Logistics for some pointers? They use a variety of vehicles, which can help us figure out what's best for making models!

(Talk to Lollo Logistics staff)
Troubled Courier: Great, now what...
Alannah: What seems to be the trouble?
Troubled Courier: I finished loading all my cargo and was getting ready to leave, but the darn engine wouldn't start for some reason...
 Let me give it a look.
 You know, I'm something of an auto mechanic myself.

(Repair the courier truck)
Troubled Courier: That's some quick fixing! Whew.
Alannah: That was awesome, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)!
Troubled Courier: Fantastic, for a while there I thought I was gonna miss my delivery. I can't thank you enough. Time for me to hit the road. See you both around!
The courier starts the engine. You watch as the vehicle slowly disappears into the distance.
Alannah: I never know you are an auto repair expert, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! So you know every nut and bolt inside that truck?
 Just a quick glance.
 Like the back of my hand.
Alannah: Then it'll make our model-making job a cakewalk. So, without further ado, let's get to work!

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: Drum roll, please. It's that time again. Let's see how our truck model was received over at Ragunna...

(Talk to Alannah)
Survey Feedback: "And the 'Ugly Car Award' goes to..." "Did someone get tired of the Cloudpuffers? Or are Tubpups not cute enough?" "Who would ride in crappy metal containers when we have Gondolas around?"...
Alannah: ...
 Probably not many car people over there.
 So they don't really need cars.
Alannah: I get it now. You can't expect to open a shoe store and make sales when the locals are running around barefoot.
Alannah: They've got cute-looking Echoes that can be used for transportation. There's no reason for them to have cars.
Alannah: *sigh* Getting that three-million-copy sale feels nothing more than a pipe dream now.
 Don't let up just yet.
 It was an impossible sale to begin with.
Alannah: You're so nice, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Alannah: Gotta do my research and learn more about their actual preferences. I can't be just making up a market willy-nilly...
(Quest Complete)
