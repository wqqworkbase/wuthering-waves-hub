Summary[]

Rover returns to Ragunna City to investigate strange disturbances tied to the Dark Tide. They confront powerful Tidespawn, explore the mysterious Civilization Capsule and uncover deeper hints about the rising threat of The  Threnodian Leviathan, and face off against a corrupted Gladiator known as the Mad King: Caligula (Avidius). Along the way, allies and enemies alike wrestle with memories, fate, and the shadows of their past as the story pushes toward the larger looming crisis in Rinascita.
