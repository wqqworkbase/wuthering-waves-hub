Steps[]

- Reach the designated area

- Clear the rocks blocking the entrance

- Talk to Faro

- Or perhaps... just smash the rocks?
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest awards the World in All Its Variety I trophy Iron Sword of Promised Victory.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionAs you travel, you hear a familiar voice crying for help. Go and see what's happening.
(Approach the designated location)
???: HELP!!! IS ANYONE OUT THERE? CAN ANYBODY HEAR ME? PLEASE, HELP!!!
Rover: That voice sounds familiar... Could it be...?

(Talk to Faro)
Faro: Phew! I'm finally out! I thought I'd be stuck in that  dark cave forever!
 How did you get trapped in there?
Faro: Well, I wanted to find a proper weapon before the official training started.
Faro: I heard that the Mad Knight's sword was hidden around here, so I came to look for it.
Faro: And there it was! Just like the legends said, stuck in a rock!
 You mean that giant rock over there?
Faro: That's right! But no matter how hard I pulled, it wouldn't budge.
Faro: I must've spent ages trying! Then suddenly, I heard rumbling and loud crashes from outside. Before I knew it, I was trapped by falling rocks.
 The sword looks like part of the rock now...
 Let me give it a try.
Faro: Maybe you are the one that can really pull it out...
Faro: Legend says this Sword in the Rock chooses its owner, and only those with true courage and integrity can wield it.
 Really?
 That sounds like me.
Faro: The stage is yours now. Oh, I can almost hear it whispering to me&#8212;"You don't have the right, O you don't have the right."

(Smash the rock)
Faro: Congratulations on retrieving the long-lost Sword in the Rock! I never expected you could just break the rock to get it...
 Is this really a sword?
Faro: Well, after all those years, I guess it's a bit weathered...
Faro: But look at its blade! It's so slender, and it definitely has a unique texture compared to an ordinary sword.
 It's not a pipe, is it?
Faro: How could that be? It is... Well, fine, it's just a piece of pipe.
Faro: Maybe some prankster decided to follow the legend and stuck it in that rock?
Faro: *Sigh* I'm becoming more gullible. I once searched for the Sword in the Rock with Carlow for ages, but we found nothing...
Faro: All these mysteries of knighthood... who knows which is real and which is not? I just didn't want to dig deeper and end up disappointed...
 Faro, the Novice Knight.
Faro: ...Yes? Are you calling me?
Rover: Can you swear to this sword that from now on, you will only fight for justice and righteousness, and help those in need?
Faro: Absolutely! I, Faro, swear to this sword that I will only fight for justice and righteousness from now on.
Rover: Good, then this Sword in the Rock belongs to you now.
You pick up the weathered "sword" and hand it to the young man before you with sincerity. He stands there, surprised, and after a moment, he accepts it with trembling hands.
Faro: But it's just a piece of pipe...
 Even if the legend isn't real, your vow is.
 What makes a knight isn't their weapon but their spirit.
Faro: You're right. When I was a child, I used to play knight with a stick.
Faro: Whether it's a weathered pipe or a magnificent sword, a knight's responsibilities remain the same.
Faro: Thank you. This looks sturdy and should last quite a while.
(Quest Complete)
