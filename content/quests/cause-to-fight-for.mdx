Steps[]

- Head to "Swim, Gulpuff, Swim"

- Talk to the elder

- Find the first soldier

- Find the second soldier

- Deliver the Lemongrass to Keni

- Find the third soldier

- Talk to Jiyan
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is time limited.

- Completing the quest unlocks Jiyan as a Lodge Wishes companion in the By Moon's Grace limited time event.
