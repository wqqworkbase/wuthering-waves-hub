Steps[]

- Look for someone who needs a Windchimer
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest unlocks the Windchimer Store.
Dialogue[]

 Quest Description"Wind whistles, water purls, bird chirps, all echoed in the melodies of the past." Deliver the Windchimers you collect to Xian'ge and obtain various rewards. Collect the Windchimers scattered in Mt. Firmament and make the music play again.

(Talk to Xian'ge)
Xian'ge: Hello, (&#8205;&#8205;good sirText for male Rover&#8205;/&#8205;good ladyText for female Rover&#8205;&#8205;)! Would you be so kind as to lend me a few minutes? Sorry to bother you, but have you ever seen a bell-like object encircled by muscial notes in the mountains?
 It does ring a bell.
Xian'ge: Does it look similar to this bell adorned on my clothes?
 I don't recall.
Xian'ge: Here, let me show you. It looks like this bell adorned on my clothes.
Xian'ge: It is called Windchimer, which can capture and preserve a variety of sounds, converting them into usable energy. Perhaps you can think of it as a sophisticated recorder to help you better understand how it works.
Xian'ge: There are many Windchimers scattered in the mountains. If you happen to find one, you may ring it to release the "sounds" within.
Xian'ge: And with the Terminal, you can absorb the converted energy.
Xian'ge: I've been collecting these gadgets to amass the energy, which can be used to repair this Guqin. Please, have a look at it.
 It's a nice one.
Xian'ge: What keen observation you have! If I manage to repair it, its melody will surely resonate across the mountain! No, even beyond!
 It's broken.
Xian'ge: Indeed, but if I manage to repair it, its melody will surely resonate across the mountain!
 I don't have the money to buy it.
Xian'ge: You got me wrong. I wasn't planning to sell it.
Xian'ge: However broken it is, I can still feel the myriad of stories it carries, which is precisely why repairing it is worthwhile.
Xian'ge: To make sure the Guqin is well-tuned, I need the energy from the Windchimer.
Xian'ge: Therefore, I would appreciate it if you could collect the Winchimers and send them back to me.
Xian'ge: And your efforts won't go unpaid. Don't worry, I know the rules. Payment for services rendered&#8212;that's what we'd call an equal exchange.
(Quest Complete)
