Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionArno, the Black Shores member, needs your help.

(Talk to Arno)
Arno: Hello, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! I'm Agent Arno from the Black Shores. I'm glad to finally meet you in person.
 You know me?
Arno: Of course, Encore was talking about you. I recognized you in an instant.
Arno: You're the first person ever to get so much praise from that girl. I had been looking forward to meet you in person.
Arno: By the way, are you available for a side gig?
Arno: I ran into a little problem with the work I'm tasked with, and I'm looking for some help. I was wondering if you'd be interested.
 It depends.
Arno: Uh... It's simple, actually. Let me explain the situation to you first. I hope you'll be interested.
 I'm happy to help.
Arno: That's great. Let me explain the situation to you first.
Arno: I think you should know what we do already. Simply put, we do Lament forecasts like meteorologists do weather forecasts.
Arno: As a special agent, I periodically travel all around the world to collect data from various locations to improve our prediction models.
Arno: The data collection for this cycle is almost done, but I suspect I'm still missing accurate data in a few spots.
Arno: I can't gather much data on my own, but with your assistance, I'm confident we can obtain more precise samples.
Arno: I assume you, your efforts won't be wasted. If we secure the crucial data, we can secure a generous amount of funding.
 What should I do exactly?
Arno: Currently, we need a special detection device to collect the Lament surveillance data.
Arno: The device detects and marks datasets that fit the criteria. Just walk near them to collect automatically.
Arno: But it cannot run continuously due to high energy consumption. If you fail to gather enough data in a set time, it will shut down automatically.
Arno: Please help us collect as many datasets as possible to yield an accurate result.
Arno: By the way, we have also set up temporary machanisms at the target locations. They'll activate along with the detection device.
Arno: If you are interested, my assistant KU-Ryan will walk you through the data collection process.
Arno: I'll be waiting for you &#32;&#91;sic&#93; good news, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
(Quest Complete)
