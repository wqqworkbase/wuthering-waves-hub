Summary[]

Cartethyia quietly stares at the Septimontian plaza. There is a hint of bitterness in her gaze, but the three wishes she once made have been fulfilled.

Steps[]

- Go to the restaurant and talk to Cartethyia
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest awards  Her Wishes and Moments with You trophy Street Where Wind Resides.
