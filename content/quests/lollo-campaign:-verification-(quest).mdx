Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionThe Lollo Campaign is facing an unexpected crisis! To save Maji's career, and to keep those convenient services running, help him verify the reliability of Lollo Campaign!

(Talk to Maji)
 Heard you messed up again?
Maji: Hello, old friend. What took you so long? I'm having the crisis of a lifetime over here!
Maji: Wait. That crisis has got your name on it as well!
&#160;?
 No need to be overly dramatic if you're asking for help.
Maji: Ahem. Remember that Lollo Stamp exchange QoL improvement I proposed after being transferred to HQ?
Maji: And the end result is that from now on, you won't ever need to personally pick up orders from me anymore. The system will automatically send contracts and Lollo Stamps directly to you, like bringing a glass of hot milk to your bed right before bedtime.
 That's a strange way of putting it.
Maji: The point is, how did you like its convenience?
 It did come in handy, now that you've mentioned it.
Maji: I bet you liked it, right? But that'll be the last of it.
Rover: What?!
Maji: Whoa. First time seeing you give off a face like that. Didn't realize it'd be that big of a blow.
Maji: Don't you worry. There's always a silver lining.
Maji: Our accounting says there are a few things I need to iron out first. Things like, lacking user incentives, not enough granularity in the work flow, and not being able to capitalize data in private domains.
 You still have that How to Talk Like a Real Business Elite on you?
 Now there's that old Maji I know of.
Maji: That's what happens when you're a cog in a wheel. Stuck spinning for life.
Maji: Anyway. What do you think will happen if my first project gets turned down right when it goes live once I'm over at HQ?
Rover: I suppose...
Maji: I'll get pushed around by my colleagues. Start getting assigned work at stations out in the middle of nowhere. Unreasonable tasks, one after the other. And then finally, I'll be forced to hand in my resignation!
Maji: Which is why I went and found the head of accounting, had my knees give the floor a gentle touch, plus a little kiss with my forehead on the ground while I was at it. And voila. They had no choice but to give me a second chance!
 Simply awe-inspiring, O great Maggie.
 I have a feeling you'll make them pay it back twofold.
Maji: For my salary, and for the happiness of my old friend. I, Maji, will do anything!
Maji: All right. Same as usual, the panel will post daily updates of requests, which you'll easily breeze through, and the Lollo Stamps will come flying into your pocket.
Maji: And remember, friend. My own fate, and the fate of you not having to travel across the entire Solaris just to reach me for rewards, depends on you and your accomplishments in the upcoming days!
Maji: Good luck, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! With our powers we shall unite our customers, and force the evil-doers at accounting flatten and overthrow themselves!
Rover: (Pretty sure that last part was his true goal...)
(Quest Complete)
