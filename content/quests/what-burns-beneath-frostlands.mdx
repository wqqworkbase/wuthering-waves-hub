Summary[]

As soon as you enter the Academy, your frequency begins to stabilize. Lynae, still concerned,  mentions that where you fell was right in the middle of an active Voidworm zone.
When you ask her why she was in such a zone, she pleads for you to keep what happened a secret, saying she can't afford another mark of absence on her record. Just then, her classmate messages her about a study matter. She promises to meet you later at the infirmary before rushing off.

On the way to the infirmary, Abby notes that while your frequency has stabilized, the lost portion is still missing. It suspects the Voidworm absorbed it, since the creature carries the same Threnodian scent as the "eyeball" you saw in the strange spatial domain you passed through to reach Lahai-Roi.

You decide to fix your fading frequency first, then find out more about the girl you saw in the spatial domain. Not far away, Lynae quietly shuts off a machine that seems to be monitoring you. She clearly knows more about you than she's letting on.
You arrive at the infirmary, and Lynae hurries in shortly after. A medical robot named N.A.N.A. greets you and performs a checkup.
Aside from a few scratches, she immediately notes your abnormal frequency loss. After hearing your situation, she says Dr. Herssen will give the final diagnosis.

Dr. Luuk Herssen reviews your report: your frequency is stable and you're not in immediate danger, but the portion already lost cannot be traced. The Research Institute, he says, has more thorough scanning equipment than the infirmary. He suggests you finish enrolling at the Academy to obtain a "Cassette," a permit and ID needed to enter the Institute.
Curiously, he whispers "Welcome back" as you leave.

Outside the faculty office, you overhear teachers discussing the unusual Voidworm activity and the Academy's countermeasures. Inside, a teacher named Voss confirms your identity and assigns Lynae to guide you through enrollment procedures: registering your personal information and taking an ID photo.

After everything is complete, Voss hands you a student ID card. You are officially a Startorch Academy student.
She explains that the Academy's goal with the Exostrider is to take on Aleph-1, a Threnodian contained beyond the Stridergate by the Exostrider's giant sword.
With your student card, you can now visit the Research Institute and have your Cassette burned.

Lynae brings you to the loading bay to pick up your ride. The stern Signal Intelligent Guidance & Management Automaton, S.I.G.M.A., insists you read the Safety Regulations before driving it, and scolds Lynae for her speeding and constant bending of modification rules. Lynae argues back while dragging you away.

Taking a short break on the roadside, you both admire Lahai-Roi's scenery. Lynae remarks that although the sky is artificial, she's found more freedom under it than anywhere else.

After seeing the incident in the tunnel, Lynae seems preoccupied. She leads you to the station below the Research Institute and tells you that the Startorch Express will take you straight there. She wants to join you, but absolutely cannot skip her afternoon class. After parting with her, you board the train alone.
Watching from afar, Lynae's expression turns cold. "...Time to take care of my own business.'

You enter the Cassette Depository and accidentally activate a memory server. Amid the data of the deceased appears a researcher named Mornye. She stabilizes your overloaded frequency and calmly guides you to the burning room, though you catch a brief, fleeting look of shock in her eyes when she first sees you.

During the burning process, your memories materialize as data projections. You see everything you've experienced since arriving in Solaris, along with scenes you don't recall, scenes featuring Mornye.

After the burning is complete, a researcher discovers your frequency code matches a deleted, anonymous entry in the database. However, Mornye tells the researcher to override it instead of dwelling further into it.

Mornye hands you the finished Cassette tape, explaining that it serves as your ID and a Terminal that warns of Void Storms. These storms disintegrate the frequencies of any living thing they touch, and the Cassette can slow that effect to some extent. As for recovering the frequency you've already lost, she cannot make any promises.

A sudden call interrupts the conversation: two professors are arguing over the artificial sun's launch procedure, and Mornye is urgently needed. You follow her to the Artificial Sun Lab.

In the Artificial Sun Lab, Researchers Warren and Anslem are in a heated dispute. Warren supports following the Roya's predicted launch window for the New Solar Ceremony, while Anslem refuses to trust their calculations. Mornye mediates, siding with the original plan. Warren stresses that the Hyvatia Detector's measurements are critical, and Anslem leaves after issuing a warning.

Mornye invites you to join the ceremony, and smiles when you agree. At that moment— a Void Storm corrupts Hyvatia, the Reactor Drive's surface detector for inspection and maintenance, triggering it to lose control. It now targets the Institute itself. On the surveillance feed, you see Lynae, who was supposedly in class, standing before it.
The crisis looms. You race off on the institute backup motorbike.

Lynae receives a message from a "liaison" who should be dead. Tracing the signal to its final location, she encounters contaminated Exoswarm.

Lynae follows the trail and finds the Fractsidus liaison holding a strange device in front of the Hyvatia Detector. He mocks her for wavering between "fugitive" and "guardian," claiming her indecision has caused him trouble. Threatening to expose her identity, he forces her to choose a side.
As Lynae refuses, he activates the device, corrupting the Hyvatia Detector and escaping.

You and Lynae work together to stop Hyvatia's rampage. When you ask why she was there, she hesitates to explain.
Warren arrives and is horrified by the damage. He demands to know why she was at the Institute during class hours and suspects her involvement. Lynae remains silent, as if holding something back.
Just as she prepares to use her Forte to escape, you intervene. You point out that she helped prevent a catastrophe and had no motive for sabotage, calling Warren's accusation premature. Ultimately, he orders you both back to the Academy for a formal investigation.

Back at the Academy, Lynae is questioned by Warren and other faculty. She recounts the Hyvatia malfunction, but you notice she omits any mention of the "liaison." Warren points out that her testimony about the "contaminated Exoswarm" doesn't match the scene investigation or surveillance footage. He questions her motives, especially in light of a recent security breach in the Institute's database. Although you and Voss speak in her defense, Lynae cannot explain the key inconsistencies.
After everyone leaves, you ask her directly why she didn't tell the whole truth. After a pause, she asks to continue the conversation somewhere quieter.

Lynae finally reveals her true identity: she is a former government-hired mercenary from the New Federation's "Lawless Zone." During a mission, she found an admission letter in one of the dead's belongings. Seeing a chance to escape her old life, she took on the dead girl's name and identity to enroll in Startorch Academy.
What she didn't expect was that the dead girl, the real "Lynae," had been a Fractsidus plant, sent to infiltrate the Academy. Wanting to stay in the place she's come to call home, she asks to work with you to uncover the truth and thwart the Fractsidus's plans.
She then takes out the device that caused Hyvatia's malfunction and prepares to investigate its origin in the Department of Engineering.

After examining the mysterious device disguised as a calibrator, Lynae discovers it can store and amplify Void Storm frequencies, triggering malfunctions in any creation connected to the Exostrider. The maker is likely someone inside the Academy, capable of remotely activating it at any moment. Handing it over to the authorities might just return it to the Fractsidus mole. To stop this, Lynae decides to overwrite the device's permissions and take control of it herself.

With you providing cover, the overwrite is successful. During the process, she finds evidence of admin-level access to the Academy's mechs and the Voidmatter Barrier security logs. The device contains three frequency waveforms. One belongs to Hyvatia, and the other two are unidentifiable.
You both decide to investigate the source of these waveforms at the Spacetrek Archive.

At the Archive, with help from the AI administrator I.R.I.S., you search for information tied to the waveform data. The search eventually narrows to two keywords: Void Storm and Exoswarm Contamination.

Based on your findings, you conclude that the Fractsidus intends to use the device to trigger a Voidworm rampage, with the most likely target being the Research Institute. You decide to alert the Institute immediately. Lynae chooses to stay behind and prepare a backup plan to boost the device's power, thereby luring the Voidworm toward the Spacetrek Collective's defensive systems. Unbeknownst to you, her overwrite of the device has already been detected by the Fractsidus "contact."

The Academy teacher finds Lynae again for a second round of investigation about the Hyvatia incident. They present an anonymous report accusing her of falsifying her admission records, linking her to a criminal record under the name "Lynae." Lynae calmly accepts the investigation and is placed under dormitory confinement. Before she is led away, she secretly passes you a message, entrusting the next phase of the plan to you.

You seek out Dr. Herssen and share your suspicion that Fractsidus agents have infiltrated Startorch Academy. Surprised by your trust, he doesn't confirm your theory outright but promises to liaise with the Research Institute and support your investigation.

You visit Lynae in her dorm to tell her that you've already warned the Institute. She listens calmly, invites you to sit, and begins talking about her past. She describes her bleak life as a mercenary in the New Federation and how everything became brighter and freer since coming to the Academy. But her life here is ending. She tells you what her disciplinary result is—expulsion and repatriation to the New Federation.
With a bitter smile, she says, "This stolen school life was always gonna end," and bids you farewell. She refuses to accept being sent back and plans to leave on her own.
Just as you ask what she truly wants, a Void Storm strikes. You realize the Voidworm that was supposed to target the Research Institute is now heading straight for the Academy. When you look up, Lynae is already gone.

You are contacted by Mornye, who you quickly brief her on your plans into giving you the locations of the Institute's anti-Voidworm defenses. Mornye warns that the defence arrays need to be manually activated on-site due to remote access being down from the Void Storm. You decide to stick to the plan and lure the creature away from the Academy.

You meet up with Lynae who was found protecting another student from the Voidworm and trying to seal the barrier. Before she gets attacked by the contaminated Exoswarm, you save her in the nick of time and quickly fill her in on your plans while luring the Voidworm away from the Academy. At the Institute's anti-Voidworm defense area, you and Lynae split up. While you activate the other arrays, Lynae went to distract the Voidworm to buy you time.

Lynae burns her Resonance power to keep the Voidworm distracted, she reaches her limit and the Voidworm then head towards Rover activating the defense arrays. However, you activate the final array just in time, trapping the Voidworm. You and Lynae work together once more, to take it down once and for all. 

You and Lynae are surrounded by excited students and reporters. Amid the questions, Lynae, normally so unflappable, shows a rare, nervous smile. Just then, an Academy officer approaches. Prepared for expulsion, Lynae is instead met with a decision that fills her with joy: due to her bravery, the Academy will allow her to take the entrance exam under her real identity. Lynae accepts, and you watch her cheerful figure depart. Just then, a faint, familiar voice whispers in your ear: "...Protect the sun."
It seems the crisis is far from over.

Glossary[]

- Voidworm - An amalgamated horror that often emerges alongside Void Storms. During lulls between Storms, it primarily inhabits the highly concentrated Voidmatter environment of the Mawburrow Desert. The Voidmatter Regulators on its head serve as both its power source and its weakness.

- Infirmary - Officially the "Resonator Nursing Unit," the infirmary is a clinical-research hybrid facility overseen by Rabelle College.
Managed by Academy physician Luuk Herssen, it utilizes Frequency Therapeutics to provide comprehensive healthcare for the students and faculty of Startorch Academy.
