Summary[]

As you find it impossible to pass through the storm looming over Reyes Islands, you head towards the nameless island Ahab mentioned. You attempt to dispel the storm following the inscription on the ancient steles, but to no avail. In the end, it's Starbuck's peculiar method of "offering his love" to the island that finally calms the storm.
When the last lighthouse becomes operational again, Cetus the Tidebreaker returns to its usual gentle self. Meanwhile, Phoebe has also made progress in her investigation of the island.
After deciphering the ancient tablets, Phoebe belives the Tidebreaker is tasked to guide the lost souls to the realm beyond, and the "siren" invading these waters may be a creature similar in nature to Lorelei. With the information at hand, the two of you seek Captain Ahab's aid to deal with the "siren."
As the storm subsides, you meet the Tidebreaker again after it carried you back from the depths of the sea. Phoebe wishes to express her apology to the beast for what happened during the Carnevale, but the Tidebreaker holds no grudge. Maybe one day in the future, as a pro angler, you might be able to reel the big guy in.

Steps[]

- Got the new ship blueprint. Go deliver it to Queequeg at the shipyard

- Deliver the required materials to continue Pequod's repairs

- Collect 200 Palm Logs

- Collect 10,000 Riccioli Fishery Coins

- Retrieve "Pequod Blueprint: Advanced"

- Locate Ahab and learn about the final island

- Sail from the dock to the small island designated by Ahab

- Discuss the island with Phoebe and begin the investigation.

- Find clues around the lighthouse

- Set sail to seek the "Gift" hidden in the ocean

- Solve the first ghost's riddle

- Catch 1 Sob Blob

- Deliver the "gift" at the first location

- Solve the second ghost's riddle

- Catch 1 Dead Man's Eye

- Deliver the "gift" at the second location

- Solve the third ghost's riddle

- Catch 1 Clione

- Deliver the "gift" at the third location

- Light up the lighthouse on Reyes island

- Find Phoebe and exchange the gathered clues

- Find Ahab

- Examine the note left by Ahab

- Meet Brant aboard "Pequod" to set sail

- Defeat Lorelei's Kindred: Outcast of the Depths

- Wait for Phoebe to recover. Adjust the time to between 4:00 and 9:00

- Go investigate Cetus the Tidebreaker near Reyes Island
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing "Deliver the required materials to continue Pequod's repairs" step awards Rover's Journey II trophy Elijah's Prophecy.

- Completing "Light up the lighthouse on Reyes island" step awards Rover's Journey II trophy Lord Keeper of the Light.

- Completing the quest awards Rover's Journey II trophy Saving Fish from Drowning.
