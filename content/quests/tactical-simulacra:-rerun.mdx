 Quest DescriptionThe Academy is recruiting volunteers for another experiment. Find Hongjing to learn more.

(Talk to Hongjing)
Hongjing: Excuse me, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). A moment of your time, please.
Hongjing: We're running a new testing phase for the research project you previously participated. Are you interested in joining us again?
 I'm interested.
Hongjing: Great. Allow me to introduce our research once more.
 I'm not interested.
Hongjing: No need to decline just yet. Allow me to introduce our research once more.
Hongjing: I'm sure you have experience with Instructor Yhan's simulated training grounds.
Hongjing: It's where our warriors go to train against holograms of enemies and improve their combat skills.
Hongjing: A while back, the Academy began testing an improved "projection" technology to simulate even stronger enemies.
Hongjing: If the technology is proven effective, we can provide more comprehensive training for our soldiers. This means better results for their overall needs.
Hongjing: We managed to make significant technical breakthroughs from the previous test, but many minute details still need ironing out.
Hongjing: Would you be willing to test it out for us and help with our research again?
 Leave it to me.
Hongjing:I knew I could count on you.
 What am I getting paid?
Hongjing:Rest assured, having been our top participant in the previous testing, your efforts will be supported by the best resources the Academy has to offer.
 Any longtime client benefits?
Hongjing: Rest assured, having been our top participant in the previous testing, your efforts will be supported by the best resources the Academy has to offer.
Hongjing: The access to the simulation is right beside me. Trust me, the rewards will not disappoint.
(Quest Complete)
