 Just a friendly tourist who happened to pass by.

 I do.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Well, then that'll make explaining all the easier. It has been troubling us since we first learned of the invitation's existence. After all, the Deep Water Zone has always been a realm we've yet to peer into its secrets.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Data samples collected from Septimont reveal a similar frequency. We reckon this was how the invitation made its way to you after traversing the Deep Water Zone through utilizing the frequency's unique signatures.
 Not really.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Worry not. Long story short, we managed to form a general idea of the reason behind the sudden burst in activity of the Somnoire's depths.
Ebony Gatekeeper: However, we still lack sufficient samples to verify our hypothesis. If you don't mind, we'd like to have you take out the remaining Nightmare Tacet Discords.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Their coordinates have been sent to your Terminal. Per usual, you will be rewarded for your hard work. We appreciate your aid, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).

Ivory Gatekeeper: ...Meow.

Ebony Gatekeeper: Yet, so far, we are unable to identify the frequency's origin.

Ebony Gatekeeper: Well, then. The situation within the Deep remains unclear, but there is no doubt that the Somnoire and Rinascita will continue to fall victim under this frequency's influence.
