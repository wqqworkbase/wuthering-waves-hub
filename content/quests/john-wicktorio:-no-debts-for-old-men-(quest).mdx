Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionJohn Wicktorio from Egla Town is in a spot of trouble.

(Idle)
Wicktorio: Alright, time for a coin toss again...
Wicktorio: Tail. Tails. And... tails.
Wicktorio: So, that settles it. Praise be to Imperator... I shall punish the wicked and fulfill the divine will.

(Talk to Wicktorio)
Wicktorio: Ahem... Apologies, friend from afar. Didn't realize you were watching.
 Is something wrong?
Wicktorio: Just a pain in the neck... When you get old, trouble finds you everywhere.
Wicktorio: Last night, some thugs broke into my house looking for documents. Daisy caught them in the act...
Wicktorio: She rang the alarm and tried to wake me up, but they were armed. Tried to cover their tracks... Daisy didn't make it.
 How could they!
Abby: That's awful! How could they?! Jerks!
Wicktorio: Daisy's been with me for twenty years... twenty whole years. If someone told me back then that people would harm the Order's Echo, I'd simply call them crazy.
Abby: So Daisy was an Echo? Wait, I'm an Echo too! How dare they hurt a poor little Echo!
Wicktorio: I lost everything after my wife passed away twenty years ago—until Daisy showed up at my doorstep. She was my wife's last gift to me... and now she's taken away too.
 What do you need?
Wicktorio: ...Guns. Lots of guns.
Abby: Huh?!
Wicktorio: I'm old now, but in my younger days, the Primus always told me: "Wicktorio, there is no need for extreme measures."
Wicktorio: "As an accountant of the Order, you must act professional and decent..."
 Is accounting that intense?
Wicktorio: Do it long enough, and you'll understand. It's all about the numbers. As long as the books balance, anything's fair game.
Wicktorio: After I retired, my buddy Cosimo once told me I couldn't just "shoot" my problems away.
Wicktorio: but when the problems kept piling up, I thought: why not deal with the people who created theose problems?
Abby: I get the logic... but it's a stretch...
Wicktorio: Anyway, my friends... how about joining me for a little adventure? I can tell you've got potential. And don't worry, my savings will make it worth your while.
Wicktorio: Nothing complicated, just some debt collecting.
 Alright.
Wicktorio: Good. I'll show you where they're hiding. You can handle them however you like. The reward will be worth it.
(Quest Complete)
