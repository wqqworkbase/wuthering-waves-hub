Steps[]

- Complete charging the steles at the first altar

- Complete charging the steles at the second altar

- Complete charging the steles at the third altar

- Find the right angle to fix the steles

- Complete charging the steles at the third altar

- Return to inspect the remains of the Lumiscale Construct

- Defeat the Lumiscale Construct

- Pursue the Lumiscale Construct

- Defeat the enemies blocking your path

- Pursue the Lumiscale Construct

- Defeat the Lumiscale Construct

- Defeat the enemies
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest grants the Footprints in Rinascita I trophy The Wind Ends Here.
