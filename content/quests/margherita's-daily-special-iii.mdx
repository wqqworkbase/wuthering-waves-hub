Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionThanks to your support, Lelia has found the courage to pursue a career of cooking. Stop by and taste her dish when you're free.

(Taste Lelia's dish)
Lelia: Oh, you got here just in time. I finally finished my firt dish.
Lelia: I choose something familiar to ease into it. So here's my Nuvola Pasta. Please give it a try!
The pasta lies beneath a thick layer of sauce, its strands barely recognizable as they seem to squirm. A questionable aroma wafts up from the dish.
Cautiously, you take a bite. For a moment, the mushy texture and sharp aroma overwhelm your senses. A heavy cloud seems to close in around you... No, it's just an illusion.
Lelia: So... How is it? Is the flavor alright?
 ...
Lelia: What is it? Say something, please!
 It's quite strong, almost a little dizzying.
 It's good, but next time go easier on it.
Lelia: Oops, sorry. That must've been the Mastic Nuvola. I thought adding a little more would make it tastier for you.
 A little seasoning goes a long way.
 It's all about balance.
Lelia: Understood. I'll adjust it right away.
Lelia: You seem to know a lot about cooking. Could I get some tips from you next time you try one of my dishes?
 No problem.
Lelia: Great! I'll be practicing more recipes&#8212;you won't be disappointed, I promise!
 There's next time?
Lelia: Please, your advice means so much to me! I'll get better, I promise!
 Okay, I'll come by again.
(Quest Complete)
