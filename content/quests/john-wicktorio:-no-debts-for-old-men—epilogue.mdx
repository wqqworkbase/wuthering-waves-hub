Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionThe tasks is complete. Wicktorio wants to discuss your... fees.

(Talk to Wicktorio)
Wicktorio: Good evening, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
 Is it that late already?
 You look refreshed now.
Wicktorio: Well, we've both had a busy day. Time for a drink, don't you think?
Wicktorio: So, care to share what you found inside?
You tell Wicktorio what happened.
Wicktorio: The gold coins they hoarded were old currency that was meant to be issued but abandoned after a currency swap.
Wicktorio: Given Ragunna's trade history over the past two decades, they probably planned to exploit the information gap and run cons overseas. That's why they needed those documents.
 Why was it abandoned?
Wicktorio: They made too many. Way more than the market could handle.
Wicktorio: People always believe more money is better, but there's a limit. Once it's breached, everything spirals—a chain reaction.
Wicktorio: As you can see Ragunna eventually adopted Shell Credits as its stable currency.
 What did you do to those people?
Wicktorio: Them? Heh. Let's just say I hope the coins I placed on their eyes cover their fare to the underworld.
Wicktorio: Sentinel above, forgive the violence. But for those blinded by greed, it was the only fitting end.
Abby: Wow... like a scene straight out of that Ragunnesi play.
Wicktorio: By the way, here's the reward I promised. As for the extra Shell Credits, consider them a handling fee for converting that oudated currency.
Wicktorio: In life, you either handle the people or settle the score. Peace doesn't come easy, even after retirement.
Wicktorio: Anyway, this old man's heading back now. Stop by Egla Town anytime. You're always welcome.
(Quest Complete)
