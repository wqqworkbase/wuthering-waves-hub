Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou meet Xuanyin again. This time, she is trying to talk to a Gray Crested Gull...

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: Great to see you again, Player.
Xuanyin: Can you guess which animal translation module I brought this time?
 I picked up the call of the Gray Crested Gull from a distance.
Xuanyin: Excellent hearing! Yes, it's the Bird Language Translation Module. The water areas around Jinzhou are a crucial route for the Gray Crested Gulls' migration. However, some stupid idiots often feed them...
Rover: Hey, watch the language...
Xuanyin: My apologies. From my observations, due to the increasingly harsh environment, a small number of the Gray Crested Gulls have abandoned their instincts of migration and become resident birds.
Xuanyin: The language of these resident birds has also evolved. In addition to communication among their kind, there are some calls directed at humans.
Xuanyin: Perhaps they're attempting to communicate with humans. Let's collect some samples to find out.
Xuanyin loads the Bird Language Translation Module onto your Pangu Terminal.
Gray Crested Gull (Translation): Give! Me! Give! Me! Gimme, gimme, gimme!
Xuanyin: Give you what?
Xuanyin: I heard that the Gray Crested Gulls have a thing for human food, especially Star Flakes. Are they asking us for food?
 We'll know when we give them Star Flakes.
Xuanyin: You're a hidden chef, aren't you? I'm counting on you this time too!
Xuanyin: By the way, the Gray Crested Gulls are infamous for their appetite for snatching food from others. If we have food in our hands, they should be rushing in to grab it.
Xuanyin: Ah-choo. Sorry. I stayed up all night, trying to build good relations with them.
Xuanyin: Ended up not only losing my food but also catching a cold. Haha.

(Deliver a Star Flake to Xuanyin)
A Gray Crested Gull grabs the Star Flakes from you by surprise and flies away, hovering above your head and mocking you.
Xuanyin: Are they mocking us?
Xuanyin: According to the information I've looked up, aside from Star Flakes, other fried foods are also very appealing to them.
Xuanyin: As the Gray Crested Gulls enjoy stealing others' food, they are also known as "Pirate Gulls".
 That's interesting.
Xuanyin: Yes. They are frequently spotted around "wharves" and places where potato fries are sold.
Gray Crested Gull (Translation): Gimme! *garbled text*
Xuanyin: Why is the module freezing? I'd better uninstall it for now. Seems like there is no storage on your Pangu Terminal.
Xuanyin uninstalls the Bird Language Translation Module from your Pangu Terminal and transmits the related data back to her.
Xuanyin: Ah-choo, ah-choo, I need to hurry back and warm myself up. See you next time.
(Quest Complete)
