We Promise, We Deliver is an Exploration Quest. The quest takes place primarily in the Port City of Guixu (however starts in Jinzhou), and is the prerequisite to dispelling the Incinero Petal Toxin effect from the floor of the Sea of Flames.

Steps[]

- Check the "Wanted Poster" at the bulletin

- Check the mail sent from Lollo Logistics

- Go to the Jinzhou branch of Lollo Logistics for details

- Talk to Tang Manliu

- Proceed to Mimika's touchpad location

- Investigate the touchpad on the ground

- Defeat the mysterious Tacet Discord

- Talk to Tang Manliu and Mimika

- Talk to Mimika by the Nexus Beacon

- Search for the source of SOS in Sea of Flames

- Head to the first abandoned research facility

- Talk to the Exiles in the abandoned research facility

- Complete the shooting puzzle

- Interact with the shooting mechanics

- Tell the Exiles the results

- Head to the second abandoned research facility

- Defeat the Exile mobsters laying siege to the research facility

- Talk to the Exiles in the abandoned research facility

- Complete the fire thrower puzzle

- Use Levitator to connect fuel tank pipelines

- Tell the Exiles the results

- Defeat the Exile mobsters in the camp

- Retrieve the filter

- Activate the suspicious Pressure Platforms

- Activate 4 Pressure Platforms within 30s

- Activate the Training Dummy

- Hack into the Signals Console

- Open the Supply Chest

- Take the filter to the third abandoned research facility

- Head to the edge of Sea of Flames and talk to Mimika

- Complete the test of intensity of the Spear body

- Find 3 standard barrels

- Talk to Mimika again

- Head to the assembly site and talk to Woodrow

- Head to the Sea of Flames to test the Explosive Spears

- Use Elevation to throw Explosive Spears at the gathering TD

- Reach the designated location

- Protect the Sound Emulator and defeat all Tacet Discords

- Talk to Woodrow and confirm the test results

- Meet up with Mimika and the others

- Defeat Inferno Rider

- Use the Explosive Spear to destory Inferno Rider's shield

- Absorb the Echo of Inferno Rider

- Shape-shift into Inferno Rider

- Dash until reaching the end of the Gravity Storm

- Head back to the Jinzhou branch of Lollo Logistics
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest awards the Huanglong I trophy Honor My Slogan.
