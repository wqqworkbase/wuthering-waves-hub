Steps[]

- Ask Fulmine about the photos

- Capture the scenery of Rinascita (0/7)

- Follow the clues in the Traces of Tides to complete the Rinascita photo set
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionFulmine, the manager of Wutherium Film Studio, looks worried these days. The deadline for the next periodical is around the corner, but he seems to have trouble picking the right photos... he beseeched the Laureate for help.

(Talk Fulmine)
 Traces of Tides
Fulmine: Here you are! Actually, I've come to ask you for a favor today...
Fulmine: The deadline for the new issue of Wutherium Film Studio's publication is approaching, and this time, we plan to capitalize on a trending topic.
 What topic?
Fulmine: Oh! No need to be modest, of course it's about you— the latest Laureate in Ragunna!
 Is it about me?
Fulmine: Of course, you are the latest Laureate in Ragunna, after all!
Fulmine: As you know, publishing is tough these days, and if sales keep lagging, headquarters will probably cut our budget again.
Fulmine: We've been following your work in Ragunna and thought if you, the Laureate, photographed a travelogue of Ragunna, it would surely be a hit!
Fulmine: So we'd like to commission you to take a series of photos for this issue to help boost sales.
Fulmine: Any profit from this will go entirely to you. Please, we're counting on you!
 Alright
 All you have to do is ask.
Fulmine: Thank you so much! We've already set the coordinates for some of Ragunna's landmarks. I'll send them to you now!

(Complete Traces of Tides I)
Fulmine: Have you taken the first photo yet? Let me take a look!
 Send the photo
Fulmine: ...!
Fulmine: What a perfect shot! Brilliant composition, excquisite colors!
Fulmine: Please, don't limite yourself to photography. What I mean is, your aesthetic should not be confined by the camera!
Fulmine: This is where my good partner comes in! Mr. C-MOSS! With your permission, it can capture the scenery you envision. Please, roam Ragunna freely and collect the scenes that speak to you.

(Approach Traces of Tides II)
Nameless Knight: Forward, the knight's mission is to move ahead... I understand.
Nameless Knight: But the knight's attire has been with me for half my life. Now, who can give meaning to the knight's path forward?
