Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionA vibrant painting in the wild, a landscape drained of its colors, and a mysterious, suspicious communication...
What is the true intent behind the caller? Head to Egla Town and speak with her to uncover the truth.

(Upon completing the first Overflowing Palette challenge)
Rover: (A call from an unknown contact... What could it be?)
???: Hey, is it Rover? I hope I've finally reached the right person...
 Yes, speaking. And you are?
???: Hello, I'm Vida, running a modest business in Egla Town.
 I'm not interested in your product, thank you.
???: Oh, don't worry. I don't do cold calls. I run a small business here in Egla Town. Just call me Vida.
 I've seen you before, haven't I?
 What can I do for you?
Vida: Have you come across these paintings of vibrant colors standing in a dreary field in the wild?
 As if the world around it has lost its color?
 As if the world around it has lost its vitality?
Vida: Sure, it seems you've encountered them already. The Pioneer Association refers to those Tacet Discords as "Overflowing Palettes", and the areas they affect are known as "Faded Zones".
Vida: Such strange phenomena have quietly spread throughout Rinascita, leaving ordinary people completely helpless...
 I've just restored a spot.
Vida: Wait... you did? I knew you were the right person to talk to.
Vida: Please find me in Egla Town when you have a moment. I have a favor to ask of you and we can discuss the details when we meet.
Rover: (The call has ended.)
Abby: Yawn - Just woke up and already hear you guys chatting...
Abby: Wait a minute, this person seems suspicious. How does she know your contact details?
 We'll find out.
 For I'm the famous Rover!

(Talk to Vida in Egla Town)
Vida: You're here at last! I was just wondering how much longer I'd have to wait.
 Why did you suddenly reach out to me?
Vida: Because... 'Here's to a world of discoveries and wonders.'
Vida: Hmm, sounds familiar. Isn't that the slogan of the Pioneer Association?
 Are you with the Pioneer Association?
Vida: I used to be. Back when I was a traveling merchant, I also worked briefly as a contract photographer for the Pioneer Association, capturing landscapes along the trade routes to earn some travel expenses.
Vida: Now... as you can see, those strange phenomena have drained the color from many places, making it harder to capture great photos. I've settled down and opened my own shop, so I haven't renewed my contract.
Vida: But deep down, I am still unable to move past the faded landscapes.
 Do you want me to restore those areas?
Vida: Always feels like a breeze to chat with a sharp mind like yours. That is exactly why I got your contact from a former colleague. After all, no one wants the places in their memories to be covered in dull patches, right?
Vida: If wa can only see those familiar landscapes in photos from now on... its a depressing thought, isn't it?
 Let's talk terms first.
 How about a little something to sweeten the deal?
Vida: Certainly, I wouldn't expect you to help for free. The Faded Zones often hold abudant mineral deposits and resources. But they can't be accessed due to the influence of the Overflowing Palettes.
Vida: Restoring the zones will not only bring back the colors but also the resources. You can trade them with me for some... rare items on the market.
Abby: Sounds like this is her true intention...
Vida: As a merchant, it's my fundamental skill. So, what do you think? Those goods and offers are exclusively for partners.
 Understood, let's seal the deal.
Vida: Alright, take a look at what's on the shelves. The resources are just what you need right now.

Vida: Thank you for your patronage. From now on, we're long-term business partners.
Vida: Let's work together to reclaim the natural colors of our world!
(Quest Complete)
