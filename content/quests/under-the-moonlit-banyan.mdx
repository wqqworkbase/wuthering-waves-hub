Steps[]

- Go to Violet Banyan

- Talk to Bei Shi

- Investigate around Violet Banyan

- Talk with Lingyang
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest unlocks 24 hours after finishing When the Forest is No Longer Dim.

- Completing the quest will grant the Moments with You trophy Crescent Moon on the Banyan Tree.
