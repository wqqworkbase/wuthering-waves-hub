Ecological Field Notes on Scourgewings(The page is filled with scattered, recently penned notes.)

As the name implies, Scourgewing dragons are often associated with ill omens in folklore, their appearance believed to be a harbinger of disaster. The first wave of sightings coincided with the eve of the first Dark Tide. Since then, their silhouettes have been reported ahead of nearly every major catastrophe.

But is such a connection real? Unlikely. It's a classic case of mistaking correlation for causation. In truth, Scourgewings don't bring disaster, certainly not the Dark Tide. They merely appear beforehand.

...

Some ecologists argue that the creature's frequency dates all the way back to the Age of a Thousand Islands. Though rare, the species has endured, likely owing to their acute sensitivity to environmental changes. The antennae on their heads can detect subtle environmental shifts, allowing them to avoid danger preemtptively. The pattern we've observed points to a more grounded explanation: the Scourgewings sense the coming of disaster, and perhaps, in their own way, try to warn us.

They are not harbingers of disaster, but early messengers.

...
