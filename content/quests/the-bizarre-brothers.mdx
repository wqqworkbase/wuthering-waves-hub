Steps[]

- Meet the three brothers again between 6:00 to 18:00 after completing the challenge

- Return two days later

- Inspect the statue mentioned by the three brothers between 6:00 to 18:00
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest awards the Footprints in Rinascita II trophy Don't Frate About It.
