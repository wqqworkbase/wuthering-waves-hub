Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionFairy Tale's Finale: Epilogue

(Say hello)
"Dorothy": Thank you! Now my friends can all go home!
 Your friends?
 I thought you said YOU wanted to go home?
"Dorothy": Yeah, the current "me" you're talking to belongs to the other side of the Gate.
"Dorothy": ...But when it comes to us in another point in time... Our home is right here, on this side.
 You live in the Somnoire?
 Who are you?
Dorothy falls silent, looking down at her toes.
"Dorothy?": Aww... I should've saw this coming. A child's dream persona is still too naive. Can't expect her to learn how to hide her true self...
"Dorothy?": Not like I ever wanted to hide it from you forever, though...
 So you lied to me.
"Dorothy?": Lying? Not quite. At least I can promise you... Everything I've told you this time is true.
"Dorothy?": Dorothy is a good girl. She won't ever intentionally lie to anyone, not even in a dream.
 You're that "Cosmos" from before, aren't you?
"Dorothy?": Not bad, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), you've figured it out already... Yes. You can say that was me.
"Dorothy?": I found the dream of a troublemaker and copied it into a Wooly plushie in the dream world.
"Dorothy?": And then... I was able to manifest in Solaris-3 and talk to you.
"Dorothy?": Figured it'd be simpler for us to talk like this, with our real positions and identities making things tricky.
"Dorothy?": Come on, don't give me that suspicious side eye... I offered to pay you accordingly. It's been a fair deal, right?
 What are you trying to do?
"Dorothy?": ...Sorry, I can't say. Not yet.
"Dorothy?": But it may not take long before the right time comes.
"Dorothy?": And when it happens... I hope you'll understand my choice, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
(Quest Complete)
