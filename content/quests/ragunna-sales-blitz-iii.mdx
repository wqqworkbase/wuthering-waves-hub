 Quest DescriptionAlannah seems to be interested in Huanglong's Terminals and Resonance Nexus...

(Talk to Alannah)

 A Gourd-shaped Terminal display model?

Alannah: Items with never-seen-before designs like these might pique their interest.
Alannah: Why don't we go take a look at the Resonance Nexus, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)?

(Talk to Mulan)
Mulan: Hello, welcome to Jinzhou.
Mulan: Allow me to give you a run-down on how to operate the Resonance Nexus.
Mulan: First, place your Pangu Terminal on the Nexus, and it will start loading the relevant information.
Mulan: Then, you can check your map and fast-travel to Jinzhou through the Nexus.
Mulan: Last but not least, thank you for choosing our services.
Alannah: I gotta say, whoever invented the Terminal was a genius.
Alannah: You can connect it to Beacons for information, use it for comms, and absorb Echoes with just a single button press.
 Never had a complaint.
 Resonators can't live without 'em.
Alannah: However, most of the people in Ragunna can only use the public Terminals. You'll need the Order's permission to have one for yourself.
 Isn't that inconvenient?
Alannah: Well, maybe. But they've probably gotten used to it by now I guess.
Alannah: Speaking of which, Ragunna Terminals come in the shape of lanterns! They look really pretty.
Alannah: I imagine they'd find the gourd-shaped design of the Huanglong Terminals interesting.
Alannah: Let's make scale models of the Pangu Terminals for merch!
Alannah: I'll start working on it straight away. With a real Gourd at hand, it should be a quick one!

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: Ohhh... Moments like this always give me goosebumps. Do you think they"ll like the Terminals?

(Talk to Alannah)

 That's great!

 A very optimistic three million copies?
 A very modest one million copies?
