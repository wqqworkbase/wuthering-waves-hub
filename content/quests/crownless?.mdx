Steps[]

- Talk to Maqi to find out about today's game scenario.

- Complete today's scenario

- (Optional) Find out about the Crownless

- Give the Crownless to Maqi
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionMaqi is trying to contact you, and it appears to be related to the Pioneer Association's ambitious project, "Second Coming of Solaris"...

(Talk to Maqi)
 Today's scenario...
Maqi: Let me explain our job today.
Maqi: In this chapter, the true identity of the protagonist's nemesis is finally revealed.
Maqi: According to the book, it's "...a towering figure with rough edges like a statue..."
Maqi: "An aura of brilliant stars surrounded this Crownless King like loyal courtiers..."
Maqi: "It nearly compelled me to bow down... I gathered all my strength from falling on my knees..."
 The Crownless?!
Maqi: Where did you learn that name?
 The Hatless, the big villain!
Maqi: Second Coming of Solaris has received overwhelming favor for this part. Fans of this book call this mighty figure The Crownless
 I see.
 Maybe I have to read this book after all.
Maqi: Really? We must have made a wise decision to pick you as our exclusive consultant.
Maqi: So you know the story.
Maqi: We want something to help the dev team to picture the image of the Crownless.
Maqi: If you happen to come across some samples, feel free to drop by.

(Optional - Talk to Maqi)
 Give me some clues about "The Crownless One".
Maqi: A ferocious opponent... The Crownless... Maybe you should ask someone who frequents the danger zone?
Maqi: Xianchen over there is an experienced Pioneer explorer. If you need help, how about asking her?

(Optional - Talk to Xianchen)
 About the Crownless King in the novel...
Xianchen: I've read Second Coming of Solaris. A good piece of work. I'd admit... But I've never finished it.
 May I ask why?
Xianchen: Because it makes me sweat bullets when reading the portrayal of the monsters.
Xianchen: Those monsters in the book... They must be the Tacet Discords.
 Do they really look that scary?
Xianchen: Yes, they do.
 I fight Tacet Discords a lot.
Xianchen: *sigh* I ventured into Tacet Fields a lot when I was young, wishing to get some first-hand reports about Tacet Discords.
Xianchen: But my adventure was cut short by an accident, where I was heavily injured by Tacet Discords. After that, I decided to leave the field and work for the support staff.
Xianchen: This is something I brought from the Tacet Field back in those days. Just take a look at this ominous structure. It's too weird to be a natural formation.
Xianchen: If you're after something related to monsters, just take it.
 Are you really okay with that?
 I can feel it's important to you.
Xianchen: Don't take it to your heart. Life is much better when you don't have to handle those Tacet Discords.
Xianchen: But it does remind me of those young Pioneer reporters. They can risk everything for a field trip about Tacet Discords. I can't help but worry about them.
Xianchen: Wait, no. Let's continue this topic next time. I'm off to talk to Zhuyi about his dangerous field trip...
(Obtain LF Whisperin Core x1)

(Talk to Maqi)

(Submit LF Whisperin Core x1)
 I'm turning in the Crownless One.
Maqi: How peculiar... I'll hand it over to the dev team right now. Thank you.
Maqi: I believe the team would have a much more vivid picture of the protagonist's first encounter with the Crownless.
Some time passed while Maqi was away to hand over the item...
Maqi: Thank you. That wraps up today's work!
 Big brother...
Maqi: So you know that name? Many take "Big Brother" as a nickname for the Crownless.
Maqi: Anyway, let's call it a day. See you tomorrow, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
(Quest Complete)
