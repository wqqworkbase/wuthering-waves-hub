Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionQinggong is recruiting volunteers for a research project. Talk to him to learn more.

(Talk to Qinggong)
 About the experiment...
Qinggong: Rover, have you got a moment?
Qinggong: Would you like to participate in our latest research project?
 Yes.
Qinggong: Great. Allow me to introduce our research.
 No.
Qinggong: Please, give us a chance. I can explain what the project is about first.
Qinggong: Have you been to Instructor Yhan's simulated training grounds?
Qinggong: Instructor Yhan would arrange simulations for his men to train against enemy holograms and improve their combat skills.
Qinggong: Recently, we have developed an improved "projection" technology to simulate even stronger enemies.
Qinggong: With this technology, we can provide more comprehensive training for our soldiers. This means better results for their overall needs.
Qinggong: We're still in the early stages of development, so there are some technical details we need to work out.
Qinggong: Would you be willing to test it out for us and help with our research?
 Sure.
Qinggong: I knew you were the right person to talk to. I've sent you the coordinates. Thank you for your help!
 What's in it for me?
Qinggong: You'll know once you get there. Trust me, it's gonna be worth your time.

(Optional - Talk to Qinggong again.)
 Tactical Simulacra
Qinggong: Temperature favorable. Humidity is...
 About the Academy's research project...
Qinggong: Oh, it's you. Have you tried testing our latest technology?
 Yes, I have.
Qinggong: With your assistance, I have faith that our technology will soon reach its full potential for our soldiers to utilize.
 Not yet.
Qinggong: Remember to check it out at some point, then! I've already signed up for you.
Qinggong: The look on your face tells me you've got something to ask.
 How did you learn about this research project?
Qinggong: You have keen eyes. As I expected.
Qinggong: I'm sorry, but I can't tell you now. Not yet.
 Are you really a researcher?
Qinggong: That is something I can't tell you now.
 Why are you not wearing a lab coat like the other researchers?
Qinggong: You have keen eyes. As I expected.
Qinggong: I'm sorry, but I can't tell you now. Not yet.
Qinggong: By the way, I have a small favor to ask. Can you keep quiet about my involvement with the Academy?
Qinggong: Thank you, Rover.

(Check the research project Qinggong talked about)
This should be the projection thing Qinggong was talking about... I should try it out.
(Quest Complete)
