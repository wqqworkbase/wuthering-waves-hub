Steps[]

- Talk to Geohide Saurian with the help of translation module

- Defeat the Geohide Saurian

- Talk to Xuanyin
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou meet Xuanyin again, who was buried in thoughts...

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: Silver-Banded Lizard, Azure Lizard, Green Pit Lizard... Oh, hey, Player, you're here.
 Are you listing dishes?
Xuanyin: Dishes? Where would you find such specialties?
Xuanyin: Oh my, where was I? Well, I'll start over again later.
Xuanyin: I was counting the language samples of lizards. The dialects of the lizards are almost catching up with the fish. It took me months to collect them all, but the performance of the translation module is somewhat not ideal.
 Do you have samples of the Viridblaze Saurian?
Xuanyin: Huh? The Viridblaze Saurian? I really haven't considered that.
Xuanyin: In terms of classification, the Viridblaze Saurian is a mimicry Tacet Discord, classified as a Howler.
Xuanyin: Though the Viridblaze Saurian and ordinary lizards have lots in common, it's a TD in essence. I don't have the courage to research it.
Xuanyin: But now with you here, I think I can give it a try.
Xuanyin loaded the Lizard Language Translation Module onto your Pangu Terminal.

(Talk to Geohide Saurian with the help of translation module)
Viridblaze Saurian (Translation): (Low growling.)
Xuanyin: Looks like it has something to say!
Viridblaze Saurian (Translation): Ahhhh! Daaaa!!!
Xuanyin: OH MY GOODNESS! It's tail is swinging over! The invincible Player, do something!

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Viridblaze Saurian (Translation):  Ahhh! Gurgle... *garbled text*
Xuanyin: The program crashed! Seems like this module can't be used on TDs! Quick, kill the background processes!
  Is this sample still useful?
Xuanyin: Let me see. Well, though they are all interjections, they still have great scientific potential.
Xuanyin: I shouldn't have messed with a TD. Fortunately, you were here.
Xuanyin: Today's trial and error was very valuable. Thank you for your help, Player.
(Quest Complete)
