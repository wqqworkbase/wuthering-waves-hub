Gameplay Notes[]

- Talking to Valentina the first time will unlock access to the Tidal Defense Simulator event.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionThe agents of the Black Shores have long been the first to respond in times of crisis, shouldering the responsibilities of fighting TD Outbreaks head on. The Black Shores has developed specialized combat devices designed to lower the risks they face in fighting the TDs—Valentina is now leading simulation tests of the devices, and she's counting on your assistance to push the tests forward.

(Idle)
Valentina: Progress report, KU-Uigon.
Ku-Uigon: Awaiting further data input. Please stand by, Valentina.

(Talk to Valentina)
KU-Uigon: Analysing data... Progress at 93.75%. The Rinascita data bundle has been fully recovered.
KU-Uigon: Initating regional TD ecosystem data import into the artificial Sonoro Sphere. Requesting computing assistance from the Tethys System. Estimated completion time: 366 seconds.
Valentina: Well done, KU-Uigon.
 What are you doing here, Valentina?
Valentina: Nice to see you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). As you can see, we're collecting data associated with the TD Outbreak for the Tidal Defense Simulator project.
 Tidal Defense Simulator?
Valentina: Yep. One of the many assignments as a Black Shores field agent is to assist local authorities in evacuating citizens in the event of Waveworn catastrophes or large-scale TD Outbreaks.
Valentina: But due to the high risks that often comes with the tasks, many Bloom Bearers gave their lives while performing their duties, just like... Niya and Hoda did in the previous Port City of Guixu incident.
 ...
Valentina: No need to feel sad, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). Their sacrifices are a testament to their dreams and oaths to protect others.
Valentina: Every Black Shores member knew what they signed up for. That includes both of us, isn't that right?
Valentina: To provide additional safety measures for our agents out on the field, the R&D department has developed a few autonomous Combat Machines by combining technologies of simulated Echoes and Tacetite weapons.
Valentina: The design purpose is to have them replace field personnel in delaying TD Outbreak attacks, buying time for evacuation. However, there are still some rough edges to be ironed out before they can be fully deployed.
Valentina: So to set us on the correct course for improvements, we're collecting TD Outbreak data to create a simulated environment for testing these devices. This is where the Tidal Defense Simulator project comes into play.
Valentina: On top of that, we hope to find more efficient deployment strategies through repeated simulations.
Valentina: To get the results we're looking for, I'd like to invite you to participate in the simulator project. With what you've achieved in multiple previous crisis trials, you'll certainly help us develop a flawless strategy.
 No problem.
 I'll do what I can.
Valentina: Thank you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Valentina: I'd like to point out that to verify the machines' combat efficiency, please refrain from using direct attack methods while in the Tidal Defense Simulator.
Valentina: Let's be honest, normal TDs are a cake walk for you, and if you get too involved, there won't be much left for the machines to do their thing.
Valentina: So, as an alternative, I'll have KU-Uigon and its offensive loadouts serve as your tactical support, which should simulate the combat capabilities of the average Bloom Bearer.
 Glad to have you with me, Ku-Uigon.
 Sure, I'll try my best to be gentle.
Valentina: Right, then KU-Uigon and I'll go get the Sonoro Sphere up and running. Good luck to you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
(Tidal Defense Simulator unlocked)

(Talk to Valentina after completing Total Defense Test)
Valentina: How's everything going so far, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)?
 Going very smoothly.
 Okay... I guess?
KU-Uigon: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)'s test results have been archived. Based on the data, Bloom Bearer (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;) has demonstrated extraordinary tactical capabilities.
KU-Uigon: Machines set up by (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;) produced a combat efficiency 52.63% higher than the Black Shores average.
Valentina: 52.63%... That's a number you don't see very often. Then again, it means that none of the previous tests truly captured the Combat Machines' full potential.
Valentina: I'll file an application to develop the tactical guidelines on the Combat Machines based on your test results.
 Glad to be of help.
Valentina: And, of course, your efforts were of considerable help to us. With these test data, the devices are one step closer to field deployment.
Valentina: We appreciate the assistance, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Valentina: We'll be staying here until we've finished analyzing the additional TD Outbreak data.
Valentina: If you wish to conduct further testing in the Tidal Defense Simulator under even more challenging environments, you are welcome to come here anytime.
