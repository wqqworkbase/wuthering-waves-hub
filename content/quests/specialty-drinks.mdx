Steps[]

- Contact Maqi to learn about today's scenario

- Give the Local Drinks to Maqi

- (Optional) Ask Ganxue about the "Local Drinks".
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionMaqi is trying to contact you, and it appears to be related to the Pioneer Association's ambitious project, "Second Coming of Solaris"...

(Talk to Maqi)
 Today's scenario...
Maqi: Let me explain our job today.
Maqi: In the opening chapter, our protagonist paid a visit to a city called Tiancheng.
Maqi: According to the book, "the cries of peddlers fill the market... a bewildering array of goods lines the shelves of every shop..."
Maqi: "We had a taste of a local drink served by the hotel. I was caught by total relief though my tongue stung a bit from that bitterness..."
Maqi: "Even on such foreign land, we feel safe and sound... We stayed there for three days to restore our strengths before we reluctantly said goodbye to this city."
Maqi: This Tiancheng here is so much alike to Jinzhou.
 I can think of nothing else.
 You can even see it as a doppelganger.
Maqi: Anyway, the dev team wants an item that embodies this prosperity of Tiancheng, which is also a significant town scene in the game.
Maqi: Well, that local drink will suffice.
Maqi: If you happen to come across some samples, feel free to drop by.

(Optional - Talk to Maqi)
 Give me some leads on "Local Drinks"
Maqi: Drinks that will prevail on foreign taste buds... Maybe I should do a street interview with those visitors?
Maqi: The Pioneer Merchandises have been Ganxue's staple. Many visitors bring one of those back as a gift.
Maqi: That would be a good idea that you ask Ganxue about their comments on Jinzhou.
(Optional - Talk to Ganxue)
 About the Local Drink in the novel...
Ganxue: Among all the drinks in Jinzhou, I like tea the most.
Ganxue: ...which is also the perfect match for what's in Second Coming of Solaris. Its taste is bitter but persistent on your tongue.
Ganxue: Tea is also the most popular seller here in my shop.
 Could I have one pack of tea?
Ganxue: By all means. That's also our greeting to visitors of Jinzhou.
Ganxue: Here. Remember to boil it with spring water. That makes the tea even better!
Ganxue: We look forward to meeting you again. Goodbye.
(Obtain Tea x1)

(Talk to Maqi)
 I'm turning in Local Drinks
(Submit Tea x1)
Maqi: Enjoying a cup of tea over chitchats in an inn... Seeing the steam of hot tea does make it more vivid.
Maqi: This would be what our dev team needs. I even can picture the lively Tiancheng right now.
Some time passed while Maqi was away to hand over the item...
Maqi: Thank you. That wraps up today's work!
Maqi: See you tomorrow, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
(Quest Complete)
