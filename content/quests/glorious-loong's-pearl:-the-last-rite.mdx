Glorious Loong's Pearl is a Exploration Quest that occurs in Mt. Firmament, Huanglong, [[{{{nation}}}]]. It is the last quest in the series: Mirrored Loong's Pearl.

Steps[]

- Talk to Shicheng

- Head to the destination and wait until night

- Head to where the meteor fell

- Activate the barrier with the Meteor

- Complete the challenges in the Offering Site

- Talk to Shicheng
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest awards the Footprints of Huanglong II trophy Gemberry.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionStranded in the wilderness, a determined young woman strives to perform a sacred rite for the Sentinel's well-being. Help her fulfill the Rite of Offering, and uphold this ancient tradition.

Shicheng: Wow, so you've completed every step of the ritual already?!
Shicheng: Is this really it, though? I still feel like something's missing.
Shicheng: Rover, could you go back to the cave in Xuanji Ridge? We may have missed some clues there.

(Talk to Shicheng)
Abby: So, we're all done with the ritual. Time to get you back to Hongzhen.
Shicheng: Hold on! It's not done yet!
 Any new discoveries?
Shicheng: Yeah, I just found a note left by Uncle Fu. Turns out it was once a simple coming-of-age tradition for the people of Mt. Firmament.
Shicheng: Only the Flamebearers who passed the trials of wisdom and strength were recognized as adults, proving they could protect the Sentinel and Hongzhen.
Shicheng: But later, it transformed into a ceremonial activity of blessings and worship.
 No wonder it's so complicated.
Shicheng: The previous trials only tested the Flamebearer's wisdom. The Flamebearer would still need to prove their strength.
Abby: Strength? That's our specialty!
Shicheng: Oh, it's time for the final ritual. Rover, please go to this place with the glazed lamp I gave you.

(Head to the destination and wait until night)
Abby: That giant rock landed by the seaside. Quick! Let's go check it out!

(Head to where the meteor fell)
Abby: Looks like the stone fits perfectly into the hole in this device. Use your Levitator to grab it.

(Talk to Shicheng)
Shicheng: Rover, what happened? I saw a huge meteor falling down!
You explain the details of the final ritual to Shicheng.
Shicheng: Thank you, Rover...
Shicheng: I never realized Uncle Fu has been performing this ritual alone all these years, praying for the Sentinel.
Shicheng: Hopefully the Sentinel will hear our prayers and get better soon.
Abby: Let's hope so...
Shicheng: I've made up my mind. I will help Uncle Fu preserve this tradition and spread awareness about it.
 Sounds like a good idea.
Shicheng: Alright then, I guess it's time to say goodbye. I'll stay here a bit longer to organize the records on this ritual.
(Quest Complete)
