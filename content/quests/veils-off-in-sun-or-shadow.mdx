Summary[]

You run into Cristoforo, the mysterious playwright, again. He's on a journey for inspiration to finish his script about the Blessed Maiden, "Fleurdelys." As for Gilberto, he tells you that a Fisalia member has been frequenting Whisperwind Haven, and Echoes have been mysteriously vanishing. Suspecting a connection, you follow the Echoes to investigate further.

Upon reaching the area where Echoes have gone missing, you're attacked by out-of-control Echoes. After quelling them, you notice that they seem to be manipulated, just like the Vitreum Dancers in Ragunna. Bidding Cristoforo farewell, you press on and encounter another familiar face - Acolyte Phoebe.

Phoebe reveals that she's searching for a missing Echo. You share your findings and she realizes Gilberto's actions are blasphemy against the Sentinel, which calls for a punishment. With shared goals, Phoebe joins your team. Suddenly, the missing Echo appears, fleeing as if bein pursued. You decided to catch up to uncover what's behind its panic.

You finally pin down Gilberto, who is reciitng eerie prayers. On seeing you, he transforms the nearby Echoes into Tacet Discords with a Lycoris flower. A fierce battle ensues. Despite his defeat, Gilberto continues to preach a dark prophecy: Lorelei will command the sea of clouds to engulf Ragunna, sparing only the faithful. Meanwhile, an anomaly at the Nimbus Sanctum suggests Lorelei might also be influenced by a Lycoris. Zani and Phoebe stay behind to watch over Gilberto until the Montelli members arrive. You head to the sea of clouds alone.
