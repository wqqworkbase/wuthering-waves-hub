Summary[]

You and Abby arrive at Solis Tavern. While waiting for contact from the Ephor's Palace, you run into a blind wandering bard, Nestor. He shares with you Augusta's stories before she rose to power.

A girl whose homeland was devastated by the Dark Tide, Augusta, follows Septimont's Gladiators to the city. Her heart yearns to find the power to protect her home, and the crowd's buzz of the Great Agon catches her attention. The girl thinks this path may help her find the strength she is seeking, so she is determined to participate in the Agon. According to the rules, she needs a mentor. With no identity or status, she faces repeated setbacks until she finally finds Cato as her "lifeline." The girl continues to hone her skills, passing the entry trials Cato set for her, and finally makes her first step into the Agon.

The Great Agon draws close as time passes. Besides a mentor, Augusta also needs a sponsor to participate in the Gladiatorial events. Sponsors are mostly nobles and merchants, and for someone with no identity or status, the only place to approach them is the underground arena, where nameless Gladiators are sold like commodities. Realizing she's become the bottom pick, Augusta takes the initiative by offering herself to the nobles and merchants in the audience, only to be met with waves of mockery. An old-school nobleman studies Augusta and offers her a deal: if Augusta manages to pass the three trials he sets, he will become her sponsor.

Glossary[]

- Sanguis Plateaus - The Sanguis Plateaus were once the Septimont frontline against the Dark Tide. Due to the region's unique landscape, Remnant energy gathers here in unusual density, along with a Blightcloud perpetually looming overhead, formed since the first Dark Tide. During High Tide seasons, Gladiators from across Septimont arrive here to hunt Dark Tide creations in the name of glory.
