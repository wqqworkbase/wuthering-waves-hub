Steps[]

- Talk to Cosimo

- Wait for the Night Reveries (18:00 to 06:00 the next day)

- Participate in the Night Reveries (18:00 to 06:00 the next day)

- Defeat the Mad Knight

- Go to the highest vantage point of the town

- Find Carlow beneath the large tree outside the town

- Touch the Primordia Bloom

- Return to the "Realm of Chests"

- Are those Supply Chests?

- Open Supply Chests

- What Happened?

- Defeat Chest Mimics

- Reach the second location visited by the knight

- Jump to the destination using Cuddle Wuddle's ability

- Reach the third location visited by the knight

- Head to the source of the strange scent (Suggestion: Equip Questless Knight Echo)

- Defeat the TDs

- Check on Carlow (Daytime)
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the "Defeat the Mad Knight" step awards the Exploration: Rinascita trophy Guardian of Egla.

- It is not necessary to start the quest for the Mad Knight to appear.

- Completing the quest awards the Footprints in Rinascita I trophy The Last Knight.
