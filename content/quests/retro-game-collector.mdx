Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionA small game cartridge could be a vessel of a captivating world of sounds, landscapes, lores, and inspirations... KU-Cass needs your assistance to recover the old game cartridges to listen again to the sounds of a lost past.

(Talk to KU-Cass)
 KU-Cass's request
KU-Cass: Ku-ku... Ku-ku... Ku-ku. Seeking transactions!
KU-Cass: For a second there, I thought you were actively ignoring these calls and would just walk away.
 Gotta look out for "shady robot dealers," they always say.
KU-Cass: Oh, the word "shady" is a rather harsh way of putting it. I thought we were friends! Did KU-Money leave you with a bad impression?
KU-Cass: As business people, we're just helping the Black Shores make a profit with reasonable methods. As for the few little tricks before sealing the deal...
KU-Cass: Well, I only use them when I'm at a disadvantage, to reclaim what's rightfully ours. Speaking of deals, hehe, I happen to have one just for you.
KU-Cass: Are you interested, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)?
 Let's talk details first.
 Need to check my savings.
KU-Cass: No need. This deal doesn't require Shell Credits. I'd like you to help me find something. Some kind of cartridge.
KU-Cass: Ever heard of The Tenth Art? I stumbled across info about it in a ruins&#32;&#91;sic&#93; while searching for data on past civilizations during an expedition.
KU-Cass: It seems to have been a very popular term back in the day. After returning to the Black Shores, I recovered what was left of the data and projected it onto a display.
KU-Cass: And then... the pixels began to bounce around, and as I recovered more data, the images on the screen kept changing.
KU-Cass: From simple flickering pixels, to being a "cowboy" roaming the western plains, and then to someone like a Lollo Logistics courier rushing to various locations after a disaster.
KU-Cass: I never imagined a tiny cartridge could pack in so many sounds, scenes, fabled stories, and projected hopes. If we're to search for civilizations of the past...
KU-Cass: I believe these are also part of their many traces, reflecting the scenes of their era, and their thoughts...
KU-Cass: Even in a time so distant, we can still explore the end of that era through these cartridges.
KU-Cass: However, after the last earthquake hit the Black Shores, the cartridges I collected are now scattered everywhere.
KU-Cass: So if you manage to find any cartridges, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), be sure to return them to me. I'll give you a fair reward for them.
(Quest Complete)
