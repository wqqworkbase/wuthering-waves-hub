Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionSee Yihu again

(Talk to Yihu)
Yihu: Hi there, what a coincidence! Nice to see you again.
 Indeed.
 What a coincidence.
Yihu: Are you busy today?
 Not really.
Yihu: In that case, how about I tell you something fresh?
 Go on.
Yihu: Have you heard about rats stealing oil?
 And that's what you call "fresh"?
Yihu: Well, for someone as knowledgeable as you, it may not be something fresh. But a mouse stealing oil to share with a cat, that's not something you see every day, right?
 No.
 Tell me more.
Yihu: Indeed.
Yihu: After we were closed yesterday, I cleaned up until late. When I was locking the door, I caught a glimpse of a gray rat dashing into the shades in the corner.
Yihu: I immediately followed it and saw the mouse hopping onto the shoulders of a little cat, sneaking into the shop through the window.
Yihu: Then, the rat dipped the tip of its tail in the oil and fed it to the cat. No wonder the cat was willing to help.
Yihu: After the mouse left, I went to the cat and asked, "Why didn't you just catch the mouse and eat it?"
Yihu: The cat glanced at me and said, "Fool. If there is no mouse stealing oil, why would they keep me? It's better to have a bit of oil every day for the long run."
Yihu: Isn't it amusing? A cat working for a mouse.
 Really?
Yihu: If you don't believe it, why don't you take a look at the windowsill? There might be traces of oil left.

(Inspect the window sill)
You carefully checked the windowsill and indeed found some traces that seemed to be lamp oil.

(Talk to Yihu)
Yihu: Did you see the traces?
 Yes, there are indeed traces that look like lamp oil on the windowsill.
Yihu: Wow, really? Well, I just made it up on the spot. Anyway, just take it as a joke.
 A not-so-funny joke.
Yihu: ... Yeah, you're right. I told it to my customers before, and they barely laughed. I still have a long way to go.
Yihu: Next time, I'll share with you a more amusing one.
(Quest Complete)
