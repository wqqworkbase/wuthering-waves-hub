Steps[]

- Check the situation

- Find the lost Tubpup in the Garden of the Lost

- Return to Septimont via the morph painting in Plushie Village
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest awards the hidden Exploration: Rinascita trophy Plushie Squad, Glorious Comeback!.
Dialogue[]

(Talk to the plushies)
Rover: Now that I think about it, this Plushie Village seems a bit different from the one in Ragunna.
You look around and notice that the Echoes all seem to look rather downhearted.
Sensing your concern, Teensy Weensy hurries to point at the tear marks across the Echoes' bodies.
Rover: Wait... Did something happen on your way here? You all look a bit... dirty.
Teensy Weensy: ...! ...!
Rover: I can't understand what you're saying, but I can tell you're all thinking about getting squeaky clean again, right?
Teensy Weensy:&#160;!!
Teensy Weensy nods frantically at you.
Rover: Hmm... Is there anything we can do to get rid of these stains?
Rover: How about a Tubpup from Ragunna? I remember there were lots of free-roaming Echoes around the Plushie Village in the Garden of the Lost.
Rover: I'll help them search around a bit whenever I find time and go back to Ragunna.

 Quest DescriptionAfter a long journey, the plushie squad is a mess… Let's help them freshen up and get sparkling clean again.
(Go to the Garden of the Lost and interact with the Tubpup)
The Tubpup seems to be looking for something...
Fulmine: Ah, finally! (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! I hear you've made it back to the Plushie Village in Ragunna?
You tell Fulmine what happened at the Plushie Village in Septimont.
Fulmine: I see. Perfect, I was just about to ask you a similar favor.
Fulmine: Ever since Teensy Weensy and the gang went off to Septimont, those new Echoes were having trouble fitting in.
Fulmine: If you don't mind, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). I'd like you to help guide them through the morph painting's Sonoro Sphere and reach Septimont.
Fulmine: After all, they could use the extra hands to help set up their new village, right?
Fulmine: I'll leave this task to you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). Let me know if you need anything!
After Fulmine hangs up, Tubpup looks at you, eyes filled with anticipation.
Rover: That being said... I've never actually tried going through the morph painting in the village...
Rover: Well... Here goes nothing.

(Use the painting to fast travel)
The Echoes seem extremely thrilled to welcome their new friend.
With Tubpup's help, the Echoes are cleaned and shiny again in no time. As music sounds all over Plushie Village.
Rover: Nice. That's a lot better!
Teensy Weensy: ...! ...!
What's this?
You received a present from the Echoes.
(Quest Complete)
