New Arrival in The Big City is a Gameplay subpart in Rust, Sword and the Sun, in Chapter II of the Main Quest.
During this quest, you must manage 4 Attributes (Health, Martialism, Willpower and Reputation). If any of those attributes reach 0, a Game Over happens.
