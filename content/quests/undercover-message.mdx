Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionFind Yihu and convey the message

(Find Yihu and convey the message)
Yihu: Here you are.
 It's my turn to share something fresh with you.
Yihu: Oh? What's it?
 There are wolves in the mountains.
Yihu: And that's what you call "fresh"?
 For those who haven't seen wolves, it certainly is.
Yihu: Haha, you're indeed interesting.
 Glad you found it interesting.
Yihu: By the way, do you remember all the stories I told you before?
 Shall we practice them now?
Yihu: Oh no, not necessary. They aren't worth the effort.
Yihu: When I come up with a new joke, I'll let you know.

(Find Langsheng and convey Yihu's message)
 That's all I know.
Langsheng: Hmm...
 Yihu assured me the information was accurate.
Langsheng: I see.
Langsheng: Thank you. The information you provided is very helpful for solving the case.
 Are all these coded messages?
Langsheng: Kind of. Yihu used plain language, easy to decipher.
Langsheng: He's not exactly a professional undercover, so I have to be really careful.
Langsheng: Thank you for your help, Rover. I'll pick up the pace of the investigation and keep you updated if there are any new leads.
(Quest Complete)
