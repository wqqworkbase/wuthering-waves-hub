Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionShinong made a mysterious discovery about Pavo Plums recorded in the Hongzhen Chronicle. For him, there is only one way to verify its validity.
(Talk to Shinong)
Shinong: Hey, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! Fancy seeing you here!
 You didn't eat any odd herbs today, did you?
Shinong: Of course not&#8212;well, not yet.
Shinong: I read an anecdote in Hongzhen Chronicles about a young man from a few hundred years ago who discovered that Pavo Plums has temporary memory-enhance effects.
Shinong: He memorized all the texts of Hongzhen with the help of Pavo Plum's incredible effects, earning him the nickname "the Walking Bookshelf."
Shinong: So... you know. I want to try it.
 It's probably just a myth.
 You sure something like that exists?
Shinong: I agree that the story might be fictional. But there might be some truth behind it.
Shinong: I've always been gathering Pavo Plums from around this area. But according to Hongzhen Chronicles, only those "found near the hot spring and that can slightly heat up" has medical effects.
Shinong: But I've searched everywhere near the hot spring and haven't found any Pavo Plums like the ones described.
 I could give you a hand with it.
Shinong: R-really? That would be great!

(Hand the heated Pavo Plum to Shinong)
Shinong: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), did you find any heated Pavo Plums?
Shinong: Exactly as the chronicles described... I didn't expect it to exist. Interesting... But it could be a mutation.
 "Didn't expect it to exist?"
 A mutation?
Shinong: H-haha. Don't be upset. Presumption is the first step toward truth.
Shinong: I suspect that the surrounding heat might have induced some changes in these Pavo Plums... potentially altering altering their medicinal effects.
Shinong: Now, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), could you please say something after I eat the Pavo Plum?
 Sure, what would you like me to say?
Shinong: Anything will do, preferably something long and complicated. I want to test if it truly helps improve memory.
As soon as Shinong finishes the Pavo Plum, his face twists into a peculiar expression.
Shinong: It's... it's... it's...
 What is it? Is it poisonous?
Shinong: It's so SPICY! I've tasted countless herbs, but I've never tasted anything this SPICY before!
Shinong: Wait, I think it's working... Say something, Rover. Anything!
 How much wood would a woodchuck chuck if&#8212;
You effortly recite a passage from a tongue twister.
 Oh, my lord, I beg your forgiveness&#8212;
You passionately perform a monologue from a movie film.
Shinong: Hm... Hm...
 Did you catch all of that?
Shinong: ...None, actually.
 Whole thing sounded like a bust from the get-go.
Shinong: After all, it's just a fictional anecdote that can't be taken too seriously. But you never know unless you give it a try.
 That doesn't mean all myths are false.
Shinong: Perhaps. If I've learned anything from revising these pharmacopoeias, it's never to underestimate the wisdom of those who came before us.
Shinong: Anyway, these Pavo Plums don't show any special effects, even though they can absorbe heat... and are extremely spicy. Well, that's a small discovery, at least.
Shinong: Thank you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). There are still quite a few herbs from the pharmacopoeia that I haven't tried yet. I'll go and search for them now.
(Quest Complete)
