Steps[]

- Learn more from the strange Acolyte

- Use the Wingray to reach the Broken Bridge

- Approach the phantoms

- Defeat the enemies and listen to the phantoms

- Pursue the Musicfly

- Get back to Lenore

- Play the record at the Aria Mummer

- Use Wingray to reach Atrium of Reflections

- Defeat the Vitreum Dancers and listen to the phantoms

- Retrieve the record left by the phantoms

- Talk to Lenore

- Play the record at the Aria Mummer

- Investigate the Gondolas

- Take the Gondola

- Follow the music to locate the third record

- Pursue the Musicfly

- Defeat the Vitreum Dancers and listen to the phantoms

- Pick up the third record

- Play the third record

- Listen to the music

- Head to the location of anomaly
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the step "Pursue the Musicfly" will award the player  The Nimbus Chorale: Soil of Abundance.

- Completing the step "Retrieve the record left by the phantoms" will award the player  The Nimbus Chorale: Crystal Sky.

- Completing the step "Pick up the third record" will award the player  The Nimbus Chorale: Order of Nature.
