Summary[]

You run into Lingyang chatting with some children. He introduces you to the Liondance Troupe and tells you about some of Jinzhou's traditions. He asks you to accompany him in preparing for the upcoming Liondance&#8212;and also offers to grab a bite en route.
After talking with the client, Lingyang agrees to celebrate an elderly person's birthday during tomorrow's performance.
After talking with the second clients, Lingyang agrees to include the "Liondance Officiant" ceremony in tomorrow's performance to celebrate their wedding.
Lingyang treats you to some local Jinzhou specialties at a food stall, suggesting the stall owner&#8212;one of his clients&#8212;offer snacks during the performance as a way to advertise. However, the owner explains they are short on ingredients due to the disruption caused by a creature known as the "Jingle Beast" and a gang called "The Savages." Hearing this, Lingyang appears deep in thought.
Lingyang heads to a teahouse to take care of an unresolved request. The stall owner expresses concern about Lingyang's tendency to prioritize others over himself and asks if you can check on him. You agree to help.
At the teahouse, you find Lingyang discussing a mysterious request with Bei Shi. They suspect the request came from Bei Shi's brother, Bei Ji, who has been missing for a week and may be involved with "The Savages." To find out more, you and Bei Shi start investigating Bei Ji's packages.
A courier tells you that locals have been paying "The Savages" to fend off the "Jingle Beast." With this clue, you head to the area where the beast is often spotted.
Near an Exile's camp, you overhear that "The Savages" are exploiting the "Jingle Beast" legend. You also learn that a new member has joined their ranks hoping to find the Beast.
Everything points to "The Savages" spreading rumors about the "Jingle Beast" and selling spoiled ingredients. You discover the new recruit is Bei Ji. To keep you safe, Lingyang volunteers to investigate "the Savages" himself while you and Bei Shi focus on finding Bei Ji.
Bei Shi shares that Bei Ji has been feeling guilty about her. She also opens up about her fear of the "Jingle Beast," which stems from a past encounter out in the wild.
Lingyang tells you that "The Savages" have taken Bei Ji to a cave. Sensing danger, he suggests you join him in the rescue while Bei Shi alerts the Patrollers.
When you reach the cave, Bei Ji faces "The Savages" with resolve, determined to bring them to justice before you and Lingyang arrive to help.
Defeating "The Savages" causes a commotion that awakens three Spearbacks in the cave. To protect you, Lingyang steps in to take on them.
Lingyang asks you to keep his beastly side a secret. After hearing about your efforts, Bei Ji opens up about his conflict with Bei Shi, sharing his true feelings.
With the Patrollers' help, you catch "The Savages" and the Bei siblings are finally reunited.
You learn that Bei Ji had originally planned to kill the "Jingle Beast" to prove his courage and earn his sister's respect. Though he feels like he has failed, Lingyang reminds him that true bravery lies in overcoming one's weaknesses for those we care about. Bei Ji gains Bei Shi's understanding, but her fear of the "Jingle Beast" remains. To help her, Lingyang invites the siblings to watch the Liondance, believing it may help dispel Bei Shi's fear.
Lingyang asks you to check backstage for props that might help Bei Shi form a new opinion about the "Jingle Beast."
Before the performance, Lingyang shares the origin and meaning of the Liondance, suggesting that it might help Bei Shi overcome her fear of the "Jingle Beast."
You meet with various clients before the performance. The Bei siblings are curious about the secret of the "Jingle Beast," while Grandpa Gan reminisces about the Liondance performances he enjoyed during his time in the army. Linling and Shengmao decide to give the "Settlement" ceremony another go, and everyone awaits the grand Liondance with excitement.
Thanks to the Liondance performance, Bei Shi can recall how the sound of a bell saved her during a previous attack in the wild. Her fears begin to shift as her courage is restored.
In the end, Lingyang reveals the truth about the "Jingle Beast"&#8212;it is the last "Suan'ni." Moved by humanity's bravery against the Lament, this creature longed to become one of them. It had been trying to understand what it means to be human and ultimately discovered that the answer lies in the "heart." Since that day, it has sought to express the beauty it sees in humans in its own unique way.
