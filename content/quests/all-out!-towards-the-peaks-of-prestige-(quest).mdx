Steps[]

- Go to the location mentioned in the strange message

- Inquire about the Peaks of Prestige

- Reach the location of the first Lord of Prestige

- Inquire about the identity of the Lord of Prestige

- Reach the location of the second Lord of Prestige

- Inquire about the Lord of Prestige

- Challenge the Excited Man

- Challenge the Shy Woman

- Challenge Liliania

- Reach the location of the third Lord of Prestige

- Talk to Marcia

- Make the patient take the medication through a duel

- Motivate the patient through a duel

- Challenge Marcia

- Reach the location of the fourth Lord of Prestige

- Challenge the Grandino Gladiator

- Challenge the Grandino Agent

- Challenge the Grandino Head

- Challenge Raul

- Reach the location of the fifthLord of Prestige

- Inquire about the Lord of Prestige

- Return to chat with Furius

- Defeat the three new challengers (0/3)

- Go to Furius and sign up for the final

- Go to the room provided by Furius to rest

- (Optional) Talk to Lupa

- Go to bed

- (Optional) Read the message in the room

- Leave the room and go to the arena

- Go to the venue for the final

- (Optional) Take the elevator

- Talk to Furius

- Get on the stage of the Colosseum

- Talk to Furius

- Talk to Brant

- Talk to Ciaccona

- Talk to Lupa

- Take on the ultimate challenge
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the step "Return to chat with Furius" awards Path of Growth title Echo Professor.

- Winning against the Sixth Lord of Prestige awards Path of Growth title Echo Tamer and Rover's Journey II trophy King of Duels.
