Summary[]

Chixia contacts you to say that several Jinzhou citizens have recently gone missing. She asks you to stay alert for anything suspicious.
An anonymous email leads you to Lirong and his daughter, Yuanyuan. Lirong is a member of the Séance Society, a group that claims to reunite members with their loved ones. He invites you to join, but becomes agitated when asked about his daughter's fate, refusing to accept that she is no longer truly alive.
A mysterious woman arrives and convinces Lirong to leave. After he departs, a fight breaks out between you and the woman over Yuanyuan. The confrontation only ends when a Patroller shows up. To uncover the truth about the Séance Society and the missing persons, you decide to cooperate with the woman, starting by cleaning up the scene.
You are both questioned by the Patroller, but the two of you conceal what happened. During the conversation, you discover that the woman is actually a Patroller named Yinlin. After the Patroller leaves, Yinlin reveals that "Yuanyuan" is, in fact, a puppet.
Yinlin explains that the Séance Society is behind the recent disappearances in Jinzhou. They provide lifelike puppets in place of lost loved ones, and many desperate people join the Society for this reason. However, frequent interaction with these puppets greatly increases the risk of Overclocking. Yinlin is on the trail of those responsible and invites you to meet her outside the city if you want to learn more about the case.
Yinlin tells you that the Séance Society is a dangerous organization that lures people in by promising to bring back their loved ones. They appear to be linked to the Fractsidus, and her mission is to gather evidence against them and identify the mastermind behind it all. Currently, she is investigating the prototype puppets in the hope they hold clues about their creator. Eager to recover your lost memories, you decide to assist Yinlin by following Lirong to the puppets' storage location.
Furious with the Fractsidus for their disdain toward the puppets, Lirong breaks their deal and attempts to escape with the puppets. You quickly follow him.
Determined to protect the puppets from becoming Fractsidus experimental subjects, Lirong attempts to hide. He reveals that the leader of the Séance Society is known as the Dollmaker. As the Fractsidus closes in, putting them all in danger, you and Yinlin step in.
Yinlin tries to mediate between the Séance Society and the Fractsidus, but the negotiations fail.
She admits she tracked you using a positioning device placed on you earlier, explaining that her undercover role makes it hard for her to trust anyone. With Lirong and Yuanyuan no longer providing new leads, your only hope lies in the prototype puppets, so you decide to gather as many of them as possible.
After collecting all the prototype puppets, Yinlin plans to take them to a safe place for further investigation. She learns from a fellow Patroller that there is a safehouse nearby for undercover agents, and you both head there.
Upon your arrival, Yinlin betrays you, and you are taken to a warehouse while passed out.
You realize the place is connected to the Séance Society, so you use the communication panel you found earlier to send the coordinates and details of the situation to Chixia.
You soon discover that it was Yinlin who trapped you here. She reveals that this is the Séance Society's base and invites you once more to join the organization, offering to introduce you to its members.
You learn that Lianyue, who also reunited with her deceased child with the Society's help, refuses to let anyone disrupt their current life.
You discover that Jinlu met his fallen comrade He Chang here and chose to stay because of their strong bond.
Wu Sheng tells you that even the best doctors can't save everyone, just as he was not able to save his wife. The Dollmaker's puppets gave them a second chance, something no doctor could offer. While Wu Sheng understands the dangers of the puppets, he also knows how much they mean to the people here.
Yinlin reveals that she empathizes with these people who just want to see their loved ones again. She reveals that she was once a member of the Séance Society herself. She grew up alongside the Dollmaker and knows that his experiments are aimed at creating two special puppets. However, she keeps the details of this final experiment from you.
When the Patrollers besiege the base, Yinlin takes you hostage but secretly passes you a message: "Trust me… Come this way."
Yinlin brings you to meet the Dollmaker, maintaining a façade of hostility due to a bug the Dollmaker planted on you. Through a hidden panel, she explains that this factory produces puppets designed to trigger Overclocking for the Fractsidus in exchange for resources. She needs your help to gather evidence against the Dollmaker's involvement in all this.
Yinlin reminds you to use your Terminal to record the prototype puppet data.
You speak to the Dollmaker and discover that the two final puppets he intends to make are Yinlin's parents&#8212;his former mentors. He captured you to get experimental data, hoping to create perfect puppets with the Fractsidus's backing. Now, with the Public Security Bureau involved, he's rushing to relocate both the puppets and his research data.
The Dollmaker confronts Yinlin after learning of her betrayal. She asserts that, like her parents, she has always been a Patroller and has never forgotten her true identity and duty. She declares she cannot accept the Dollmaker harming others to recapture the warmth of the past.
In the face of the Dollmaker's twisted obsession, Yinlin argues that puppets cannot replace real family and that it is wrong to take advantage of others' happiness to cope with one's own misfortune. The Dollmaker retorts that he has erased her Patroller records, making it impossible for her to prove her identity. But Yinlin asserts that even without this identity, she will continue to fight evil. That is the spirit her parents instilled in her, and the one that motivates her to pursue her goals in her own way.
You learn from Chixia that the injured are recovering, but the Dollmaker still refuses to cooperate. She thanks you for your help, and you hint that the real thanks should go to a mysterious partner. However, you make sure to keep the partner's name a secret.
When you meet Yinlin again, she tells you that the Dollmaker attempted to extract your memories but failed. The cause of your memory loss is far more complex than you thought. She thanks you for your help and coyly suggests that your next meeting may be under a completely different guise.
