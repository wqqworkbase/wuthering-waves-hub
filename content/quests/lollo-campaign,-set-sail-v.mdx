Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionBakethoven's dream is to belong to the Echo Depository of Averardo Vault. Can you help him out?

(Talk to Maji)
Maji: Oh, what should I do... Oh, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! Thank goodness you're here!
 What's going on this time?
 Why is there an Echo here?
Maji: I'm not sure. It was already here when I arrived, with this note...

Translated by a Kind StrangerI am sincerely seeking a kind-hearted person to take me to the Echo Depository of the Averardo Vault!

If I had the choice, I would prefer to live a short but meaningful life over a long but ordinary one.

&#8212;&#8212;Bakethoven the Echo

(Transcribed by a helpful passerby. Please help, it really wants to grow as an Echo.)

Maji: Seems like this is the situation now... Any ideas?
 ...Maybe we should help it.
Maji: Maybe... but are there any rules for this Averardo Vault when it comes to accepting Echoes? They can't just take in any Echoes off the street, right?
 ...That might be a little tricky.
Maji: True... Are there any rules for this Averardo Vault when it comes to accepting Echoes? They can't just take in any Echoes off the street, right?
Maji: Perhaps we should ask around. Do you know anyone who works at the Vault, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)?
 Working at the Vault... Aha.
 I could ask my friend, Zani.
Maji: Excellent! Now we've got a chance to help Bakethoven! We'll wait here while you check with your friend!

(Talk to Zani)
Zani: Understood. I'll have two plans ready for you to review today.… Yes, everything on the list will be completed by the deadline.
Zani: And... Hm, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)? When did you get here?
 I've been here for a while.
 Right when you said you'd send the two plans…
Zani: Sorry... I have a deadline tomorrow and things are tight. Do you need something?
 I just wanted to ask you something.
Zani: Go ahead. I'm waiting for things to move forward anyway. Ask me whatever you need.
 Am I bothering you?
Zani: No, it's fine. I'm waiting for things to move forward anyway. Ask me whatever you need.
You tell Zani about what happened...
Zani: So, you met an oven Echo that wants to visit the Vault's Echo Depository, right? Got it.
Zani: Every Echo or piece of artwork needs to be appraised to see if it's valuable enough for the Vault. Is there anything particularly notable about this Echo?
 It's especially large.
Zani: Especially large… Well, I don't think the Vault has a shortage of large Echoes.
 It smells really good.
Zani: Smells really good, huh? An... interesting observation. Does it smell like the food it cooks?
Zani: Or maybe it's particularly talented? You see, talent is something the Vault looks for too.
 Okay, I'll be sure to find out.
Zani: Sure. I'll be available tomorrow after I wrap up my current job. You can find me here if you need anything.

(Talk to Maji)
Maji: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! How's it going? Any news on Bakethoven's chance to enter the Vault?
 It needs to have something special, like a unique talent…
Your words seem to be dimming Bakethoven's spirit, causing it to lower its head. All it can do is bake, which probably doesn't count as a special talent.
Maji: I see… So, what should we do now?
Rover: Let's head to the Vault tomorrow. You said you'd prefer to live a short but meaningful life, right? From where I stand, making food for others seems like a pretty meaningful thing to me.
(Bakethoven lets out some grateful noises.)
Maji: Alright then! Let's meet at the Vault tomorrow! Thanks as always, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)!
(Quest Complete)
