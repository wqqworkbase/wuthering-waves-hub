Summary[]

Even after drinking the Fisalia's family "antidote," your heart remains unsettled.

Galbrena's words help reveal that it is Abby who has been corrupted by the Dark Tide. Because you share life and energy, the corruption passed to you as well. Now, the only way to reverse it is to defeat Leviathan and destroy Its Paradise.

From the Fisalia's ancient records, you learn that the Dark Tide is the "subconscious ocean of the Rinascitans' shared trauma." You also uncover Leviathan's final plan to bring Its Paradise to the world. Galbrena and Cartethyia have a plan to stop this, but because you bear the Mark of the Dark Tide, Leviathan can read your thoughts. For this reason, they cannot reveal everything to you. Yet throughout this journey, you have felt the trust others have placed in you. You are not just the "Harbinger of Despair," you are also a vessel of Rinascita's hopes. In this war of spirit, only an unshakable resolve can stand against Leviathan.

Galbrena spreads her wings and takes flight. With the Dark Tide in Ragunna contained, it's time for the next step. You prepare to head to Septimont. Everything will come to a head there.

After parting ways with Cantarella, you set out alone for Septimont City.

By the time you arrive, the city is already preparing for war. At the gates, you encounter Priestess Iuno helping to evacuate civilians. You recount what happened in Ragunna, and she tells you that the Priestesses can no longer deliver new prophecies. Because of this, many people now believe the apocalypse is nigh.

Griffrex "Aquila" brings unprecedented news from the Plateaus: the Dark Tide has erupted near the Three Heroes' Crest. Named after three Heroes of Heroes, the peaks have been one of the few sacred places in Septimont untouched by the Dark Tide. You and Iuno decide to head to the Plateaus to meet Ephor Augusta, while the Senate stays behind to oversee the evacuation. In this time of crisis, everyone is doing everything they can to hold the line.

Now, mount the Griffrex with Iuno and fly toward the battlefield.

From high above, you and Iuno witness the Dark Tide's onslaught. Yet the warriors of Septimont stand unbroken. Their unyielding spirit stirs hope in your heart.

The Griffrex dives, bringing you swiftly to the battlefield. There, you reunite with Augusta and your old partner, Lupa. Iuno, once "forgotten," seems to have rekindled her friendship with Augusta as well.

Augusta explains the abnormal Dark Tide surge at the Three Heroes' Crest. To hold it back, they've set up Anti-Corrosion Wards using Anchor Obelisks. You share your own intelligence, but the Tidespawn strike again before more can be said. As Septimont's Hero of Heroes, you leap into the fray without hesitation. Inspired by your resolve, the warriors rally behind you. It's time to show the enemies once more the fury of Septimont.

The Dark Tide's assault is relentless, targeting the weakest links of the Obelisk array. You and your companions cannot cover every front, and the situation grows dire...

Then, several familiar teleportation circles light up. The voices of your companions from Ragunna ring across the battlefield, and Galbrena arrives as well. They've come to reinforce the other fronts, helping the warriors reclaim the Anchor Obelisks.

Heroes from distant lands reunite here to face the crisis together. You take heart, and steel yourself for the battle still ahead...

The battle rages on. Just as you begin to worry it will drag on into a war of attrition, Galbrena makes for the cliff's edge, preparing to throw herself into a strange opening within the Dark Tide. It is the gateway to the Journeying Paradise, and Leviathan's sanctuary.

Galbrena explains that Leviathan's true goal is to use humanity's fear of the Dark Tide to accelerate the creation of the Journeying Paradise. Only by defeating Leviathan and destroying Its Paradise can the crisis truly end. But with Leviathan's eye on you, she only offers a cryptic hint on how to enter, trusting you to understand.

Galbrena then plunges into the Dark Tide, drawing Leviathan's attention and buying you time to plan. Seeing the Tidespawn immediately pull back to defend the opening, Augusta orders all Anchor Obelisks to be activated at once to seal the Dark Tide's spread.

At a makeshift camp near the Three Heroes' Crest, your companions are already waiting. You worry about the situation in Averardo Vault and ask why your Ragunna allies have come… Just then, a familiar voice sounds on your Terminal…

Aalto briefs you on the situation at Averardo Vault. All available Bloom Bearers from the Black Shores have been sent to Rinascita. 

He explains the nature of the Threnodian's revival and a possible countermeasure. The Threnodian you are facing is one the Fractsidus revived and recreated using the Gem. If Abby, with the ability to absorb Threnodian frequencies, can carry the Gem within itself, the Black Shores might be able to contain It using Tethys's infinite Sonoro expansion.

But Leviathan is rooted deeply in the minds and souls of Rinascita's people. Only they can truly face it. Therefore, at Ragunna's request, some powerful Resonators have been sent to reinforce you at the Three Heroes' Crest. As for Averardo Vault and the civilians of Septimont, the Black Shores will stand as the last line of defense, guarding them until your safe return.

Reassured by the Black Shores' support, you turn your focus to Leviathan. Galbrena's parting words hinted that if Augusta's Anchor Stone in the Dark Tide can be reactivated, the resonance between Anchor Stones may reveal a path to the Journeying Paradise. But to purify the corrupted Anchor, the Sentinel's power is still needed.

A stranger from Huanglong brings the "Lantern of Napoli the Second," which contains a fragment of that very power, but only enough for one person. Each of your companions offers to take the risk, but you argue firmly and claim the task.

Still, they refuse to let you bear the burden alone. Together, you devise a final plan: you will enter first to activate the Anchor Stone and create a path. After that, the others will drink the Fisalia "antidote" and follow you into the Dark Tide. There, in the Journeying Paradise, you will reunite and face Leviathan together.

Until then, they will hold the line at the Three Heroes' Crest. The Dark Tide has yet to settle, and the moment to enter has not yet come. For now, you can rest and gather strength for the battle ahead.

You speak with the stranger from Huanglong named Qiuyuan. Dispatched by the Mingting Capital, he is hunting a fugitive while gathering intel on the "Threnodian's Resonator." He had originally sought Ragunna's Blessed Maiden, but instead found Primus Fenrico, who entrusted Qiuyuan with the Lantern of Napoli the Second before giving his life to drive back the Dark Tide beneath Ragunna's sea.

Qiuyuan speaks of your identity as a "Harbinger of the Dark Tide" and warns that all Harbingers share the same fate—death. But you have long since come to terms with that.

Looking at your companions busy in their preparations, you ask Qiuyuan for one favor: to help Rinascita weather this calamity. He replies that the Dark Tide rejects all but Rinascitans, and you are a rare exception. But he promises to defend the surface in your absence.

Before parting, he tells you the Magistrate of Jinzhou sends her regards. Those whom you once aided are still waiting for your return.

The Dark Tide has stabilized. Now is the time to strike.

Before you depart, Iuno advises anchoring to Galbrena once inside. If she dares to hunt the Threnodian, then surely she knows how to navigate the Dark Tide.

With preparations complete, you and Abby leap into the Dark Tide together.

You land in another world. It's not the chaos you expected, but something almost holy. Yet beneath its solemn surface lies a sinister, unsettling frequency.

Your first goal is to reunite with Galbrena, but you barely know anything about her and have no memories to Anchor to.

At that moment, Galbrena's companion, a Tacet Discord called Chimera, appears. He reveals the truth of this Dark Tide world and offers to help you Anchor to Galbrena and guide you through this land.

Follow him. The journey into the underworld begins now.

Within Galbrena's fragmented memories, you see her as a child dazzling the arena with her radiant Forte. That bright-eyed girl is almost unrecognizable from the woman she has become...

In another memory, you watch young Galbrena fall into the Dark Tide. Her Resonance Ability was corrupted, and from that day on, she could no longer wield it...

In yet another memory, you witness Galbrena turn the Dark Tide's power against itself to devour Tidespawn. In the end, Chimera appeared and helped her escape.

But even after escaping, Galbrena was relentlessly hunted by the Tidespawn. Fearing she might endanger Rinascita, she chose exile, fleeing her homeland for distant lands. Over time, she learned to track Tacet Discords through the Dark Tide's fluctuations, roaming the wilds to warn others of impending disasters. It was during this exile that she encountered a Flame Demon Tacet Discord named "Galbrena."

What came after, the Dark Tide cannot reveal. Chimera will tell the rest...

From Chimera's account, you realize it wasn't him who saved Galbrena. It was the human souls that gave her the strength to escape. Chimera only seized the opportunity to inhabit her body, trying to devour her frequency and be reborn through her in a desperate bid to escape the Dark Tide.

But in the end, she chose to surrender more of her frequency, sealing a pact with Chimera in exchange for greater devouring power. By feeding on Tacet Discords, she gradually became what she is today... Someone strong enough to stop greater calamities.

The name "Galbrena," once belonging to a Flame Demon Tacet Disord, was never hers. As for her true name, Chimera suggests you ask her yourself.

The mists lift, revealing Galbrena locked in battle with the Tidespawn at the path's end. Her guns are knocked away, but she holds her ground with nothing but skill and grit.

You pick up her guns, fire a shot that turns the tide, and rush to her side. As promised, you meet again in "hell." With your backs to each other, you face the encroaching swarm. No words are needed. Together, you fight, each guarding one side.

After the fight, you and Galbrena release the human souls trapped within the frequencies of the Tacet Discords.

At last, she reveals the plan she and Cartethyia devised. Leviathan, revived through the Gem, has merged back into the Dark Tide, like water returning to the sea. To defeat It, Its form must first be Anchored. Cartethyia proposed using the godhood within her—its frequency now nearly indistinguishable from her humanity—as bait. If Leviathan could be lured into consuming her humanity, it would be Anchored in the process.

To accomplish this, they first used the Sword of Divinity to separate Cartethyia's godhood and humanity. Then Galbrena's Dark Tide bullet devoured the frequency of her humanity, sending it into the Dark Tide. But since Leviathan cannot discern truth, only belief, the one closest to Cartethyia had to truly believe she was dead. Only then would Leviathan lower Its guard, letting the deception succeed.

You see the unshakable resolve in Galbrena's eyes. In return, you offer her your trust once again.

Fighting through more enemies, you and Galbrena finally reach the "gates of hell."

You and Galbrena arrive at Styx Lake. To activate the Anchor Stone on the far shore, you must cross. But doing so would alert the Dark Tide.

From the darkness, a lone ferryman approaches. If this realm truly mirrors the underworld from Septimont legends, then this must be Kharon, ferryman of the dead.

He exists solely to perform his duty within the Dark Tide. Strange as it is, you have no better option. You ask him to ferry you across the lake.

The crossing is surprisingly smooth. Too smooth. A sense of unease stirs, but for now, your priority is to activate the Anchor Stone so your companions may follow.

You and Galbrena activate the Anchor Stone within the Dark Tide, sending a signal to your companions in the real world.

With the defenses at the Three Heroes' Crest holding steady, Augusta and the others prepare to drink the "antidote" and follow after you. But just then, Iuno senses a powerful frequency moving toward the western front. Scar appears, shattering the Gladiators' defenses in an instant.

Just as all seems lost, Scar's assault is intercepted by Qiuyuan's blade. The very man Qiuyuan was hunting turns out to be none other than this Fractsidus Overseer.

Though Scar is driven back, his attack was only a diversion. High atop the Three Heroes' Crest, Phrolova's "symphony" is already in motion. A rain of Lycoris corrupts the Anti-Corrosion Wards, and the Dark Tide surges through the breach to swallow the lowlands of Septimont. Before they can drink the "antidote," your companions are swept into the Dark Tide...

Then, from the darkness, the ferryman Kharon returns and destroys the Anchor Stone you had just activated. In that moment, Galbrena realizes that his true identity is none other than Schwarzloch, the Grand Architect of the Fractsidus.

In this grand play, his role is to stop "the one who would end Leviathan." Kharon attacks, and Galbrena throws herself in front of you to shield you. She resolves to stay behind while you lead everyone to finish the mission—Knock upon the gates of Paradise and let its bells toll through the dark to guide their way.

By the shores of Styx Lake, Galbrena faces Kharon alone, bloodied and spent. She realizes she has been the Fractsidus's true target all along.

Condescendingly, Kharon questions and tempts her: After devouring so many Tacet Discords, she has long since crossed the threshold of what it means to be human. The Fractsidus is where she can belong and find purpose.

Galbrena answers with scorn. She strikes again, but her bullets freeze in mid-air, her flames cannot reach him, and the Tacet Discords within her are torn away. Even so, she gets back up and charges in a final, desperate strike.

This time, Kharon does not hold back. A shadowed claw pierces straight through her chest. Galbrena only smiles faintly, as if this was her plan all along.

At the stairway to the Paradise, Galbrena arrives only to find you already engulfed by the Dark Tide. To save you, she unleashes the Tacet Discords within herself to burn the Dark Tide with hellfire. Yet against the purest of Dark Tides, even her flames fall short.

So she chooses to ignite her own "life force," setting herself ablaze, all for the sake of a single hope.

After being engulfed by the Dark Tide, you awaken in an unknown space.

On a pallid shore stand countless gravestones, each bearing the name of a companion…

Through the mist, a familiar vision takes shape. Cristoforo's whisper claims this is the truth you cannot escape. On destiny's stage, only you will remain for the final bow.

He claims your plan was doomed from the start. When the Discord Slayer fought to her last breath, your final hope turned to ash. As the Harbinger of the Dark Tide, you already know the only way to change the ending. A sword waits in front of you.

But Galbrena's voice pierces through the mist. She descends, alive and well, shattering Cristoforo's lies. She tells you to look to the sky, where the starlight dispels your doubt. What you must do now is take the next step.

She places a spark in your hand, a flame to carry you forward. Now walk. Through darkness, into the light.

You awaken from the illusion. Your companions are by your side once more, and Galbrena returns atop the back of the Dragon of Dirge.

To reach you, she ignited her own life force, Overclocking her power to tear open the Dark Tide's illusion. Chimera bore part of the cost for her. Meanwhile, when Septimont and your companions were engulfed by the Dark Tide, the Echoes responded to humanity's hope and resolve. They came together in their strongest form to aid. Though the Anchor Stone was destroyed, your companions still followed a different beacon. You, the "Anchor of Hope."

Through the Dark Tide, all of your memories connected. They have learned of Galbrena and Cartethyia's plan too. But Abby can no longer bear the burden of the Gem. Galbrena offers to use her power of devouring to seal the Gem within herself. It is for this very reason that the Fractsidus made her their target.

Cristoforo believed his script to be flawless, but underestimated what small hopes, when united, can become. Now, before the gate of the Paradise, you and your companions stand together. It is time to break it open and cast down the false Divinity that waits beyond.

With the aid of Rinascita's Echoes, you reach the heart of the Paradise. The Dark Tide surges, surrounding you. Leviathan attempts to sway your resolve with Its whispers, but you see through the deception and fight on. At last, Leviathan reveals itself in the likeness of the Blessed Maiden, Fleurdelys.

The moment you have fought so long to reach has arrived. Now, brace for Leviathan's judgment.

Together with your companions, you defeat Leviathan. The Gem is devoured and sealed within Galbrena. With the will of Fenrico and all the past Primuses carried in the Lantern of Napoli the Second, you return safely from the Dark Tide, dispersing it once and for all. The Paradise collapses, the endless night fades, and at last, dawn breaks over Rinascita.

Atop the Three Heroes' Crest, Qiuyuan bids you farewell. While you ventured deep into the Dark Tide, he kept his promise and held the line with Septimont's Gladiators. Now, his journey must continue.

With her soul returned to her body, Cartethyia is revived. Through a voice in the sky, she promises to meet you again in Ragunna. You part ways with your companions from Septimont. Both city-states must now rebuild in their own way. But now, each day to come holds more hope than the last.

You return to Ragunna City with your companions. In the aftermath of battle, the Ragunnesi have come to learn that Primus Fenrico gave his life to disperse the Dark Tide, and Sentinel Imperator is gone as well. Though grief and uncertainty are still fresh, many are already taking the first steps to rebuild their future.

Cartethyia arrives at your side, just as she promised. Together, you recall Imperator's words: The path of civilization is never fixed. It shifts with the needs of the present. And that's up to the people of a civilization to decide.

You turn to Phoebe, still caught in quiet confusion, and give her the Civilization Capsule, the Relic Imperator left behind. She quickly understands what you are entrusting to her.

Now, Cartethyia and Phoebe both step forward to help rebuild Ragunna. Naturally, how could you not be part of it too?

Some time later, Ragunna City's reconstruction is nearly complete. You stop by Margherita's Restaurant to find Galbrena, ready to depart with her for the Black Shores to extract the Threnodian's Gem.

But she seems fascinated by the restaurant's new gelato special. Naturally, you don't want to spoil her good mood. Besides, it is about time you kept that promise—to treat Abby to a well-earned feast.

Still, even Galbrena seems a little daunted when Margherita brings out the extra-large serving. With a familiar smirk, she turns to you and makes an offer you have heard before: "So, one side each?"

After the meal, you and Galbrena head toward the harbor. You spot Calcharo, who aided Ragunna in the battle, waving from afar. 

Galbrena mentions she once worked briefly with him back in the New Federation, and that, surprisingly, he's not all that hard to get along with. If you ever find yourself in the New Federation, you will probably have the chance to become better acquainted.

At Ragunna's harbor, a Black Shores vessel awaits. Cartethyia and the others arrive as well, offering thanks for the aid Galbrena and the Black Shores have given.

Soon, the time for departure comes. Your friends from Ragunna gather to send you off, and even your companions from Septimont arrive on Griffrexes to bid their farewells. Cetus the Tidebreaker rises from the sea to escort your ship. A wave of memories rises with you. Precious memories you will never forget.

To meet and to part, that is the rhythm of life. All that remains to say is: "Till we meet again."

The extraction of the Threnodian's Gem is a success, though Aalto reveals that the recovered Gem is incomplete, and the Black Shores will continue investigating the cause. Still, to contain even one Gem infused with the Threnodian's power is already a huge step forward in the fight against the Threnodian and the Lament.

With the matter concluded, Galbrena prepares to leave. Aalto suggests that the Black Shores could always use more hands.

You invite Galbrena to join the Black Shores. Though you don't expect to tie down a free spirit like the Discord Slayer, she accepts your offer. She pins the Blake Bloom to her chest and fulfills her promise to tell you her true name: Angel.

She spreads her wings, and you let her go. You believe neither storm nor sea could stop her. One day, you will meet again.

As you watch Galbrena vanish into the skies, the paper crane that was once Avidius slips from you, rising gently into the high heavens...

Somewhere in Rinascita, the members of the Fractsidus gather once more…
