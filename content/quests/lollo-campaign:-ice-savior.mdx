Dialogue[]

(Hiya, Maggie!)
Maji: Old friend! Finally, you're here! I'm facing the biggest crisis of my life!
Maji: Who wouldn't thought that after years of running logistics, my delivery speed still can't outpace melting ice cube!
 Quit exaggerating, Maggie. You're still a rookie.
Maji: Hey, I've been running errands for my mom since I was four! Don't tell me that's not logistics experience!
 Wait... are you saying even ice cubes are using Lollo Logistics now?
Maji: Please, don't spin surreal fantasies out of a nine-to-fiver's words.
 Alright then, tell me your trouble, Maggie-kun.
 So what exactly are you racing against ice cubes?
Maji: You know me, every crisis I have starts with a business opportunity.
Maji: Not long ago, I went to Septimont to watch an Agon. The Gladiators' fiery passion, their movements... It moved me to my core!
Maji: Even I was waving my arms, screaming myself hoarse.
Maji: And right then, I thought... sure, there were vending machines in the stands, but if someone came running over with a drink clinking with ice cubes... Ah! Wouldn't that be more refreshing than any perfectly-aligned workflow?
 ...Up until you said "workflow," it actually did sound refreshing.
Maji: You don't get it! Lose freedom, you lose a lot. Lose perfectly-aligned workflow, you lose everything!
Rover: (...Pretty sure that's now how the saying goes.)
Maji: Anyway! I jumped right into putting the plan into action.
Maji: But as I said, no matter what I try, I can't outrun melting ice!
Rover: Not even with coolers or ice packs?
Maji: No! Friend, no cooler and ice pack can withstand the blazing passion of the crowd!
Rover: Don't suddenly get all worked up, Maggie!
Maji: Sorry, sorry. Septimont's Agons just fire me up. But you see, my career is now melting away like those ice cubes.
Maji: A cold drink is heaven in a glass in this scorching heat.
Maji: But once the ice melts, everything is watered down. No one likes watered-down content, be it a drink, or a game.
Maji: If Lollo Logistics get branded as a "watered-down company" because of this...
Maji: My colleagues will drag me back to HQ as cargo and put me on trial!
Maji: So please, my old friend, save me!
 Somehow, I get the feeling I'll be hearing this sort of wailing from you often...
 Then I suggest asking Lumi to deliver you back to the HQ. Maybe she'll go soft on you.
Maji: ...
Maji: Come on, I know you'll help me. I don't expect you to shoulder the risks for me. But if you do, I promise you'd always have a stake in my payroll. We'll go from rags to riches together.
Maji: So, same as always! New requests will pop up on the board every day. With your skills, it'll be a walk in the park, and Lollo Stamps will slip right into your pocket as usual.
Maji: Think about it! Who else will answer the desperate cries of those parched, hoarse, dizzy fans? No one but us!
Maji: We'll be the rising heroes! Not only the Gladiators fighting valiantly in the arena are heroes! So are we, delivering chilled drinks with swift and shining valor!
Maji: So remember! Beat the ice before it melts!
Rover: (Honestly... it's your fiery speeches that keep melting the ice, Maggie...)
