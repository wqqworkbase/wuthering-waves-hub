Steps[]

- Talk to Maqi to find out about today's game scenario.

- Complete today's scenario

- (Optional) Ask Fuyan about the Mithril.

- Deliver the "Mithril" to Maqi
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionMaqi is trying to contact you, and it appears to be related to the Pioneer Association's ambitious project, "Second Coming of Solaris"...

(Talk to Maqi)
 Today's scenario...
Maqi: Let me explain our job today.
Maqi: In this chapter, they ventured all the way to the North and reached Mt. Moore, preparing to meet the final enemy.
Maqi: Before their enemy showed up, the air of Mt. Moore had already chilled the protagonist to the bone.
Maqi: It goes: "...Obscuring the moonlight, black clouds even made the Mithril on the hills lose its sheen..."
Maqi: "Everything seemed to be flowing, filling my lungs like condensed ink. As I took my breaths, I was dragged into darkness further."
Maqi: This part can never cease to impress me with the howling sense of despair, even after all of my repeated readings.
Maqi: I can't help but wonder, what would induce such a sensational scene? And this Mithrill&#32;&#91;sic&#93;... what does it look like?
 We need a reference for this.
 It says it shines bright like the moon.
Maqi: Indeed. The black clouds had an overwhelming presence as they had swallowed the glow of the moon and minerals.
Maqi: Samples of Mithrill&#32;&#91;sic&#93; will help improve the mental image of this chapter, I believe.
Maqi: If you happen to come across some samples, feel free to drop by.

(Optional - Talk to Maqi)
 Give me some clues to the Mithril.
Maqi: Well, maybe you should ask the owner of Huaipu Mining Firm. He is a knowledgeable mineral expert.
Maqi: Since it's about the Mithril, I suggest you go ask for his advice.

(Optional - Talk to Fuyan)
 About the Mithril in the novel...
Fuyan: Second Coming of Solaris? I could spare little interest for these casual reads of you youngsters...
Fuyan: ...though I do know a thing about this Mithril.
Fuyan: A translucent mineral called Lampylumen would produce an azure shine that makes it even brighter in the dark.
Fuyan: I'm assuming the Mithril probably originates from the Lampylumen.
Fuyan: You've mentioned its writer, Karl, right? I would like to have a little chat with a mineral expert like him.
 I don't know if it's possible.
 He is far out in the New Federation now.
Fuyan: *sigh* What a shame... As you get older, you'd realize how hard it is to meet someone who shares your passion.
 Anyway, do you have some Lampylumens available?
Fuyan: What a coincidence. I happen to keep a Lampylumen remnant left by the previous guest.
 Brillant!
 May I have it?
Fuyan: The Lampylumen is a specialty mineral only found in the caves of the Panyan mountains.
Fuyan: But if it's useful to you, I'll not reserve it.
 Thank you, Mr. Owner!
Fuyan: You're welcome, hehe! Business is all about honesty and I'd like to maintain a good relationship with my customers.
Fuyan: Do remember to come over next time! Huaipu Mining Firm has the best mineral salt and petrolatum.
Fuyan: Take it. I know you have a busy schedule today.
(Obtain Lampylumen x1)

(Talk to Maqi)
(Submit Lampylumen x1)
 I'm turning in the Mithril.
Maqi: Wow. This mineral surely fits the depiction of the Mithril. I'll hand it over to the dev team right now!
Maqi: When the black clouds veil the moon... This is the perfect trigger!
Some time passed while Maqi was away to hand over the item...
Maqi: Thank you. That wraps up today's work!
Maqi: See you tomorrow, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
(Quest Complete)
