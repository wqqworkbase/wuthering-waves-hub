Summary[]

The Shorekeeper warns you of Abby's anomalies. Through your intervention, the Tethys System has returned to its rightful state, now operating with peaceful and harmonious frequencies.

Steps[]

- Wait for a while

- (Optional) Adjust the time to two days later

- Head to the Greenhouse and find the Shorekeeper
