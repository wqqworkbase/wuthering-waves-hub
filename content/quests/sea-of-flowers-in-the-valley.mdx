Steps[]

- Talk to Maqi to find out about today's game scenario.

- Complete today's scenario

- (Optional) Find Linsu and find out about the Sea of Flowers.

- Give the Sea of Flowers to Maqi
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionMaqi is trying to contact you, and it appears to be related to the Pioneer Association's ambitious project, "Second Coming of Solaris"...

(Talk to Maqi)
 Today's scenario...
Maqi: Let me explain our job today.
Maqi: In this chapter, they passed right by a valley on their way to Mt. Moore.
Maqi: It goes: "We ventured through the valley as the night fell..."
Maqi: "In the sole darkness of night, the Sea of Flowers seemed to fall noir... Pressed between petals and leaves, we walked as clothes uttered gentle friction..."
Maqi: "Pale and silent as it was, the night permitted our disturbing existence. No, I heard murmurs upon the waves, saying "O travelers O winds..."
 I could feel the tranquility.
 A relaxing scene, as it is.
Maqi: Yes. I find inner peace in this.
Maqi: But to think of what comes after this chapter, I slow down my pace...
Maqi: Anyway, we need the reference for the Sea of Flowers to create the valley according to this chapter.
Maqi: If you happen to come across some samples, feel free to drop by.

(Optional - Talk to Maqi)
 Give me some clues about Sea of Flowers.
Maqi: Speaking of the Sea of Flowers, I do visit Bogu's Arbor around the business area when off shift.
Maqi: If you want to inquire about something related to plants, you can ask Grandma Lin, the owner there, for some information.
(Optional - Talk to Linsu)
 About the Sea of Flowers in the novel...
Linsu: Rongqing has told me some about this book.
Linsu: I can't say I have much experience with stuff like this... I'm just too old for a packed page...
 I can read the pages to you if you like.
Linsu: You're so sweet. Just tell me if you want to buy some PenjingBonsai. I'm giving you discounts!
 Anything similar to what's described in the novel?
Linsu: Well, you certainly can't expect a potted plant to look nearly as impressive as an actual sea of flowers.
Linsu: But I guess a luxuriant pot of irises may capture the feel.
Linsu: It originates from Whining Aix's Mire and its flower language is fragile, untold affections. Romantic, isn't it?
 Do you sell it here?
Linsu: It has a short flowering season, making it unsuitable for PenjingBonsai. So we usually don't sell them here. But Verina happened to drop by a few days ago and gave me a few.
Linsu: I'll give one to you if you need it.
 Thank you, Grandma Lin!
Linsu: You're welcome. Just drop by anytime.
(Obtain Iris x1)

(Talk to Maqi)
 I'm turning in "Sea of Flowers"
(Submit Iris x1)
Maqi: It's beautiful. Thank you for the work. I'll hand it over to the dev team.
Maqi: It would be easier for them to imagine a sea of flowers with this.
Some time passed while Maqi was away to hand over the item...
Maqi: Thank you. That wraps up today's work!
Maqi: See you tomorrow, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
(Quest Complete)
