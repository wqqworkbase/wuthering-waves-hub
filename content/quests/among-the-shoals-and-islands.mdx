Among the Shoals and Islands is a Exploration Quest that occurs in Mt. Firmament, Huanglong, [[{{{nation}}}]]. It is the second quest in the series: Mirrored Loong's Pearl.

Steps[]

- Activate the Altar of Initiation

- Connect the Light Path with the Altar of Radiance

- Complete Rite of Offering where light beam points

- Stand in the light and adjust the camera to align the Pearl with the Loong's mouth
