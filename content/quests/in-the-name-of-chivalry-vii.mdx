Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYour training with Faro has proven successful, and now stories of a living knight have been echoed through Whisperwind Haven. But now, the nearby TDs are troubling passersby. Eliminate them before they cause harm.

(Talk to Faro)
Faro: Phew... You're here. I managed to defeat those Tacet Discords, and the people were only a little shaken.
 I thought you needed my help.
Faro: Oh no, I may have used too much force and broken my weapon again. It took me quite a while to find one as suitable as that.
 Out with the old, in with the new.
 Weapons are expendable.
Faro: You're right. What matters most is the chivalrous spirit of protecting others, right?
Faro: I will keep moving forward, following in your footsteps.
(Quest Complete)
