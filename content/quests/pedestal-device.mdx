Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionSome strange pedestals in the wild pique Researcher Qinggong's interest and he wants your help over this issue.

Qinggong: Rover, excuse me.
Qinggong: When you are exploring outside, have you seen some pedestals with strange projections?
 I've seen them.
Qinggong: Nice, saves me a lot of explaining.
 Never saw anything like that.
Qinggong: No worries, let me explain.
Qinggong: While researching, I stumbled upon these weird round pedestals with holographic projections.
Qinggong: The circuit design was pretty strange. Apparently, they're called an Imitative Sonic Device according to the records.
Qinggong: I've always been curious about these devices, so I made a special trip just to check them out.
Qinggong: But nothing happened when I tried to touch the projection. My hand slid through it. It's likely because I'm not a Resonator.
Qinggong: So I was wondering if I could ask you to investigate these pedestals in person. What do you think?
 Leave it to me.
Qinggong: Great, I'll send you the coordinates. Thank you, Rover!

(Talk to Qinggong before investigating the pedestal device)
Qinggong: The temperature is just right, and the humidity is...
 What are you doing?
Qinggong: Me? I'm observing the experiment subjects and recording data.
Qinggong: The weather in Jinzhou has been stable lately, so the subjects are in perfect condition.

(Talk to Qinggong after investigating the pedestal device)
Qinggong: The air temperature is comfortable, and the humidity level is...
 About those pedestals...
Qinggong: Have you investigated those pedestals? How did it go? Wait, what? They let you fight simulated monsters?
Qinggong: I see... Now I can understand.
Qinggong: The Court of Savantae probably created these pedestals to study dangerous Tacet Discords.
Qinggong: Researchers can learn more about those Tacet Discords by studying data from simulated battles.
Qinggong: Thank you for your help! By the way, I recently found a couple more of these pedestals.
Qinggong: I'll send you the coordinates. If you're still interested, go check them out.
Qinggong: Who knows, they might help you on your journey!
(Quest Complete)
