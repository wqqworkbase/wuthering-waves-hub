 Quest DescriptionThe Day 5 Campaign content has been updated, but a courier has encountered difficulties and is calling for help...

(Talk to Maji)
Maji: Gloves, boots, spare crates, and emergency rations... All that's left is...
Maji: Oh, it's you, Rover. Just in time to show you another feature of "Lollo Navigator."
Maji: Lollo Navigator can give couriers info on supply wear and consumption along the route based on real-time geographical and road conditions.
Maji: And whenever they need a resupply, they can just request it through the app. This batch of orders is still missing some items.

 Feels like I've heard of this one before.
 This guy never changes, all right.
