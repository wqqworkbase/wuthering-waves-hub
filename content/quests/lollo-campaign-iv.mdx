Steps[]

- Ask Maji if he needs help

- Go find the missing Lollo Logistics staff in Dim Forest

- Disperse the horde of Hoochiefs

- Talk to Maji
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionMaji looks strange today. Ask him what happened.

(Ask Maji if he needs help)
 Why are you still wearing the uniform?
Maji: I'm sorry, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). We'll have to suspend the pilot business for today. I have something urgent to attend to.
 What happened?
Maji: A courier at the Jinzhou Station, Guanxian, has gone missing. He was supposed to deliver the goods to the port Gunchao Logistics Station an hour ago, but we haven't been able to reach him.
Maji: The navigation system shows that Guanxian is in Dim Forest. I was about to go and find him, that's why I changed into this outfit.
 Why did he go to Dim Forest?
Maji: Most likely, trying to take a shortcut.
Maji: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), the situation is urgent. I heard that you can use the Resonance Beacon to reach Dim Forest faster. I must ask you for help.
 Let's go.

(Disperse the horde of Hoochiefs)
After dispersing the group of Hoochiefs, you and Maji bring the frightened freshman courier back to Jinzhou.

(Talk to Maji)
 Report today's results.
Maji: Thanks for your help, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). Guanxian is fine, with just a few scratches.
Maji: When he was taking the shortcut, a group of Hoochiefs found him and got greedy for the package in his box.
Maji: Even though he was chased by the Hoochiefs all around, he managed to hide the package while the Hoochiefs were distracted.
Maji: When we rescued him, he somehow managed to find the items from somewhere.
 Luckily, everything turned out fine.
Maji: As a Lollo Logistics courier, the first priority is to take responsibility for your own safety. Otherwise, how can you take responsibility for the packages you handle?
Maji: What Lollo Logistics couriers least want to hear is the alarm for the activation of the "relay plan," which means...
Maji remains silent for a while before calming down.
Maji: I'm sorry, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), I was being frivolous. Thank you very much for your help.
Maji: Here is today's reward. Thank you for lending a hand!
(Quest Complete)
