Steps[]

- Search for clues downstream

- Use "Hero's Rend" to cut open the suspicious object

- Pick up the box

- Give the box to Samousas
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest awards the hidden Exploration: Rinascita trophy I Abandon Here My Happiness.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionSamousas stash was washed downstream. To fulfill his childhood dream, he asks you to retrieve the lost box.

(Talk to Samousas)
Samousas: How could it just disappear like that?! I'm 100% sure it was right here a moment ago!
Samousas: How could this happen? My money! My money, my soul... My only hope!
 What happened?
Samousas: The money I hid here is gone, but there aren't any other footprints here. Did it get washed downstream?
Samousas: You look quite nimble, my friend. Perhaps you could help me? If you could find my money for me, I'd be happy to repay your efforts.
 Leave it to me.
Samousas: You're a lifesaver! If you just follow down the river, I'm sure you'll find clues!
 Why don't you contact the bank?
Samousas: Because... my family doesn't know that I play Peaks of Prestige! And if they were to check my bank records...
Samousas: If people find out I was using all of my salary to buy limited edition card packs... As an adult, that's equivalent to a death sentence!
Samousas: But if someone were to ask me to give up my Peaks of Prestige addiction... No, that's impossible! A life without PoP would be as meaningless as living as a Tacet Discord!
Samousas: I'm begging you, great guest from afar! Just follow the river and I'm sure you'll find something!

(When interacting with Samousas before picking up his treasure)
Samousas: How about it, friend? Have you found the money yet?
 Found it.
Samousas: OHHH! You are truly my savior! Give it here...
 No, not yet.
Samousas: Oh, I see... Well, do keep looking, please. Just follow the river and I'm sure you'll find something!

Samousas' Treasure
A heavy chest, stashed away here by Samousas.
It gives off a dull metallic clatter when shaken. It seems like it's packed with coins... and some dreams long since  forgotten.

(Deliver the box)
Samousas: My benefactor! My savior! Oh, thank you! Thank you! You've found it!
Samousas: Now I'm one step closer to buying my next card pack... And if I finally manage to draw that Echo card, I'll become the ultimate duel master!
Just as Samousas thanks you, a woman walks over to you.
Favnia: Samousas? What're you doing here? Did I just hear you yell "card pack" and "ultimate duel master"? What's this all about?
Favnia: You're not planning on playing Peaks of Prestige, are you? And where'd you get that money from?
Samousas: Eek! Favnia! I-I didn't... I'm n-not... This is not my money! My friend here was asking me for some safekeeping! A-And I was just about to give it back to you, isn't that right?
Samousas gives you a look.
Rover: ...That's right, of course.
Favnia: Well, is it now? Then you'd best give it back, Samousas!
With great pain, Samousas hands you the box of money before being dragged away by Favnia...
(Quest Complete)
