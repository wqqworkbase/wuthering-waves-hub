Steps[]

- Talk to Xiuyi near the Souvenir Store

- Step onto the platform made of wooden boxes

- Complete the challenge

- Start a new session of footwork practice.
Gameplay Notes[]

- Hidden Challenge near the Souvenir Store, comes up as "Inquire about Xiuyi's challenge"

- You get six chests for completing the multiple challenges.
