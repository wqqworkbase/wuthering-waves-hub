[Sword of Mercy Pedestal][]

Let this pedestal stand as a testament!

Here, the Knightly Virtue of Mercy endures!

It is told that the knight "Silver Moon" once stood vigil to the west of Mistveil Bay, guarding the merchants who passed through. There, he drove away the fierce TDs and vanquished the ruthless Alari family.

To show mercy to the innocent, one must be merciless to the wicked.

[Sword of Honor Pedestal][]

Let this pedestal stand as a testament!

Here, the Knightly Virtue of Honor endures!

It is told that the knight "Silver Moon" once dueled on the bridge leading to the Thorncrown Rises near Egla Town, where he bested a hundred challengers.

Honor is a fine blade tempered in the fires of challenge.

(Deliver Sword of Vanity)

[Sword of Generosity Pedestal][]

Let this pedestal stand as a testament!

Here, the Knightly Virtue of Generosity endures!

It is told that the knight "Silver Moon," in his valiant struggle to protect the land from TDs, emptied his own coffers and called upon the people to donate. The gathered wealth was displayed in the cottage by the windmill and used to fortify defenses which, at only a modest cost, repelled the TD assault.

In giving, one finds reward.

(Deliver Sword of Greed)

[Sword of Wisdom Pedestal][]

Let this pedestal stand as a testament!

Here, the Knightly Virtue of Wisdom, endures!

It is told that the knight "Silver Moon", in his time, devoted himself to the study of the knightly path in Silver Moon Grove. Over the years, he wrote volumes on what he learned, so that future knights might follow his teachings.

To the wise, fate serves; to the foolish, fate rules. 

(Deliver Sword of Ignorance)

[Memory Fragment 1][]

On the first full moon of my knighthood, I was defeated by a band of Plushies. The big Plushie was more terrifying than I imagined―a slight hesitation almost cost me my life. I was lucky to survive. But Sentinel, I ask you to witness how I conquer my fear and become unyielding. Under your divine gaze, I will forge myself into the very embodiment of the Five Knightly Virtues!

Knight of Atrocity[]

(Approach the Knight)
Abby: Woah! All these Fractsidus fellas lying on the ground... Was it that big guy's doing?
Knight of Atrocity: Force... is justice... Force... is mercy!

[Memory Fragment 2][]

On the forty-second full moon of my knighthood, a young man stabbed the right of my abdomen. In retaliation, I took his life. The Order rewarded my killings and celebrated my bloodlust. Yet Sentinel, if my words reach you, I beg for clarity: is mercy the path to saving grace or a swift descent into an early grave?

Knight of Vanity[]

(Enter the bridge)
Knight of Vanity: Come. Fight me, and fall before me.

[Memory Fragment 3][]

On the ninetieth full moon of my knighthood, I was assigned to duel on the great bridge. Day after day, knights came to challenge me, only to fall again and again―all to hasten my so-called glory of a hundred victories. O Sentinel, do you hear me? The weight of honor grows heavier, yet my sword feels ever more brittle.

[Memory Fragment 4][]

On the ninety-fifth full moon of my knighthood, I managed to gather fifteen crates of donations. After giving dues to the Order and the family I serve, only five remained. Even with the three crates I donated... No, it's not enough. It's nowhere near enough! O Sentinel, please enlighten me: why do the greediest souls always hide behind the banner of generosity?
