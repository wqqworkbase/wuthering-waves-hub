Marco: Hey there, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! Come over and meet Julien. He's a cat that just can't resist the harbor life.
Marco: Every time I give him a fish and try to talk about his grand feline aspirations, he meows back at me.
Julien: Meow!
 What are his feline aspirations?
 Why exactly is he meowing?
Marco: He vows to achieve peak cat greatness&#8212;catching all the critters! Isn't that right, Julien?
Julien: Meow... Meow!
 Sounds like he's on board.
Marco: No doubt about it! If he fufills his birthright, he'll have all the fish he can eat!
 Sounds like he's disagreeing with you.
Marco: Impossible... There's no doubt in my mind he aims to fulfill his birthright. He'll have all the fish he can eat!
Upon hearing the word "fish," Julien begins to meow in excitement. His true feline aspirations are suddenly clear.
 He only meows at you hoping for fish.
Marco: Outrageous! I thought he saw something in me...
 He only hangs around the harbor looking for fish.
Marco: How could that be?! It's the cat's dreams we're talking about here. It can't just be about fish!
 He craves total dominion over all fish.
Marco: There's some truth in that. Fish are the crown jewel of a cat's life!
Marco: Oh, Julien, tell me it's not true! Is it really just about the fish?
Julien: Meow, meow!

Marco: Good idea... But I haven't managed to catch a single one today!
