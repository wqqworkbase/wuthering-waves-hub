Steps[]

- Head to the test site and speak with Elowyn

- Move the Cube Blocker to the target point and activate the Route Constructor to print the test track

- Ride the motorbike and complete the track test

- Jump onto the track

- Collect Riding Points and complete the track test

- Talk to Elowyn and Zahira
