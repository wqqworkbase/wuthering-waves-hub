Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionEbony Gatekeeper tells you of an anomaly inside the Somnoire. Something lurking there is sinisterly gazing at this world. Perhaps this is not the end, but the beginning.

(Talk to Ebony Gatekeeper)
Ebony Gatekeeper: It's been a while, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). You did it. The Somnoire has returned to peace.
 Where's Dorothy?
Ebony Gatekeeper: I've sent her back to the Somnoire. She doesn't belong here. It's not good for her to stay in the outside world for long.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Once again, thank you for helping us through the crisis.
Ebony Gatekeeper: All that talk about balance... and look at the mess that ivory fool made. We had to clean up after him.
 So he created those nightmares?
Ebony Gatekeeper: You could say that. Though it was more of an accident than anything.
Ebony Gatekeeper: He opened a gate that should never have been opened, allowing those monsters from the depths to slip into the shallower realm.
Ebony Gatekeeper: The good thing is, we managed to draw their attention and buy him some time. If he's smart, he should've sealed the gate by now.
 Why did he open the gate?
Ebony Gatekeeper: I have my suspicions, but it's too soon to reveal them. I'll have a "talk" with him first.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Our paths will cross again soon, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)... And that's when you'll once again determine the Somnoire's fate.
(Quest Complete)
