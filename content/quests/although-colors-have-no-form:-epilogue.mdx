 Quest DescriptionThe investigation of the mysterious morph painting concludes smoothly, and it seems Claudia wishes to thank you in person.
Perhaps it's time to pay her a visit.

(Talk to Claudia)
Claudia: What did you see in that painting, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)?
Claudia: What? A different world every time? The painting is filled with monsters? My talisman turned into an Echo? How quaint...
Claudia: But thank the Sentinel you're safe and sound, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), and that's the most important thing. I'm actually curoius about your thoughts, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). What did you feel in the worlds inside the painting?
 It feels like a dream from the Somnoire...
Claudia: A dream from... the Somnoire? Is that a place where people's dreams come together?
Claudia: I've heard from the elders that the Sentinel can connect our souls, making us one... But looking at it now, is that really a good thing?
 I felt a vortex of heartfelt emotions from many creators.
Claudia: I see. No wonder I've always felt that this painting doesn't seem to present a full concept. My elders also mentioned that the Sentinel can make people's hearts connect, becoming one...
Claudia: But if all become one, then no matter how deep the artists' feelings were, everything loses its shape and meaning when the colors mix together.
 I was exhilarated, as if something was calling me to battle.
Claudia: A surprising answer. You always speak your mind, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). I... admire how straightforward you are.
Claudia: But no matter how vibrant the colors are, they just turn into a grayish-black mess when mixed together...
Claudia: Painting, after all, is an art that expresses the colors of the heart. Those stifling blues and fiery reds are the hidden reflections of the creator's inner world.
Claudia: The worlds within the painting are fascinating, but being trapped in them prevents me from creating my own work... I should shift my focus outward and observe more.
Claudia: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), thank you for your assistance in completing this investigation and for sharing your feelings.
Claudia: Please take this talisman, if you don't mind.
Claudia: After all, it can only serve its purpose in those worlds.
(Quest Complete)
