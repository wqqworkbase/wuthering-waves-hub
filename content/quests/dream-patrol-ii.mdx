Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionContinue the challenges of Dream Patrol and defeat the nightmares hidden within the Dreamscape Nodes.

Strange White Cat: I see... so that's what was lurking deep within the network of Dreamscape Nodes.
Strange White Cat: There's something actively collecting people's nightmares and negative emotions...
Strange White Cat: Well done, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). For now, the Somnoire connection has seen some progress. Let's discuss it further in person.

(Talk to the Strange White Cat)
Strange White Cat: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), do you think that special TD is similar to some of the memory fragments in the Somnoire?
 There are some resemblances.
Strange White Cat: Yes, if you look closely, it's very similar to those corrupted dreams.
 And it reminded me of an old acquaintance...
Strange White Cat: I'm talking about the corrupted dreams, meow.
Strange White Cat: Dreams are visions from the subconscious. Even when they solidify into memory fragments, they still carry all the joy and sorrow of the dreamer.
Strange White Cat: But those special TDs... it's as though people's subconscious have been unified by a single concept, carrying nothing but one desire.
Strange White Cat: I tried to peer into the depths of the network to see what lay beyond. For a brief moment, I saw a single frame of image and almost drowned in the tidal sound, losing myself.
 What kind of image?
Strange White Cat: I saw a pitch-black abyss, where people were neatly lined up, crawling, performing the same gestures, murmuring the same words, trapped in the same dream.
Strange White Cat: In the next moment they noticed my presence and turned to meet my gaze... Their eyes... Their pupils were stained with the same substance...
Strange White Cat: And in the darkness behind them, a pair of enormous eyes slowly opened... I've never seen a dream like this before.
 That sounds unsettling...
Strange White Cat: Yes. Just thinking about it gives me an uneasy feeling.
 They all turned to look at you?
Strange White Cat: It wasn't normal, meow... Just thinking about it gives me an uneasy feeling.
Strange White Cat: That dream was far too dangerous and unsettling. What could be lurking in the darkness beneath Rinascita's waters? Have the people of Ragunna always been living with such a threat?
Strange White Cat: So, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), I'd like to ask you to keep eliminating those TDs hidden within the network. Only then will the dreams housed in the Somnoire be safe.
(Quest Complete)
