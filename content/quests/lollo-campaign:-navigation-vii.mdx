Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionAs the Campaign nears its conclusion, an emergency arises. Your immediate assistance is needed to address the situation...
(Talk to Maji)
Maji: The rain from last night finally stopped, Rover. And the trial run of our program is finally drawing to a close.
 Time to throw the confetti!
Maji: Let me see if "Lollo Navigator" has received any... Wait a sec, someone actually used the expedite feature.
Maji: I's from a courier near Taoyuan Vale. Looks like that heavy rain last night did a number on the shipping labels.
Maji: Now they can't make out the goods and need help from the "Lollo Navigator."
 Can the shipping labels also be recorded?
Maji: Of course not, it would count as a violation of the user's privacy. But don't forget that the map is updated live.
Maji: It can't record the user's information, but it can record and track their transportation routes on the map.
Maji: Each goods use different routes, so we can make a rough estimate based on this information, and then use the logistics system to verify.
Maji: Let's go, Rover. Gotta save that perfect score on the last day of the trial run!

(Complete the final day's job with Maji)
After some hard work, you and Maji used the data recorded by the "Lollo Navigator" to help the couriers verify their goods. In the end, all the couriers gave you a thumbs up.
Maji: Phew... I'm so beat. Even with the "Lollo Navigator," it took a helluva longer than I expected.
 But it was worth the good reviews.
Maji: You're right. "Lollo Navigator" did its job well, and thank you for testing out all of the many features during its trial run.
Maji: I'll sort out all this data and fix any potential problems. Here's hoping that "Lollo Navigator" can officially go live and be promoted for mass use someday...
Maji: See this, I left a spot right next to the developer's credits.
 I wish you success.
