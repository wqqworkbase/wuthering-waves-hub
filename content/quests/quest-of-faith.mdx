Steps[]

- Explore the strange ruins

- Defeat enemies within the illuminated area

- Investigate this area

- Investigate the ruins

- Use Fae Ignis's attack to ignite the torches

- Defeat enemies within the illuminated areas

- Investigate this area

- Go to the third ruin

- Ignite the torches

- Ignite the torch and defeat enemies within the illuminated area

- Check the information left by the TD

- Go to the fourth ruin

- Defeat the enemies in the ruins

- Read the knights' final records
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest will award the player the Footprints in Rinascita I trophy We're All Alone, as well as access to the area's Dreamscape Nodes.
