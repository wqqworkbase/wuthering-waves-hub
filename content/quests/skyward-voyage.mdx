Steps[]

- Show Davide the Cuddle Wuddle

- Witness Davide's departure

- Talk to Davide

- Show Davide the Fae Ignis

- Witness Davide's departure

- Talk to Davide

- Defeat the Fractsidus members

- Save the Wingray

- Talk to Davide

- Witness Davide's departure
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest awards the Footprints in Rinascita I trophy My Conquest is the Sea of Stars
