Serendipity is a Side Quest that occurs in Septimont, Rinascita, [[{{{nation}}}]]. It is an Afterstory to Flames of Heart.

Steps[]

- Go to the cable car station and talk to Buling
Gameplay Notes[]

- Talking to Buling down the cable car awards the Moments with You trophy Mirror, Mirror.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionThe turmoil in Septimont has been resolved. The young lady who offered you help is now on vacation.

(Head to the designated location)
Buling: Boss! What a coincidence! You here for the cable car too?
Buling: From up here, the Septimont view is so good! Before I go, I think I will ride a few more times, definitely.
Rover: You're getting ready to leave?
Buling: Yes! My holiday fun in Septimont is already finished. Time to go back.
 About the copper mirror...
Buling: You keep it, no need to return! It's a little token of good fortune for us.
Buling: What's real or illusion, hard to say now. Master always said I had bad luck. Easy for me to get into trouble. Guess he was right!
Rover: Did you realize you were in a Sonoro at the start?
Buling: I had no idea! For a Taoist like me, it felt like... I dunno, some kind of "out-of-body" thing.
Buling: I asked for your help because... it just felt right. Master always said no need to overthing these things.
Buling: Bye bye, Boss! You're a very fun person.
Buling: Who knows, maybe we'll meet again someday?
(Quest Complete)

(Talk to Buling down the cable car)
Buling: Hey Boss, want to do this together?
 What are you doing here?
Buling: Riding the cable car! It's so fun! The wind's nice, and you get a good view of all of Septimont.
Buling: When I was traveling across Huanglong, some places didn't have bridges or roads. I like checking out the views better this way.
Rover: I thought you'd do what you usually do and vanish on me the second you said you were leaving Septimont.
Buling: That was the plan, but... guess what? I got new orders. Can't run off just yet.
Buling: The sky is changing...
Buling: To "see" clearly, you need to pick a good spot, somewhere high up to get the full view.
Rover: What are you trying to see?
Buling: Oh, you know... just the fate of Rinascita! Gotta stay sharp, don't want to miss anything important.
