Steps[]

- Go to the Ministry of War and ask about Guhong.

- Find Guhong

- Defeat the Tacet Discord

- Go back to the neighborhood and visit Mrs. Jué.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionMrs. Jué is worried about Guhong, who regularly visits the elderly but hasn't shown up for a few days. Help her find him.

(Talk to Grandma Jue)
Grandma Jue: Oh... I wonder how everything is going on the frontlines...
Grandma Jue: Guhong hasn't visited in a while... I hope the child's doing okay...
Grandma Jue: *sigh*... If it weren't for my age, I'd want to go up there and have a look for myself...
Grandma Jue: (&#8205;&#8205;Young manText for male Rover&#8205;/&#8205;Young ladyText for female Rover&#8205;&#8205;). Can you help an old lady find Guhong?
 Sure thing, ma'am.
Grandma Jue: Thank you. Do be careful on your way.
Guhong is a Midnight Ranger, which is under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of War.
Let's pay the Ministry of War and ask about Guhong there.

(Talk to Shiyu)
Shiyu: Hello. Can I help you?
 Mrs. Jué would like to know where she can find Guhong.
Shiyu: Guhong? Last I heard he returned to the frontlines... You're saying even Mrs. Jué hasn't seen him since?
Shiyu: This is not good. Maybe something's happened to him...
Shiyu: Rover, do you think you can help find Guhong for me? Here's his contact information.
 Leave it to me.
Shiyu: I can't thank you enough. As you can see, I'm still on my sentry duty and cannot leave the post unattended, so I have no choice but to leave the task to you.
(Dialing the number)
Guhong: ... Desorock...
Guhong: ... Cave...
Guhong: ... Requesting... Backup...

(Find Guhong)
Guhong: I-I was careless...
Guhong: That was too close... Luck for me you found me just in time, or else...
 Are you all right?
 We're not out of the woods yet. We should head back to Jinzhou.
Guhong: Look out, behind you!

(After defeating enemies)
Guhong: Thank you, Rover.
 Mrs. Jué asked me to come find you.
Guhong: I was careless and got ambushed. Got held back... I regret having Mrs. Jué feel worried.
Guhong: Can you help me back to the city, Rover?
Guhong: I wish to tell Mrs. Jué what happened on the frontlines in person.
 I got you.

(Visit Mrs. Jué)
Grandma Jue: Oh, Guhong. What happened to you?!
Guhong: I'm okay, Mrs. Jué. Rover pulled me out of danger just in the nick of time.
Grandma Jue: Nonsense! Just look at those wounds! Whatever it is that you want to say, it'll have to wait. For now, you are in great need of rest.
(Quest Complete)
