Steps[]

- Head to the shooting party's starting location.

- Wait for the shooting party to begin.

- Talk to the Exile leader organizing the shooting party.

- Participate in the Novice Challenge

- Talk to the cunning Exile leader

- Participate in the Intermediate Challenge

- Talk to the cunning Exile leader

- Participate in the Advanced Challenge

- Confront Exile leader

- Defeat the Exiles in the camp

- Claim your rewards
Gameplay notes[]

- Part of the No Response Tonight quest, complete the steps to unlock.
