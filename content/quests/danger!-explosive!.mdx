Danger! Explosive! is a Side Quest that occurs in Dim Forest, Huanglong, [[{{{nation}}}]]. It can be started by detonating the Explosive Charges.

Steps[]

- Talk to Exile Yongli

- Collect Explosive Charges in the Exiles camp

- Talk to Exile Feng

- Collect ingredients and deliver them to Feng

- Collect the remaining Explosive Charges from Feng

- Deliver Explosive Charges

- Blast the Rock Wall

- Talk to the Exiles (0ptional)

- Detonate Explosive Charges

- Defeat the attacking Exiles

- Talk to Exile Yongli
