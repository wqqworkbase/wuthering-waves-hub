Steps[]

Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionAlannah appears to have her sights set on a key figure in Jinzhou...

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: Who do you think is the richest person in Jinzhou, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)?
 Magistrate Jinhsi?
Alannah: The Magistrate does hold significant authority, but she's not the one.
 The boxing gym owner Yuanwu?
Alannah: Yuanwu has his own boxing gym and is secretly the proprietor of the Liuxian Teahouse. A good guess.
Alannah: Still not the richest in Jinzhou even with all of that, though.
 Is it... me?
Alannah: Hahaha. So you're saying you've got a hefty saving, huh? But don't feel disappointed when I reveal the real name.
Alannah: I heard a rumor that this mysterious tycoon dwells in this Gourmet Street...
 Wait, you mean Mahe?
Alannah: Exactly! Mahe's that No.1 rich guy in Jinzhou's urban legends!
Alannah: Why don't we pay him a visit? A documentary showcasing Jinzhou's richest person will definitely catch the attention of those in Ragunna!

(Talk to Mahe)
 About the richest person in Jinzhou...
Alannah: Excuse me, Mr. Mahe. Do you mind revealing to us your net worth, please?
Mahe: I... What?
 Word is, you're Jinzhou's top rich.
 Impress us, richest person in Jinzhou.
Mahe: Well, see the shops and vendors on the street? They've all been around for decades, simple and honest.
Mahe: And I'm just another humble grocery store owner like them. How could I be the richest person in Jinzhou?
Alannah: I heard a guy say he couldn't even afford 10 eggs with his monthly salary at your place...
Mahe: Nonsense. If he's tight on money, I wouldn't mind giving him a few eggs for free.
Mahe: But going broke over a few eggs? That's just ridiculous.

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: Mahe says he's not the richest person in Jinzhou. Well...
 It seems so.
 Do we have a Plan B?
Alannah: Hmm... We'll just run with it and continue working on this merch.
Alannah: I know Mahe may just be an ordinary grocery shop owner, but making him the man who has Jinzhou's lifeline in the clutches should make for a more interesting story.
Alannah: Once I have all this information in fine print, it's gonna blow Ragunna away!
Later, by adding a few edits here and there, Alannah finishes compiling a printout called Mahe's Spicy Secret.
Alannah: Haha. The product testing survey just came back from Ragunna. Time to dive into the reviews.

(Talk to Alannah)
Survey Feedback: "A grocery shop owner is actually the richest guy? Yeah right." "A month's worth of salary for just 10 eggs? Life must be hard over at Jinzhou."...
Alannah: I guess the Ragunnesi don't really buy into mysteries of family secrets...
 Gotta sell with honesty.
 We should try something else.
Alannah: I'm sorry... Unreasonable sales target shouldn't be our excuse for bad journalism.
Alannah: As a member of the Pioneer Association, I should always strive to uphold honesty and integrity in everything I create.
Alannah: Thanks for reminding me that, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! I should go work on my next idea!
(Quest Complete)
