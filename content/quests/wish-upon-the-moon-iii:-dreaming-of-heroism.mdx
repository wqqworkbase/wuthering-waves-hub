Steps[]

- Head to the "Wishing Stall" and continue the preparations

- Build "Thus Says the Moon"

- Build "Fireworks Shop"

- Build "Riddle Twiddle"

- Decode the third Wish Tag with Patty

- Follow the noise

- Go to the secret base

- Search for clues with the Sensor Module

- Save the trapped kid

- Defeat the Tacet Discords

- Talk to Xiangli Yao
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is limited.

- When completing the "Head to the 'Wishing Stall' and continue the preparations" step, the quest name changes from Festival Festival Preparations III to Decode corrupted wishes: III.

- When completing the "Decode the third Wish Tag with Patty" step, the quest name changes to Wish Upon the Moon III: Dreaming of Heroism.

- Completing the quest awards the player the Rover's Journey trophy What Is a Hero and unlocks Xiangli Yao as a Lodge Wishes companion in the By Moon's Grace limited time event.
