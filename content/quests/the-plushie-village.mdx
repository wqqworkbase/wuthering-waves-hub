Dialogue[]

Unknown Contact (Contacting): This message is from the Pioneer Association to all members currently exploring the Rinascita region.
Unknown Contact (Contacting): Marino, one of our Association photographers, is asking for help from any members with strong combat skills. You can find the location on the map marked for your reference.
Rover: (Looks like someone needs a hand. I should go take a look when I'm around.)
 Quest DescriptionGuided by the Pioneer Association, you arrive at an intriguing place known as the Plushie Villaae.
(Talk to Marino)
???: You look familiar... Are you THE Rover? The great hero who singlehandedly saved Jinzhou?
 No one can do that singlehandedly.
???: Oh, sorry, you're right. Mr. Fulmine also taught me that "It's the glimmer of each and every soul that forges the glories in history."
 In the flesh.
???: That's awesome! I'm in need of a savior right now.
Marino: I'm Marino, an intern photographer of the Pioneer Association. Well, this may seem a bit too much to ask from a stranger, but could you lend me a hand? Only a hero like you can handle this!
 I seem to have heard the line pretty often lately...
 What happened exactly?
Marino: It's a bit complicated, but you can start by touching those Plushies over there to get a better sense of the situation.

(Talk to Marino)
Rover: These Plushies need Sonance Caskets?
Marino: Yes. By using the Sonance Caskets, the morph painting can stabilize the masterless Echoes here with its Sonoro Sphere, keeping them from fading or becoming hostile.
Marino: Mr. Fulmine and I have been grabbing as many Caskets as we can, but it's still not enough to power the painting fully. That's why we need your help.
 That's a lot of work to do.
 What's in it for me?
Marino: We will definitely make it worth your while.
Marino: Every time the painting opens its Sonoro Sphere, the Plushies take some resources from it to keep the village going.
Marino: In fact, there's still a ton of treasure left in the Sonoro Sphere. I'm sure the Plushies would be happy to haul out of a few pieces as a reward for you!
Rover: Fair enough. I have no use for Sonance Caskets anyway.
Marino: Thank you so much!
Marino: We're counting on you to collect more Sonance Caskets to power the painting. I'll also keep an eye out for any Echo weirdness and let you know if something comes up.
(Quest Complete)
