Steps[]

- Talk to Maqi to find out about today's game scenario.

- Complete today's scenario

- (Optional) Find out about the Burning Jade from Xieyang

- Give the Burning Jade to Maqi
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionMaqi is trying to contact you, and it appears to be related to the Pioneer Association's ambitious project, "Second Coming of Solaris"...

(Talk to Maqi)
 Today's scenario...
Maqi: Let me explain our job today.
Maqi: Coming to the end of this chapter, the protagonist engaged in a showdown with the Crownless.
Maqi: It goes: "...We compromised to the overwhelming darkness... Sword and spear, wand and gem, all had shattered like broken pieces..."
Maqi: "We stared at the night, almost covered by the rising Crownless... Its gaze pieced like a razor..."
Maqi: "...We cannot retreat, even wielding the Burning Jade as our only weapon..."
Maqi: "...March till the end if you're lost in the wild. Rip the moon apart if it fails to shine."
 This makes my adrenaline race.
 I can't wait to see this in the game.
 You should add grand music here.
Maqi: It marks the climax of the first chapter. I have the same exciting feeling as you. I wonder what it would be like in our game.
Maqi: But with one issue remaining: What exactly is this Burning Jade??
Maqi: Though it appears in the previous chapters before, the writer has omitted the description of its shape and appearance.
Maqi: To reproduce the showdown, we're gonna need a reference for it.
Maqi: If you happen to come across some samples, feel free to drop by.

(Optional - Talk to Maqi)
 Give me some clues about the Burning Jade.
Maqi: If you ask me, there is a clue you can get from one book fan.
 May I ask for a name?
 Where is our book fan, then?
Maqi: His name is Xieyang, often seen around the Jinzhou Institute during the daytime.
Maqi: He has a reservoir of ideas for this book, generous in providing suggestions. But...
 But...?
Maqi: His words are usually saddled with riddles.
 Is he a difficult guy?
Maqi: No, he is easy to get along with. But his words are usually saddled with riddles...
Maqi: We all can tell his passion for Second Coming of Solaris, but that doesn't make our conversations easier.
Maqi: However, I have the feeling that you can understand him.
 I'll go find him.
 Well, I happen to speak the language of these geeks.
Maqi: Though I'm not sure if Riddler is the right word, you seem pretty confident in this.
Maqi: Then I'll entrust you to this.
(Optional - Talk to Xieyang)
 About the Burning Jade...
Xieyang: The Crownless's tyranny rules the failing moon...
Xieyang: ...while civilization stems from the silent Burning Jade...
 Impressive speech!
 I don't understand a word...
Xieyang: Finally! Someone reaches out to ask me, the most loyal fan of Second Coming of Solaris...
Xieyang: Only the expansive world in the book can match my incessant stream of creativity and overflowing talents for verses...
 What's this Burning Jade in your mind?
Xieyang: I know you'll come to me for this, from the moment you joined this project.
 How can you know?
Xieyang: Because it's the key to turn the tide in the incredible final showdown!
Xieyang: A precious of such eminence will not tolerate any plain mold in this world.
 Then where can I find a reference?
 Enlighten me, please.
Xieyang: Such nerve... Fine, I'll tell you. Only in the ever-changing Sonoro Sphere could you find a substantial copy for this Burning Jade.
 The Sonoro Sphere...
 You really know a thing or two!
Xieyang: But it's a piece of cake for a No.1 book fan. I've prepared what you want.
Xieyang: Now, I can give this precious item to you, if you wish.
Xieyang: But with one condition.
 What condition?
 Do tell me, please.
Xieyang: I want you to ask Maqi to... add my name to the Special Thanks list.
Xieyang: How can I remain anonymous as the No.1 fan of this book?
 Leave it to me
 Only the Pioneer Association can decide.
Xieyang: Just... do your best. Success only comes after you give it a go!
 I'll help.
Xieyang: Here, have it. And don't forget your promise!
(Obtain Impure Phlogiston x1)

(Talk to Maqi)
 I'm turning in the Burning Jade.
(Submit Burning Jade).
Maqi: I see. It matches the image of the Burning Jade in my head.
Maqi: I'll deliver it to the dev team. They will need it for the last scene.
Some time passed while Maqi was away to hand over the item...
Maqi: All chapters of Tiancheng have been completed.
Maqi: The Pioneer Association would like to thank you for your continued support, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
 I love the design of the scenes.
Maqi: Same here.
 Hope it will release soon.
Maqi: Same here. I'll let you know if we have any progress.
Maqi: By the way, Mr. Karl has heard stories about this. He would like to meet you if possible.
 It's my honor.
Maqi: Mr. Karl also expressed his appreciation for your help in the game development.
 I guess he's prepared a special gift for me.
Maqi: I can't tell. You could ask him when you meet him.
Maqi: Anyway, our job is done here. But...
 But...?
Maqi: The references you brought in made our developers' lives much easier. They would like to come to you for more of this kind of favors. What would you say?
 Sure.
 As long as you pay.
Maqi: No worries. The Pioneer Association will pay you in full if you agree to help.
Maqi: Well, until we meet again, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)!
(Quest Complete)
