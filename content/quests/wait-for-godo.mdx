Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionTake Midnight Rangers Godo's place on guard duty

(Talk to Dedi)
Dedi: Did you see a roaming soldier on your way here?
Dedi: I just caught an alert that signals abnormal energy fluctuations at the embankment. Tacet Discords might appear anytime.
Dedi: Godo was supposed to be manning the watch tower on the other side, but he's nowhere to be found.
Dedi: Can you do me a favor and take his place for now?
 Reluctantly agree.
 Politely decline.

(Climb the watchtower and stand guard)
Mysterious Sound: As the sun beats down and the ocean breeze lulls you, your eyes can't help but be drawn to the massive shadow looming over the beach. It beckons to you, calling for your attention and submission&#8212;Those man-made, sinister shadows will dance for you. You need only waive the scepter in your hand.
 Scepter?
Mysterious Sound: "Yes, you remember what those shadows looked like. The memories are crystal clear. They flow in and out of your mind like waves, yet they leave you with nothing to keep."
 Waves?
Mysterious Sound: "Yes... like waves. Time and tears swirl together in an eternal dance. I admit, you know everything about the past and future. Yet here you are, shipwrecked in this era, stumbling like a newborn. Welcome to this era, Rover, as you awaken again... that's my warm greeting for you."
Mysterious Sound: Heed my advice: Don't touch the truth yet. This is for your own good.
