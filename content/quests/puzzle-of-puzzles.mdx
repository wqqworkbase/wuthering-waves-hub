Steps[]

- Talk to Changli

- Arrive at the platform Xianchen mentioned

- Investigate the clues of the puzzle

- Follow the instructions and solve the puzzle

- Talk to Changli for instructions

- Find the treasure

- Return to the stall and talk to Xianchen
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is time limited.

- Completing the quest unlocks Changli as a Lodge Wishes companion in the By Moon's Grace limited time event.
