Steps[]

- Head to the Pioneer Asssiociation

- Ask Alannah about the plan for day 1

- Photograph the great tree in Taoyuan Vale for "Huanglong From Above"

- Give the photo to Alannah

- Ask Alannah about the reviews on "Huanglong From Above"
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionAlannah from the Pioneer Association is looking for a reliable consultant.

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: T-Three million?!
Alannah: But Ragunna doesn't even have that population.
Alannah: That's 3 copies each Ragunnesi? I doubt they sell as much pizza over there...
 Excuse me, hi...
 I heard you were hiring a consultant.
(Upon hearing your voice, Alannah turns her back and lowers her voice.)
Alannah: Understood. I'll come up with a plan. I'll report once I've made progress, sir!
Alannah: *sigh*...
Alannah: What am I gonna do...
Alannah: Oh, sorry about that. Haven't introduced myself yet. My name's Alannah, I'm with the Product Development Team at the Pioneer Association.
Alannah: You know, all the merchandise and souvenirs you get from the Association are our handiwork!
 What was that call about?
Alannah: Oh, that... I'm recently brought onboard a merch project from our Ragunna division in Rinascita.
Alannah: And our manager just gave me a sales target of three million copies.
 Is that why you're hiring a consultant?
Alannah: Yes, it'll be one heck of a job.
Alannah: If it's the release of something as highly anticipated as the Second Coming of Solaris, sure... But some average merch can never hit those sales figures.
Alannah: Still, I gotta figure something out.
Alannah: Everyone thinks I'm never gonna pull it off. But maybe, just maybe, there's a chance I might make the sale.
Alannah: From what I know, information in and out of Ragunna is pretty limited, so it takes time for people there to learn what's happening on the outside.
Alannah: I thought they might find designs from outside their culture intriguing, so I came to Jinzhou for inspiration.
Alannah: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), Ms. Maqi told me you know the ins and outs of Jinzhou. Would you like to be my consultant?
 If the pay is good, sure.
 I might have to reconsider.
Alannah: It'll be an uphill battle, but on the plus side they gave me a huge budget, so I'll definitely make it worth your while.
Alannah: Then I'll start brainstorming some plans myself. Come find me when you're ready!

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: Oh, yeah. They'll be flying off the shelves!
 I'm listening.
 What's the big plan?
Alannah: You see, Rinascita is comprised mostly of islands out at sea, with Ragunna City on one of the isles.
Alannah: On the other hand, Huanglong is made up of large areas of land, as far as the eye can see!
Alannah: I'm sure most people in Ragunna have yet to ever lay eyes on some extraordinary mountains and rivers like the ones in Jinzhou.
 So you're saying...
Alannah: Let's make good use of that. I shall name this set of merch Huanglong From Above!
Alannah: Whaddya say, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)? Time to capture Jinzhou in all its glory from high up in the skies, and give the Ragunnesi something that will knock their socks off!

(Talk to Alannah)
Alannah: They're gorgeous, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! I'd even recommend you for an exclusive photographer with the Association!
Alannah: Our product testing survey from Ragunna just came back. Let's see what they have to say.

(Talk to Alannah)
Survey Feedback: "Boring image spam." "Lacks the unique angles of humanist photography and the diverse styles of oil paintings."...
Alannah: Well, that's unexpected. Does this mean the Ragunnesi prefer art galleries over photos to enjoy their landscapes?
 At least we know their taste now.
 At least these photos were well received in Jinzhou.
Alannah: Yes, you're right! We're heading in the right direction towards success.
Alannah: All we need to do is learn from our mistakes, and the next one will be bound to impress!
Alannah: Since the locals really like them, I'll save up these photos of Huanglong From Above and pass them out in the office for everyone to enjoy.
Alannah: Let's come up with something else tomorrow, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)!
(Quest Complete)
