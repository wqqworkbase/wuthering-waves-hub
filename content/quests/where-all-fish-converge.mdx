Summary[]

The Tidebreaker has indeed been wandering in Serene Bay. After the lighthouse was relit, the beast swam towards a small island covered in storm...

Steps[]

- You have obtained the ship blueprint. Go ask Queequeg about ship repairs

- Deliver the required materials to continue Pequod's repairs

- Collect 100 Palm Logs

- Collect 2,000 Riccioli Fishery Coins

- Retrieve "Pequod Blueprint: Basic"

- Prepare for departure at the dock and head to Serene Bay

- Travel to Serene Bay

- Defeat the Tacet Discords

- Locate the dock in Serene Bay and ask Planck for details

- Complete Planck's mission request

- Complete Priority Requests (0/4)

- Complete Priority Request "Ahab's Request: Finding the Keyname in event "Find the Key"&#32;&#91;sic&#93;"

- Complete Priority Request "Ahab's Request: A Feather?name in event "Feather?"&#32;&#91;sic&#93;"

- Complete Priority Request "Ahab's Request: Into the Underworldname in event "Meet the Phantom"&#32;&#91;sic&#93;"

- Complete Priority Request "Ahab's Request: Setting the Trapname in event "Rainbow Rush"&#32;&#91;sic&#93;"

- Set sail and catch Titanium Mackerel at the given location

- Light up the lighthouse in Serene Bay
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing "Search for Starbuck" step awards  "Sweet Puff".

- Completing "Travel to Serene Bay" step awards Rover's Journey II trophy Tormented Prophet.

- Completing "Set sail and catch Titanium Mackerel at the given location" step awards  Titanium Mackerel and Wilderness Calls trophy Mackerel Gear.

- Completing the quest awards  Pequod Blueprint: Advanced.
