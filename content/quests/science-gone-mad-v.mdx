Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionRecently, Milo has started his research project, and he wonders what you might make of his ideas...

(Talk to Milo)
Milo: Oh, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! What a coincidence, here to check my new expirement?
 What experiment?
Milo: I've been trying to build a "plant signal transmission model" to see how potted plants react to different acoustics.
Milo: I jest played sounds to three plant samples. Guess which pot reacted the most?
 The one listening to classical music.
Milo: Hmm... That's what we thought, too. But it didn't give any reaction at all. It's completely quiet, might even be asleep.
Milo: Conclusion: The potted plant may lack interest in fine art.
 The one listening to heavy rock.
Milo: That's right! That one gave a strong reaction!
Milo: We observed it swaying along with the music—its leaves opened and closed three times their normal rate. It seemed to be thoroughly enjoying it.
Milo: You could say that's its own kind of "rocking out."
 The one listening to Campus radio.
Milo: ...Plant's reaction to that is weird.
Milo: Every time the end-of-class bell sounded, it slowly leaned toward the window. I suspect it's trying to escape reality.
Milo: A physiological, world-weary response. Worth further study.
Milo: See? Pretty interesting, right, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)? I like to think the plants are enjoying their own little campus lives, too.
Milo: Drop by the AnomSci Group whenever you have time, maybe it'll give you some new inspiration.
Sounds like a good idea.
If I get the chance, I will.
Milo: Sure. You're always welcome.
