Gameplay notes[]

- This quest has two parts.

- This quest unlocks the Phantom: Kerasaur

- Listen to the Illusions award Exploration: Rinascita trophy Yesterday's Battle Marks.

- Defeating the Phantom awards the Exploration: Rinascita trophy Fading Maroon

- Completing the quest awards the World in All Its Variety I trophy A Gladiator Decided to Die.
