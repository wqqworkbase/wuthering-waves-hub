Steps[]

- Talk to Shilang

- Find the rift and investigate

- Investigate "Breath Rift"

- Interact with the Remnant Key

- Inform Shilang about the situation in the rift
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is limited.

- Completing the quest will unlock access to the Alloy Smelt event.
