Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou meet Xuanyin again, while the sounds of frog surround you...

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: Hey, Player? What brought you here?
 Just wandering around.
Xuanyin: You always manage to stumble upon important places.
Xuanyin: This is a holy dating spot for couples. It's said that if couples come to this pond and make a wish to the sky while the frogs croak, they can stick by each other as long as they live.
 Doesn't make sense to me.
Xuanyin: This grassy slope and pond are indeed nothing special. The key, however, lies in the call of the Black-Striped Frogs.
Black-Striped Frog: Alwa, alwa, alwa.
 ...
Xuanyin: Doesn't that sound like "always"? Couples think that means "always and forever", and wish this will bless their love.
Xuanyin: They even give the Black-Striped Frogs a nickname, "Alwa Frog".
Xuanyin: Well, it's just couples project their own expectations on an animal.
 It's a cultural thing.
Xuanyin: Well, for me, it's more like a faith-shall-work-wonders thing.
Xuanyin: Anyway, we're taking this a little too far. Let's install the module first.
Xuanyin loaded the Frog Language Translation Module onto your Pangu Terminal.
Black-Striped Frog (Translation): Run, run, run!
Xuanyin: ...
 ...

Zhongwan: Oh no, someone's already here.
Pushen: Should we go somewhere else?
Xuanyin: Sorry, we're just here for an experiment. We'll leave right away.
Pushen: Wait you're... Xuanyin? I'm Pushen from the Huaxu Academy. I heard from Qiaomu that you're researching animal language translation modules. Are you here for the Black-Striped Frogs?
Xuanyin: Oh, yes!
Pushen: Do you know what they want to say to us?
Xuanyin: (Looks at you with an inquiring gaze.)
 Nods
Xuanyin: Actually, they were disturbed by humans and were signaling warning calls to their kind, urging them to escape.
Pushen: Well, in that case, I think we'd better find another place for our date. What do you say?
Zhongwan: Yeah. Thinking from their perspective, I would feel anxious too. Let's go.
Pushen: Okay then...
The original romantic atmosphere was spoiled, and the couple left disappointed.
 Shakes head.
Xuanyin: My apologies! They said, "Good luck with your marriage!"
Pushen: Wow, thank you!
Zhongwan: It's our first date though...
Xuanyin saved the awkward atmosphere, making it lively once again.

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: What a dramatic night. Sometimes, the truth can hurt people.
 That's right.
Xuanyin: Anyway, I need to go back. See you next time!
(Quest Complete)
