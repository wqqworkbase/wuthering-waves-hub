Glossary[]

- Startorch Academy - Startorch Academy is a comprehensive research facility that doesn't belong to any country, built with the goal of collaborative scientific research. It is an academic metroplis driven solely by educational and research pursuits. Compared to its official title of "Startorch Academy," the people of Solaris prefer to call it the "Resonator Academy."

- Spacetrek Collective - An international scientific organization jointly established by various nations and organizations of Solaris. It oversees the research, development, and construction work in Lahai-Roi. It was the Spacetrek Collective that built the Research Institute and the Startorch Academy in the vast space beneath the frostlands.

- Void Storm - The regional Waveworn Phenomenon of Lahai-Roi that disrupts the frequencies of anything around it. It happens in a somewhat cyclical pattern.

- Waveworn Phenomena - The Lament's power causes destructive waves across the land of Solaris. Manifestations like the "Tacet Fields," "Tacet Discord Outbreaks," and the enigmatic "Tacetite" are part of what is collectively known as the "Waveworn Phenomena."
