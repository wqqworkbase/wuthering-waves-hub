Summary[]

Your attempts to contact Cartethyia go unanswered. The only clue left is a faint flicker of her energy's explosion, pointing towards a cluster of islands opposite Ragunna.

Since arriving in Septimont, Abby has remained silent within your Tacet Mark. You try to sense Cartethyia but are confronted with an unbelievable vision&#8212;Cartethyia facing a half-human, half-lion creature. Following her tracks, you head toward a sculpture beneath a stone wall where you find the Hero's Legacy Armor.

In the first village you reach in Septimont, you find no leads on Cartethyia. However, you do learn that the half-human, half-lion Guardian you saw, the mighty Arsinosa, will appear in the city's Agon of Champions. Held every four years, it is the biggest Gladiatorial event in Septimont. The match will be held at the Colosseum on Septimont's hilltop, and you plan to continue your investigation there. You encounter a young Taoist girl named Buling, who claims to be from Mengzhou. She seems to have sought you out deliberately, but after a few cryptic exchanges, she vanishes without a trace.

You meet a young Gladiator named Lupa. Once part of a Gladiator team, she left after learning about her House's involvement in match-fixing. She recognizes your strength and invites you to team up. You sense a faint trace of Cartethyia's frequency on Lupa, but she denies ever meeting her. You and Lupa register a new team and ready for your first battle together.

You and Lupa secure your first victory. But as her supporters and fans swarm you, the weight of her fame as a Gladiator comes crashing down. To avoid lingering too long, Lupa laughs and takes your hand, pulling you away from the arena. Lupa's former manager, Cartur, waits for her by the road, but it's clear Lupa has no intention of returning to fight for her patron House. The power struggles between Septimont's noble Houses simmer beneath the surface as the Great Agon unfolds.

After your victory in the second Agon, you sense Cartethyia's presence by the waters of Septimont, just as you notice the strange energy affecting Septimont's Echoes. Meanwhile, Lupa's departure from House Silva sparks retaliatory rumors, but she appears unaffected, focusing on helping your search for Cartethyia. You arrive at the waters where you felt Cartethyia's presence but still find no trace of her. After coming to Septimont, you learn from Lupa just how important the arena is to the city. It can elevate someone to the heights of power or cast them into obscurity.

Meanwhile, in a side of the city unseen, Cartethyia walks beneath the shadowed skies of Septimont and meets the Nameless One.

You and Lupa go to a restaurant, drawing the stares of many. She mentions a former Gladiator she admires, Mya, a true competitor. However, Mya's career was cut short during the Great Agon two decades ago when she vanished under mysterious circumstances. Lupa books you into her lifetime suite. Now, no matter when you return to Septimont, you will always have a place to stay. She confides her worry: Is the joy she finds in the arena due to her passion for it, or because of the favor it brings her? As she says goodnight and leaves, you see her suddenly transform into another young woman. It lasts only a moment, like a fleeting illusion.

In your dreams, you see Phrolova floating in the air with pages swirling around her. She gives you a play that contains an account of the future. You don't believe her, but she seems indifferent. Phrolova tells you that Lupa will be the one to trigger the Dark Tide's surge in Septimont, and it will happen within three days.

The next morning, you and Lupa face House Silva in the semi-finals. Ephor Augusta gives a stirring opening speech. Now it's time to prove that true victory should be won by strength alone. During the fight, Lupa's weapon turn against you as you feel a frequency similar to that of the Dark Tide in the Inverted Tower, unbeknownst to her. Lupa announces to Ephor Augusta that her weapon was tampered with, but House Silva denies the accusation. In the standoff, Iuno, a Priestess of Septimont, declares House Silva guilty. Augusta presents evidence of Julia's secret dealings, and House Silva is expelled from Septimont.

After handling Cartur's invitation to become her manager, Lupa rests with you in the lounge. During this time, you ask her about Ephor Augusta and Priestess Iuno. The former, hailing from humble origins, has risen to power, while the latter is rumored to possess a gift for prophecy. After the semi-finals, Lupa blocks the journalists crowding around you, telling you to rest and that she'll find you later.

As you consider the connection between Lupa and the strange happenings in Septimont, Buling appears once more. She tells you that she also senses the oddities around but cannot find a way to break the deadlock. She hands your a copper mirror. As you examine it, the scene shifts to reveal a conversation that occured two decades ago during the Great Agon. Two Septimontians discuss a shadowy underground bet involving Gladiator Mya. In the mirror, Mya's manager and her instructor are seen exploiting her, indifferent to her unraveling mental state. You also see Septimontian nobles planning to use Mya's matches as a cover to incite chaos and bloodshed to quietly solve the Senate's problems. In the mirror, Mya is seen trembling, sobbing, and digging deep to continue to fight as a Gladiator. It is unclear whether the visions in the copper mirror are reality or illusion. However, when you meet Lupa again you once more glimpse her transform into that same strange young woman. This time, you recognize her as Mya. You return to the hotel with Lupa and ask her about Mya. She shares everything she knowns, including the glamorous and dark aspects of Gladiating in Septimont.

In a side of the city unseen, Cartethyia once visited this very same room with the Nameless One.

The day of the final, Lupa tells you that before the new Ephor took office, Septimont's Gladiator matches were far bloodier and more brutal. "The Bloody Agon," it was called. As you mull over the term, you prepare to enter the finals with Lupa. During the fight, the real world and the anomalous one overlapped as you end up participating in the Bloody Agon. As the anomaly ends, you and Lupa emerge victorious, but the strange events you just experienced go unnoticed by the crowds.

Meanwhile, in an unknown place, Cartethyia steps into the same arena, her swords aimed at the playwright Cristoforo. You and Cartethyia stand together, unknowingly united in the light and shadow of glory, fighting side by side in a battle that no one is aware of. The story is far from over.

Glossary[]

- Capitoline Hill - Septimont's central city&#8212;home to Gryphon's Fortress and the stage of the Great Agon.

- Copper Mirror - In the tales of Huanglong, mirrors are said to reveal hidden truths. And this one appears to show things unseen...

- Echo Gladiating - Septimont's signature sport, where Echoes battle beside Gladiators. Victory in the arena hinges on their cooperation.

- Ephor - The ruler of Septimont. Ephors wield absolute power, as only the strong are deemed worthy to lead.

- Guardian Echo - The Lioness of Glory, Arsinosa. Born of battle cries and laughter, fused into the stone form of a mighty half-human, half-lion Echo. Since her genesis, she has stood watch over the City of Septimont.

- Gladiator - Participants in Gladiator matches. They are renowned for their strength and battle prowess and driven by an unyielding thirst for victory and glory.

- Great Agon - The largest and most influential Gladiator event in Septimont, as well as a key economic pillar of the city.

- Heartpour - An energy drink endorsed by Lupa.

- Hero's Legacy Armor - An Echo formed from the Reverberations of a fallen hero, fused into his armor. It longs to relive the thrill of glorious battle.

- House Silva - The House Lupa fights for. One of Septimont's founding bloodlines, House Silva's roots once ran deep in the city's spire of influence. But with the rise of the new Ephor, such past glories have begun to wane.

- Mengzhou - One of the six major cities in Huanglong, renowned for its abundant waterways, thriving greenery, and enchanting landscapes. There, commerce and Taoism thrive and coexist in perfect harmony.

- Play - A play script given by Phrolova. Every actor has their assigned role, and all stories unfold exactly as written, unless...

- Priestess - Leaders of Septimont's temple who can foretell fate. They listen to the strings of destiny at a personal cost and offer guidance to those who seek answers.

- Senate - The governing body of Septimont, tasked with advising the Ephor and wielding authority in key administrative matters. It also exercises power over the appointment and dismissal of public officials.

- Solis - A popular tavern situated in the commoner neighborhood, famous for its signature drink "Solis." Gladiators often spar there, with the loser footing the bill.

- Team - The current Ephor reformed the Great Agon into a two-on-two competition. Now, gladiators must find a trusted partner to win the championship together.

- Tidal Blight - Scars of the Dark Tide's touch. It may appear as discoloration, calcification, or even coral-like growths, depending on what has been corrupted.
