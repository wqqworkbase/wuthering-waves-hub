Gameplay notes[]

- This quest begins after repairing any one of the five lighthouses.

- Completing this quest awards the Exploration: Huanglong trophy May the Beacons Guide Us.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionLocate five lighthouses in Huanglong, collect information, and help the historian to uncover the truth

(After repairing any lighthouse)
???: Hello? Can anyone hear me?

???: A connection? Outstanding!
Historian: I'm a historian of the Jinzhou Imperial Academy. I am currently conducting a study on these old lighthouses scattered all over Jinzhou. There must be many fascinating stories stored inside them.
Historian: Yet I have been unsuccessful in deciphering the information inside the lighthouses' consoles... However, I did manage to discover that the console will send out a certain alarm signal when its system is being interfered with.
Historian: I accidentally connected to your Pangu Terminal while I was investigating these signals. It appears that your Terminal's ID was transmitted to me as an intruder alert.

Historian: Ahh, yes. I really wish to know the stories surrounding these lighthouses in detail. Do come find me in Jinzhou city when you have the time, and we'll discuss it in person.

Historian: Hello, hello! You must be the one who solved the mysteries of the lighthouses.
 That's me.
Historian: Please, describe to me your findings within the consoles. I am truly intrigued.
You tell the historian about what you found inside the consoles.
Historian: Hmm... I didn't expect such stories to occurred...

Historian: Obtaining the records from all of the lighthouses' consoles may just reveal the whereabouts of the "Stingray."

 Doesn't matter. I'm just here for the history part.
Historian: Haha. Then I shall entrust the task to you!

(Talk to the historian again, optional)
Historian: How did it go? Did you find anything?
Historian: Do come back to me after you have found all five lighthouses. I await your successful return!

Head to Jinzhou[]

(After repairing all five lighthouses)
Historian: Hello Rover, do you still remember me? I'm a historian from the Imperial Academy.
Historian: It appears you have activated all five lighthouses. I'm sure you must have learned a great deal about the Stingray.
Historian: Please, come back to the city and tell me all about them when you have the time to spare.
Historian: Maybe this time, we can finally solve this unknown piece of history!

(Talk to the Historian)
Historian: Greetings, Rover. Did you find the remaining lighthouses?
You tell the historian what you found inside the consoles.
Historian: Is that so? You managed to find information from all five lighthouse consoles you say...!
Historian: Your passion for history is much greater than that of my own pupils. This brings tears to my eyes.
 Are we now able to find the location of the Stingray?
Historian: Of course, this information is more than adequate for me to calculate the Stingray's whereabouts. But there is something strange about this...
 What is it? You can't find it?
Historian: No. It's just that it is not worth searching for it anymore.
Historian: According to those coordinates, I can say with confidence that this Stingray is the same ship that we salvaged near the harbor of the Whining Aix's Mire a few years prior.
Historian: Inside the wreckage, we found some of the crew's logs, although broken into pieces, we still managed to link them with the stories found in the consoles.
 So were Moak and his crew all safe?
Historian: Unfortunately, we also discovered six bodies inside... Based on the information we have, they belong to Moak and his crew members.
 How could this be?
Historian: Further investigations found that the deaths of the other five members date back even further than the time when Moak made his first recording in the lighthouse.
Historian: In other words, Moak had been sailing on the sea alone all this time, and his crew members had long since fallen victim to the TDs...
Historian: Experiencing such losses, Moak refused to accept such an ending. So he fell into a fantasy, that he and his crew members all found the lighthouses one by one, found their own homes, and left the Stingray safely.
Historian: In the end, only he himself completed the voyage and forever laid in that seabed with the Stingray.
 Are you sure this is what truly happened...?
Historian: I cannot make any guarantees, as these are just my own speculations. Yet, unfolding forgotten stories, is what makes history fascinating.
Historian: No matter the case, I'm very grateful to you for letting me witness the entire story in its entirety. You deserve these rewards for your passion for history.
(Quest Complete)
