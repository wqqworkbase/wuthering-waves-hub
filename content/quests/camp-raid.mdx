Steps[]

- Head to the makeshift camp halfway up the mountain

- Inquire the team members about what happened

- Wait until 12:00 PM

- Ask Lingdi about the situation

- Head to the observation camp

- Rescue the "hostages" trapped in the camp

- Defeat the Exiles in the observation camp

- Defeat the Exiles at the elevated position in the camp

- Defeat the Exiles in the center of the camp

- Talk to Cunren
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest grants the Huanglong I trophy The Brave Wins When Paths Cross!
