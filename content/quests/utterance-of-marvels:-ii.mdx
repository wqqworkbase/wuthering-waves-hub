Steps[]

- Find a suitable Tacet Discord

- Defeat Stonewall Bracer

- Absorb the Reverberation and obtain an Echo

- Equip the Echo

- Defeat nearby TDs: (0/2)

- Return to Jinzhou

- (Optional) Activate Resonance Beacon

- Head to Jinzhou
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest will grant the Rover's Journey trophy From the New World and Resonator Chixia.
