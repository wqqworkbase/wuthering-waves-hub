Summary[]

Steps[]

- Find Bouton de Rose

- Complete Requests or update the Index to increase your Fame to Level 10 or above

- Find Ishmael in Roccioli Port

- Complete Requests or update the Index to increase your Fame to Level 12 or above

- Find Ishmael in Roccioli Port
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest awards the Rover's Journey II trophy Take Up the Mantle.
