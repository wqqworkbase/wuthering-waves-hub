Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionMaji's business is expanding rapidly. New errands just came in... An Echo needs help looking for someone?

(Talk to Maji)
Maji: Greetings, my dear friend! How wonderful to see you again.
 Likewise, my friend.
 You do sound like a real Ragunnesi now.
Maji: Haha, you flatter me. Unfortunately, today is my last day in Ragunna. But as people say: "There's always more to the journey!" There will be a next time. Life is long, and there's always something to look forward to.
Maji: Well, about Bakethoven, while it is a pity that the Averardo Vault declined its application...
Maji: ...It still wanted me to thank you and Zani! The Vault staff seemed to really enjoy its pizza, saying it was a hundred times better than the canteen food. Bakethoven felt that it had found its value.
 That's great news.
Maji: Right? Who says an Echo's worth is determined only by "its place in the Vault?" It's the same with life, really.
 Absolutely right.
 That's quite profound, Maji.
Maji: Haha, thank you! Just a sudden insight. But there's another good piece of news!
Maji: Thanks to Bakethoven's case, Lollo Logistics' reputation for helping Echoes has spread far and wide. That's why our order today comes from another Echo, one who's looking for a little girl named Dora.
Maji: I've heard she's been spotted in the city. You might want to start there. I'm sure there's no one better to handle this final order than you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)!

(Talk to Dora)
Dora: So tired… but I haven't found it. No, can't give up, can't go home...
 Hey there, are you Dora?
Dora: W-who are you!
Rover: Don't worry, I won't hurt you. I was asked by an Echo to find you.
Dora: An Echo? C-could it be…?
Dora's eyes widen. Before she can say anything else, a small Tubpup runs over with a loud bark!
Dora: …Dumdum! Where have you been? I've been looking for you everywhere… Where did you go…
The Tubpup, Dumdum, clumsily licks the tears from the little girl's face, whimpering anxiously.
Rover: So it was trying to find its way back to its owner… and so secretly followed me the whole way?
Dora: Thank you, thank you so much! Sorry I was unfriendly earlier, (&#8205;&#8205;sirText for male Rover&#8205;/&#8205;missText for female Rover&#8205;&#8205;). Thank you for helping me find Dumdum… Dumdum, you should thank this (&#8205;&#8205;gentlemanText for male Rover&#8205;/&#8205;ladyText for female Rover&#8205;&#8205;)…
Dumdum: Woof!
 You're welcome, just be sure to take good care of your pup from now on.
 Thank you, Dumdum.
Dora: I'll take Dumdum home now. Mother has been so worried… I hope you have a wonderful day, (&#8205;&#8205;sirText for male Rover&#8205;/&#8205;missText for female Rover&#8205;&#8205;)!
 Be careful.

(Talk to Maji)
Maji: Welcome back, my dear friend!
 Mission completed.
Maji: I've already heard the news! You reunited the Tubpup with its owner! A feat worthy of praise. Feels like we've done something good again.
Maji: Well, it's almost time for me to head back. Before I leave Ragunna, I want to know what you think of our Lollo Errand campaign.
 It's fun.
 It's profitable.
Maji: Ha, at one point it didn't even feel like an errand service anymore. But since everything worked out, all is well! This trip has really given me an appreciation for this Land of Echoes. Quite the fascinating place, wouldn't you say?
 It is, indeed.
Maji: Your efforts never go unnoticed. It's always a pleasure working with you. I look forward to whatever adventure awaits us next!
(Quest Complete)
