Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionBeside a mysterious gate stands a girl who has accidentally wandered through it—Dorothy. She seeks your help to defeat the monsters lurking behind the gate. 
In this challenge, you can summon "Dorothy's Companions" as Echoes to assist you in the battle, amplifying your dominance in the battle. 
You may complete this task with a friend.

(Say hello)
"Dorothy": A-are you here to help "Dorothy"?
 What happened here?
"Dorothy": I... I'm not sure.
"Dorothy": "Dorothy" can't find the way home... I saw Mr. White Cat and wanted to pet it. I chased after it, and before I realized, I had walked through that gate and ended up here.
"Dorothy": And Mr. White Cat was no where to be found... I wanted to go back, but scary monsters started showing up...
 That sounds like the gate to the Somnoire...
 You shouldn't go through unfamiliar gates.
"Dorothy": I wanna go home...
"Dorothy": But not even Dorothy's friend can beat those monsters.

 Really?
