Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionIt's time to fulfill your promise to Faro. Set up a training session to get him started.

Faro: So, today's training's about breaking rocks? I remember I once heard from a Pioneer Association member that...
Faro: Breaking rocks with your chest is quite popular somewhere outside of Rinascita.
 I think you might have misunderstood.
Faro: Haha,  you're right. It's impossible for an ordinary person to do that!
 Well then, today's training is "breaking rocks with your chest."
Faro: What? Really? But that sounds impossible!
Rover: Alright, chit-chat's over. Today, we simply focus on fundamental strength training. We'll be shattering the rocks with our weapons.
Faro: Just like how you got the Sword in the Rock? I'm ready. We can start any time!
 No rush. I'll show you how it's done first.
Faro: Understood! I'll watch and learn carefully!

Faro: Wow! That was so smooth! A solid rock like this crumbled like a crispy cookie under your strike!
 Go practice.
Faro: Understood! I'll work hard and become a master of rock breaking!
 Now you're making me hungry.
Faro: Haha, after seeing your moves, I'll work hard and become a master of rock breaking!
 ...I recall you wanted to be a knight, right?
Under your guidance, Faro has fully trained his strength...

(If Chest Mimic appears)
Faro: What? A Tacet Discord in the stone?!
Rover: Looks like today's strength training just turned into a real combat session.
(After defeating the Chest Mimic)
Faro: Sorry, I messed up again. I didn't help at all and just had you protect me once more...
 Are you alright?
Faro: Yeah, just a few bruises. A good rest will heal them.
Faro: I need to practice more. I couldn't even catch your moves!
 That happens a lot for beginners.
 It's because I'm strong.
Faro: Just give me a little more time, and I'll prove myself to you!
(Quest Complete)
