Summary[]

You receive a letter from Carlotta inviting you to Averardo Vault as an intermediary for an escort mission.

After accepting the request, Zani gives you special security clearance prepared by the Montelli family upon your arrival at the vault.

You and Zani head off to meet the representative from the Order. To Zani's surprise, it is Phoebe, the Acolyte who joined your adventure before.
Upon your arrival at the Precious Metals and Artworks Depository, the vault's security mode activates, locking you in the area. You decide to head to the security room.
After finally arriving at the security room, Zani informs you that to leave the vault you must use your clearance to acces its deepest area.
She suggests you utilize the vault's cameras to avoid any possible defense systems.
After reviewing all the surveillance footage, you discover that a person named "Ash" might have taken the vault's security module. You'll need to find Ash to leave.
Zani mentions rumors about a "secret cache" inside the Averardo Vault and suspects it's what the thief is after.
You encounter a vault employee who claims to be Lily, but she can't remember why she is there. Although you find Lily's identity suspicious, for her own safety, you invite her to accompany you.
You find a note left by Ash for his family, mentioning a treasure deep within the Averardo Vault. You make the assumption that this is where the imposter obtained clues about the "secret cache."
You must get to "Ash" before he can trigger more defense systems.
Seeing there's no end to this chase, you decide to use the vault's defense systems to guide "Ash" towards the Echo train terminus and intercept him there.
You manage to find "Ash," but he jumps onto a train before you can grab him. Still, everything is going as planned. The train runs on a single rail, and the final stop is where you will catch him.
The Echo train experienced an accident and fell, but fortunately, no one was injured. While the train Echo seems to have sustained only a minor scratch, it is now unable to move...
You unexpectedly arrive at an unmarked area on the map. Here, Lily seems to recall some memories.
You defeat the Echo guards and corner "Ash." Lily now remembers everything: She, along with many researchers and artists, had been imprisoned by the Order and were deprived of their rights to research and create.
The Montelli family built a sanctuary deep within the vault to protect them. They lived their final moments here.
Lily's soul can finally rest in peace. Before leaving, she urges "Ash" to check the vault's secret cache.
Ash learns about his grandfather's fate and the truth about the vault's secret cache. He will now bear the consequences of his actions.
The path has been opened. It's time to leave.
You discover that the "secret cache" is actually a tree, and the item the Order requested the Montelli family to transport was a bomb that would react to the Reverberations in the vault.
