Summary[]

Passing through the mist, you encounter Aalto on patrol. After you recount your recent experience, he speculates that the blue figure you just saw might be The Shorekeeper from the Black Shores. However, the immediate priority is to upload the abnormal frequency to the Tethys System for analysis. Meanwhile, the Black Shores seem to be grappling with certain anomalies...

You hand over the data to the specialized robot at the Black Shores, which swiftly uploads it into the Tethys System. The merchant known as KU-Money, perhaps mistaking you for one of their own, rewards you with a substantial sum of money. However, despite its extensive knowledge of the Black Shores, the robot knowns nothing of Hoda's true identity.

Even Monica, the chief administrator of the Black Shores, has never heard of the name Hoda. Right after your discussion, another quake shakes the Black Shores, echoing the disaster in the Port City of Guixu. To safeguard the Tethys System, Monica instructs you to inspect the Modulation Device in the Greenhouse.

In the Greenhouse, you encounter two robots that recognize you as the lone Rover of Solaris. They speak of Blake Blooms as if you share a connection with them, but it appears that the Tethys System is somehow restraining them from revealing the truth of your identity...
