The Sailor's Treasure is a Side Quest that occurs in Riccioli Islands, Rinascita, [[{{{nation}}}]].

Steps[]

- Talk to Phoebe

- Ask Toretto about the treasure

- Look for the Gondola

- Go to the treasure spot

- User Sensor to search for treasure

- Investigate the treasure spot

- Bring the treasure map to Ahab
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest awards World in All its Variety I trophy Sailor's Legacy.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionNot all treasure of the sea is left behind by pirates, some could have been left by ordinary sailors... or a captain.

(Talk to Phoebe)
Phoebe: I heard a fascinating tale while we've been here, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). Would you care to listen?
 What sort of tale?
Phoebe: It's a fishermen's legend passed down for generations on this island. It speaks of Rinascita during the ancient Age of a Thousand Islands, when the Riccioli Islands were ruled by a sailor driven from his homeland.
Phoebe: The sailor possessed untold riches, yet no one knew the source of his fortune.
Phoebe: He used his vast wealth to build a citadel that brought peace and prosperity to the people of the land.
Phoebe: Just when they prepared to build a monument to honor him, he vanished without a trace.
Phoebe: Whispers claimed he sailed away to seek revenge for a dark past.
Phoebe: Before he departed, he left behind his most cherished treasures on these islands and set forth on a path of revenge, never to return.
Phoebe: I'm curious what kind of treasure a man like that would leave behind. It must've held great significance to him.
 Shall we set out to uncover it?
 I can hear the treasure calling to me!
Phoebe: I knew you'd be intrigued! Perhaps we could seek out someone well-versed in these legends? They may hold the key to our quest.

(Talk with Toretto)
Toretto: The acolyte? Back again so soon?
Phoebe: Mr. Toretto, we're here to learn more about the "Sailor's Treasure."
Toretto: Since when did the Order start caring about treasure?
Phoebe: Err. No, actually...
Toretto: Ah, spare me the details! Treasure hunters come and go, you lot are a dime a dozen. You'll end up empty-handed like the rest of 'em anyway.
 Maybe we'll find it. You never know.
Toretto: Awfully sure of yourself, aren't ya? You know how long ago the Age of a Thousand Islands was. Besides that old legend, there's not a sliver of evidence proving any of it is true.
Toretto: Without real clues, it's just a wild goose chase. The islands have already been turned upside down by those treasure hunters, and not a single coin has been found.
 Are there any other stories about the sailor?
Toretto: Other stories, you ask? Lemme see... There's the Wandering Gondola. Or there's the Master of the Fish King...
 Tell us about the Wandering Gondola.
Toretto: Legend has it a sailor arrived here in a Gondola. Even after he disappeared, it still drifts among these islands waiting for his return.
 Tell us about the Master of the Fish King.
Toretto: The Fish King is a notorious foe for any fisherman. Legend claims that once a sailor caught one in these very waters.
Toretto: No one has caught one since. They say a Fish King only heeds the call of someone as great as that sailor.
 I don't have any more to ask.
Toretto: All right, you already know what you need to know. Off you go, then. I'll be watching to see if you can find that treasure, ha!

Phoebe: What do you make of the clues, Rover?
Rover: Finding that Gondola might be a good start.
Phoebe: Good idea. The sailor may have vanished, but if his Gondola is still here we may be able to find some clues.

(Head to the designated location)
Phoebe: ...
Phoebe attempts to communicate with the Echo and decipher what it's saying.
Phoebe: They seem to know something! It may be trying to guide us in a certain direction... Shall we go take a look?
 Onwards!

(Investigate the treasure spot)
Rover: ...
Phoebe: I never expected to actually find something... This appears to be...
 Looks like a treasure map. A piece of one at least.
Phoebe: There looks to be something inscribed inside the chest. Hmm... It's hard to make out. The script is unfamiliar to me.
 Let's ask a reliable local for advice.

(Talk to Ahab, if Old Man and the Whale (Quest) is not completed)
Ahab: Ah, well met, friends! What's this ye've got there?
Phoebe shares the story of your treasure hunt with Ahab and shows him the chest with inscriptions.
Ahab: So, ye're telling me this here chest is from that legendary sailor?! Let me have a look.
Ahab: Hmm... there's a seal coverin' up these isncriptions. Can't get a good look at 'em. Heh, but watch me!
Ahab searches the surrounding area and returns with a unique plant. He squeezes it to extract its sap and applies it to the chest. Almost immediately, the inscriptions become clear.
Ahab: See that? It's an old trick we used to write hidden messages. Now we can read what it says.
Ahab: "No victory is instant. The truth must be pieced together bit by bit."
Phoebe: It seems like... we need to gather the rest of this map to find the treasure.
Phoebe: Let's keep our eyes peeled from now on, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Ahab: I wonder what that sailor left behind. Endless riches? Ancient relics?
Phoebe: Or perhaps... a Fish King?
Ahab: Huh? Hahaha. Never thought I'd hear that from an acolyte!
 Thanks, Ahab. We'll make sure to share the treasure with you once we find it.
Ahab: That's mighty generous, but I'm content with me lot in life. Whatever you find, keep it for yerselves!
(Talk to Ahab, if Old Man and the Whale (Quest) was completed)
Ahab's Notes: "Someone has surely found that legendary sailor's treasure by now."
Ahab's Notes: "If you can't see it, just try squeezing some waterweed juice onto it."
Ahab's Notes: "What? Any waterweed juice will do. Do you think a sailor has time to be choosy?"
Ahab's Notes: "No victory is instant. The truth must be pieced together bit by bit."
Ahab's Notes: "Search for all the scraps of that treasure map. Piece it together, and who knows what you might discover?"
(Quest Complete)
