Steps[]

- Chat with the elderly man

- Reach the first marked location

- Photograph the Holy Spire of Confluence in the distance

- Investigate the frequency

- Reach the second photography location

- Photograph the sights by the waterway

- Listen carefully to the reverberations of the past

- Reach the third photography location

- Photograph the dome roof from inside the Titular Repository

- Use Sensor to look for clues in the Titular Library

- Investigate the lingering frequency in the letters here

- Head to the area around Zargon Garden

- Listen carefully to the reverberations of the past

- Leave the garden and head towards the Holy Spire of Confluence

- (Optional) Free "Sloppy"

- Follow the waterway to the Holy Spire of Confluence

- Ride the Gondola

- Investigate the frequency here

- Investigate the second frequency

- Investigate the third frequency

- Investigate the last frequency

- Deliver the images to Andrea

Rosalie
