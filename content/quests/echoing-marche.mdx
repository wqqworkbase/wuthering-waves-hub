Steps[]

- Rest until the morning (7:00 - 10:00)

- Meet Chixia at Panhua's Restaurant

- Head to the city gate

- Arrive at the gate

- Get ready to leave the city

- Arrive at the gate

- (Optional) Learn about the Simulation: Resonance Potion

- Arrive at city gate

- Go to the Rearguards Base

- Ask the soldiers about Zhiyuan

- Use Sensor to confirm the patrol route

- Follow the patrol route to find the Detection Beacon

- Defeat the Exiles

- Repair the Detection Beacon

- Check the other Detection Beacon

- Use Levitator to burn off the thorns with Explosive Charge

- Repair the Detection Beacon

- Arrive at the Rearguards Base

- Ask the staffs about the current situation

- Look after the wounded soldiers in the Rearguards Base(0/2)

- Talk to the medics

- Take the elevator to reach the upper level

- Reach the upper level

- Ask the quartermaster about the situation

- Ask Jianxin about how it went for her

- Discuss your plans with Yangyang

- Stand your ground. Defeat all Tacet Discords
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the "Ask the quartermaster about the situation" step will award the player Mangosteen.

- Completing the quest will award the player Leaf affected by "Waveworn" and the Rover's Journey trophy Thus Spoke the Magistrate: Frontline Calls.
