Steps[]

- Talk to Dashbon in Jinzhou to start the quest

- Start the Overdash Challenge

- Reach the finish line and complete Overdash Challenge

- Complete the challenge at the north gate

- Reach the finish line and complete Overdash Challenge

- Complete the challenge at the south gate

- Reach the finish line and complete Overdash Challenge

- Report to Dashbon

- Complete the Overdash challenge - Enhanced Difficulty

- Reach the finish line and complete the Flying Eaves Dash

Gameplay notes[]

- You get supply chests after completing the challenges.

- First supply (Standard) chest gives;  Astrite ×10,  Union EXP ×20,  Shell Credit ×3000, and  Medium Energy Core ×2

- Second supply (Standard) chest gives;  Astrite ×10,  Union EXP ×20,  Shell Credit ×3000, and  Medium Energy Core ×2

- Third supply (Standard) chest gives;  Astrite ×10,  Union EXP ×20,  Shell Credit ×3000, and  Medium Energy Core ×1

- Last supply (Advanced) chest gives;  Astrite ×20,  Union EXP ×30,  Medium Sealed Tube, and  Shell Credit ×6000

- Completing this quest awards the Huanglong I trophy The Master of Overdashing.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionOwing to Dashbon's great efforts, you learned of the legends of the Hero of Leap. Follow his guidance and conquer the Overdash challenge and claim title of Overdash master!

(Talk to Dashbon)
Dashbon: In the land of the Daxia, I am practicing the art of overdashing, hoping to one day become a skilled swordsman.
 Are you not a Hunaglong person?
Dashbon: Indeed, I am a fan of the novel "The Seven Swords of Qingren" by Kuhai. I aspire to become a new generation of Kung Fu heroes! So I am learning from him, or rather, trying to talk to him.
 Why are you speaking like this?
Dashbon: I learned these words from the novel "The Seven Swords of Qingren" by Kuhai. This book is very popular in my homeland, and if I want to become a new generation of Kung Fu heroes, I must learn from all aspects!
 What is a Qixia with a sharp blade?
 Why do you need to Overdash and walk on walls?
Dashbon: Qingren heroes are the main characters in the novel "The Seven Swords of Qingren" and are mysterious swordsmen of unknown identity, known as Chiqiu.
Dashbon: Hidden in the darkness, swiftly overdashing, landing silently, and striking mercilessly.
Dashbon: This is the greatest characteristic of Qingren heroes. I am also following in their footsteps, starting from the most basic overdashing, and practicing supreme Kung Fu!
 Is this the reason you are here?
Dashbon: Not only that! Overdashing is also the most important competition in the global fan club of Qingren heroes.
Dashbon: Every year, at the beginning of the year, enthusiasts gather together to showcase their skills and compete for the title of Overdashing Master.
Dashbon: This world championship has been going on for many years and has expanded several times. It has become a famous street competition and a fashionable sport in my hometown.
Dashbon: I am not only a participant, but also a leader and promoter in the training field of Jinzhou. I work hard to become the Overdashing Master next year, one step closer to being a Qixia!
 What will happen if you become the Overdashing Master?
Dashbon: Ku Hai will tell the winner a Qixia story that no one else knows! It is a hidden Easter egg that belongs only to one person, and it is our greatest honor and motivation!
Dashbon: If you want to participate in the competition, you can sign up and train with me.
 How do you play this challenge?
Dashbon: First, as a hero, you rarely taste defeat with your mortal body. As followers, we need to complete the challenges relying solely on our own abilities, without borrowing any external powers.
Dashbon: Secondly, as a hero, with a sharp blade in hand, a weapon by your side, challengers can use grappling hooks and other external objects to assist in their swift movements and complete the challenges.
Dashbon: Third, as a hero, you can hide in high places, your presence unknown to others. Swift movements can only be made on the rooftops, challengers must not touch the ground and must not seek other paths.
Dashbon: These are the requirements for the art of overdashing.
 Let me give it a try.
Dashbon: Great! You have a unique talent. You must be a martial arts expert. You are our partner!
Dashbon: After the conversation, the challenge begins. Please reach the distant finish line as soon as possible within the rules!
 I'm not interested for now.
Dashbon: No problem, Overdashing is always open to everyone.
Dashbon: If you want to challenge yourself in the future, come find me.

(If you fail and touch the ground)
Dashbon: The third rule of overdashing is that landing means defeat!
Dashbon: Do you want to challenge again?
 Challenge again!
Dashbon: Alright! Please return to the starting point.
 I will come back to challenge later.
Dashbon: Alright, take your time. This sport often requires long-term training to make progress. Looking forward to your next challenge!

(If Flight is used)
Dashbon: I never imagined completing the challenge like this...
Dashbon: It won't help you improve your Overdash technique, but it doesn't violate the regulations either. Off you go, then, "Woosh" to the finish line!

(If you talk to Dashbon again on the rooftop)

 Challenge!

 What are the rules again?
Dashbon: First, the heroes rarely know defeat. As followers, we must rely solely on our own abilities, without relying on external powers to complete the challenge.
Dashbon: Secondly, as a Qixia, with a sharp blade in hand, a weapon by your side, challengers can use external objects such as a grapple in their swift movements to complete the challenges.
Dashbon: Third, as a Qixia, hide in high places, with no one knowing your whereabouts. The path of swift movements can only be on the roofttops, challengers cannot touch the ground midway, and cannot find another way.
Dashbon: These are the requirements for the Overdashing challenge.
Dashbon: How about it, do you want to start a challenge?
 Challenge!
Dashbon: Alright, after the conversation, the challenge will begin. Please reach the distant finish line as soon as possible within the rules!
 Next time for sure.
Dashbon: Alright, I look forward to your next challenge!
 No, I am just passing by.
Dashbon: I see, looking forward to your next challenge!

(After completing the first challenge)
Dashbon: Congratulations on a successful challenge!
Dashbon: Friend, the global fan club of Qingren heroes will give you a set of "The Seven Swords of Qingren" and a collector's edition art book.
Dashbon: Moreover, you have obtained the qualification to participate in the World Championship. If you're interested, I can help you with the registration!
Dashbon: I have also designed a new track! If you want to break your record or challenge new difficulties, come find me!
 New tracks?
Dashbon: Yes! More training will make you faster, higher, and stronger, bringing you closer to becoming a true hero!
Dashbon: I will tell you where the challenges are, and I look forward to your attempts!
Dashbon: If you successfully complete two new tracks, there are new records waiting for you!

(After completing the second challenge)
Dashbon: Brother, I noticed your successful challenge record!

(After completing the third challenge)

(Report to Dashbon)
