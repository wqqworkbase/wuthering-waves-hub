Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionBeside a mysterious morph painting, you find a painter named Claudia, trapped in her nightmares.
Claudia asks you to investigate the world within the painting and gives you a protective talisman.

(Idle)
Claudia: Hmm... Sentinel in the sea. The Golden Bough. Is that... ah... so you've been with me all along...
Claudia: Mm... Where are you taking me... So many mirrors, crows, like writing desks...
Claudia: Fisalia... The Dark Tide. Stay away. Mother, save me...

(Talk to Claudia)
A finely dressed girl sleeps before the painting, her brow furrowed, beads of sweat trailing down her face. She seems ensnared in a nightmare.
 (Gently wake her.)
After hearing your call, the girl wakes from her nightmare.
Claudia: Mmm... I... Where am I? Am I awake? Or is this another dream... Oh, did you wake me up?
 It seems like you had a nightmare.
Claudia: Yes, I did... Thank you for your help. I'm Claudia, a traveling artist capturing inspirations and the history of artworks.
Claudia: I fell asleep after touching the painting and had a long nightmare. It felt like I became someone else in the dream... How embarassing for you to see me like that.
Claudia: By the way, my foreign friend, are you familiar with morph paintings like this one?
 I know it's a painting that links to another dimension.
Claudia: I see, you must have glimpsed some of the mysteries hidden within these paintings.
 Is that a pocket that pulls out magical tools from another dimension?
Claudia: What? They can do that? No wonder the senior line possesses so many secret treasures of mysterious origin...
Claudia: Anyway... despite the danger, the memories I glimpsed in my dream may be connected to the artist of this painting.
Claudia: Unfortunately, I can't recall the dream's details. If only I could investigate again... But since I'm not a Resonator, I can't grasp the essence of this morph painting.
Claudia: Could I entrust this quest to you? I hope you can uncover the secrets hidden within the painting and reveal the fragments of past memories it holds.
 I'll consider it.
Claudia: Thank you. I will prepare a reward for your help. Oh, and please take this talisman. The world inside the painting will be dangerous, and it should help you.
Claudia: It is a gift from my ancestors, believed to be a fragment of the beacon that once guided people to Ragunna.
Claudia: With its aid, should you lose your way within the painting, the light of the Sentinel will guide you.
(Quest Complete)
