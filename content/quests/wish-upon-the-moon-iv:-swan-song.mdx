Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is limited.

- When completing the "Wait for the Moonlit Fair to begin" step, the quest name changes from Festival Preparations IV to Before the Moonlit Fair starts, the name changes back when completing the "Head to Panhua's Restaurant" step.

- When completing the "Tap Patty to enter the unknown Sonoro Sphere" step, the quest will be renamed to Wish Upon the Moon IV: Swan Song.

- When completing the "Back to reality" step, the quest will be renamed to Eve of Celebration.

- Completing the quest awards the player the Rover's Journey trophy The Moon Knows My Heart.
