Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest unlocks 24 hours after finishing The Fabled Magnetic Cube Mechanics - II, displaying "Wait for Mi'zhi to wake up." before being unlocked.

- Completing the quest will grant the Huanglong I trophy Your Mystery, I'll Answer.
