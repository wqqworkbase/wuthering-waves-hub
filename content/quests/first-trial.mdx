Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou receive a Pioneer Association Leaflet in your mailbox. Go to Jinzhou Branch and ask Maqi what has happened.

Maqi: Hello! Welcome to the Jinzhou branch of the Pioneer Association!
Maqi: I guess you must be Rover?
 How did you know?
 Yes, I am.
Maqi: Our headquarters has extended an invitation to you. Please consider joining the Pioneer Association!
Maqi: Measure the planet with your own feet, and witness civilizations through your own eyes.
Maqi: Let's go explore the world together!
 The Pioneer Association?
 Explore the world?
Maqi: Allow me to make a brief introduction.
Maqi: Our core mission at the Pioneer Association is to push the boundaries of humanity's understanding through exploration. We ignite curiosity and embrace a trailblazing mindset, inviting adventurers from all backgrounds.
Maqi: Our team consists of top journalists, writers, photographers, and expeditioners from around the world.
Maqi: Pioneers are free to plan their own expeditions with our support, and we offer platforms for publishing high-quality content such as magazines, podcasts, and books.
 What can I get from this?
Maqi: Our Pioneers earn cash for submissions, plus dedicated funding for approved expedition plans from the Association.
Maqi: Pioneers also have the opportunity to receive special outdoor tools essential for their journeys. As they venture further, these tools can become invaluable aids in exploration.
 I'm in.
Maqi: Great! I'll get your profile set up real quick. You'll be able to check your exploration process so far on your Terminal at any time.
Maqi: And... all set! From now on, Rover, you are a member of the Pioneer Association. You are sort of like my family now!
Maqi: By the way, you should check your expansive selection of publications and merchandise here, Rover. Now we've got the latest issue of Wutherium Geographic, the thrilling series of Into the Depths of Waves, and subscription.
Maqi: There's also the bestselling All You Have to Know about Wuthering Waves and The One Hundred Lives of a Pioneer Association Reporter, plus our customer favorite--Gulfpuff plushies!
Maqi: New customers can buy one and get one 50% off!
 Latest issue?
Maqi: We publish two periodicals actually. Wutherium Geographic for general readers, and Post-Lament Anthropocene for academics.
Maqi: If you're interested in subscribing to our magazines, tell me now and we'll send the latest issue to your doorstep every month!
 Podcast subscription?
Maqi:For adventurers looking for a top-notch entertainment, our Pioneer Podcast has it all. The Podcast is free, and there's also an Insider Channel for paid subscribers.
Maqi: With diverse shows covering news, music, and science, there's something for everyone.
Maqi: The Insider Channel features updates on ongoing explorations by contracted reporters and seasoned explorers.
Maqi: Let me know if you'd like to subscribe!
 Plushies?
Maqi: Oops, Gulpuff plushies sold out... Want me to reserve one for you?
 I'll pass for now.
Maqi: Anyway, you can always reach out for help on your journey. The Pioneer Association is here for you through any obstacles!
(Quest Complete)
