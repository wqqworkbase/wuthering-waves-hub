Steps[]

- Head to "Yumyum Haven"

- Find "Twin Lotus Seed"

- Defeat TDs

- Collect "Twin Lotus Seed"

- Return to the stall
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is time limited.

- Completing the quest unlocks Sanhua as a Lodge Wishes companion in the By Moon's Grace limited time event.
