Steps[]

- Head to Septimont's Old District and ask Maji about its recovery

- Head to Mournfell Canyon to retrieve the lost construction materials

- Defeat the "undying monsters"

- Talk to Medulius

- Talk to Lenore

- Bring Lenore to Septimont's Old District and inform Maji

- Talk to Medulius

- Head to the Old District

- Wait two days
