Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionA priceless package has been shipped across the ocean to Ragunna. It is said that its freight insurance premium alone costs millions of credits!

(Talk to Maji)
Maji: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! Oh dear, you won't believe this!
 What's wrong?
Maji: The freight insurance of the item we're delivering today is worth a billion! I can't even imagine how much the actual item must cost... probably a number I could never earn in my lifetime!
 What could it possibly be...
Maji: No idea. Based on the tiny package, it could be a precious gem? We need to be extremely careful with this one...
 Got it. I'll take care of it.
Maji: Safe delivery!
 I wonder if my payment's going to be in the 10-digit range too.
Maji: Well, that's a bit beyond my reach... But maybe one day, when I strike it rich!

(Talk to Frejon)
Frejon: Oh, my package has finally arrived!
 Here you go, the invaluable, precious package.
 Yes, Mr. Super Duper Rich.
Frejon: Let me check. Hmm, looks perfect, no damage at all...
Mr. Super Duper Rich eagerly opens his packages and pulls out a smooth, delicate egg.
Frejon: Very well! This will be my dinner tonight!
 Is this... some kind of super expensive egg?
Frejon: Ha, indeed it is! I found it so lovely when I first saw it. Too bad I couldn't get it myself since I was busy with my business. You've been a great help!
 Is this... Ovathrax?
Frejon: Ha, it's just an egg, but I found it so lovely when I first saw it. Too bad I couldn't get it myself since I was busy with my business. You've been a great help!
Frejon: Just look at its gleaming shell and perfect size. It must taste divine... Couldn't have asked for better.
 ...
Frejon: Thank you so much! This package really made my day!
(Quest Complete)
