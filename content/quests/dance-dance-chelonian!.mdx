Steps[]

- Assist the Hoartoise and defeat the enemies

- Catch up with the Hoartoise

- Assist the Hoartoise and defeat the enemies again

- Catch up with the Hoartoise, again

- Assist the two Hoartoise and defeat the enemies

- Inspect the note on the ground

- Play the "Miraculous Music Instrument"

- Take out the "Instrument"
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest will grant the Footprints of Huanglong II trophy Ballad of Sword.
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionOn an island in a lake aroung Hongzhen, a Hoartoise seems to have a conflict with other Tacet Discords... Do Tacet Discords lock horns?

Rover: This Hoartoise is getting mobbed by other Tacet Discords... Looks like it really needs help.

(Assist the Hoartoise and defeat the enemies)
Rover: Seems like it wants to take me somewhere. I should follow it and see what's going on there.

(Catch up with the Hoartoise)
Rover: Why is it fighting with the Tacet Discords again...

(Assist the Hoartoise and defeat the enemies again)
Rover: Is there another fight? Seriously?

(Catch up with the Hoartoise, again)
Rover: So you're leading me to help your friend? Didn't expect camaraderie among Hoartoises.

(Inspect the note on the ground)
Performance Invitation LetterHello, stranger!
If you're reading this message, you must have been selected as the first audience member of our Chelonian Dance Troupe!
A long time ago, I stumbled upon an instrument that can work miracles—the melodious music it plays can make me, animals, and even Tacet Discords dance to its tune. Of all the dances, the dance of tortoises is the most moving.
Since I was a child, I've always dreamed of becoming a performer who sings and dances. Unfortunately, probably due to my lack of talent, I've never been able to sing and dance well despite how much time and effort I've devoted to my training.
However, the moment I saw the tortoises dance, I set a goal for myself—to found a dance troupe!
With my guidance, the Chelonian Dance Troupe has been established for some time now, and our members' rehearsal efforts are beginning to bear fruit.
Now, it's time to pick up the instrument and let the music play!
Let's get moving, Chelonian Dance Troupe! Show the audience a dance they will never forget!
—Founder of Chelonian Dance Troupe
Rover: A miraculous music instrument? Chelonian Dance Troupe? What's this all about...
Rover: The Hoartoises are headed back to the place we came. Is that "instrument" mentioned in the letter there?

(Play the "Miraculous Music Instrument")
Rover: They are jiggling more happily now...
(Quest Complete)
