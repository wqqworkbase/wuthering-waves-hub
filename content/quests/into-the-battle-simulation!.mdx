Into the Battle Simulation! is a Tutorial Quest received by players when first unlocking Lahai-Roi Resonance Nexus. 
Starting this quest unlocks Tactical Hologram: Synchronization.

 Quest DescriptionThe Rabelle College seems to be recruiting participants for a virtual combat course. Head to the training platform to learn more.

Napolita: Hello there, fellow student. Welcome to the Academy's virtual training platform. Interested to know more?
 Training platform?
 What's this for?
Napolita: This is a virtual combat course co-developed by the Rabelle College faculty, students, and engineers. It's to test the combat capabilities of students in extreme enviornments
Napolita: The Rabelle College serves as the birthplace for Synchronists. In addition to various Exostrider training, personal fitness is also crucial factor taken into consideration!
Napolita: We've gathered data from across various regions and managed to simulate highly formidable frequencies that have appeared all over the world!
Napolita: We've also set in place a comprehensive evaluation system. Bring our combat results to department instructors and earn rewards!
 Sounds interesting.
 Where do I start?
Napolita: Oh! You wish to give it a spin? It's easy. All you have to do is sit in this simulation cockpit, and you're good to go.
Napolita: And no need to be nervouse, even if it's your first try. Just start off from a low difficulty and steadily move your way up.
Napolita: Well, good luck!

(After entering simulator cockpit)
Napolita: So, how was it? Did the simulation go well for you?
 Piece of cake.
Napolita: Let's see your results... Amazing! You managed to achieve such a high score on your first try!
Napolita: I haven't met you before at the Rabelle College. Are you a freshmen? We don't get to see such incredible freshmen very often!
Napolita: We also had a new kid with crazy combat results before. What was her name again... Ae... Aemeath? It's a shame she only came here once, and I never got to see her again...
