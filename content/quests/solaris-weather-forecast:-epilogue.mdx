Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionHaving finished the data collection for Arno, you receive a message from him requesting a meeting in Jinzhou.

(Talk to Arno)
Arno: Thank you, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Arno: The surveillance data you collected has been analyzed and sent back to the Black Shores.
Arno: The data is top-notch—maybe enough for us to add a new variable in our forecast model.
Arno: Hopefully, we'll be able to provide even better predictions soon.
 I'm happy I could help.
Arno: You're being too kind. We couldn't have done this without you...
Arno: KU-Ryan and I are not exactly the athletic type. It would've been nearly impossible to gather sufficient data in such a short time.
 And will that new variable improve your predictions a lot?
Arno: It may not have a huge impact. Maybe just a few seconds or milliseconds of difference.
Arno: But who knows what situations we'll face in the future? Our new model might be the key to unlocking any loopholes in the old one.
Arno: Many people may not understand why I collect data in such a tedious way.
Arno: But like they say in Huanglong, a miniscule error could lead to major consequences.
Arno: "If only the forecast was one second closer..." "If only the projected spot was ten centimeters more precise..."
Arno: ...But by then, it would be too tardy for regrets.
Arno: A few seconds or centimeters could mean life and death. I could've saved more lives with a more precise prediction—
Arno: The thought of that makes me unable to accept any error in the data I handle.
Arno: Oops. Sorry, I got ahead of myself.
Arno: Anyway, thank you so much, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
Arno: I look forward to working with you again!
(Quest Complete)
