Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou meet Xuanyin again. This time, she is spying on a Green Rabbit...

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), nice to see you again!
Xuanyin: We really have a connection, literally meeting everywhere.
 Indeed.
Xuanyin: And for the record, I absolutely didn't follow you! Even though your Pangu Terminal is essential for my research.
Xuanyin: I believe in the inevitability of objectivity. Just like the numbers rolled on the dice aren't random; our every encounter is destined by past causes and future consequences.
Xuanyin: Doesn't that sound romantic?
 Let's stick to the topic.
Xuanyin: Okay, fine. Actually, I'm now observing these Green Rabbits. Did you notice anything special?
 They don't have tails?
Xuanyin: Exactly, their tails shed periodically. After shedding, the Green Rabbits will hide away from their original group.
Xuanyin: There are various interpretations of this behavior. With the translation module, we can probably find out the truth today!
Xuanyin loads the Rabbit Language Translation Module onto your Pangu Terminal.
Green Rabbit with Broken Tail (Translation): Sobbing...
Xuanyin: Is this little tailless one crying? Seems like there is a lot happening inside them.
Green Rabbit with Broken Tail (Translation): Sobbing...
 Is the translation module malfunctioning again?
Xuanyin: Hmm, the information transmission is all normal, and no issues were found. How about we try finding some control groups?
 Do you mean the Green Rabbits with tails?
Xuanyin: Yes. Green Rabbits feed on grass or leaves. We can use common plants to attract them.
Xuanyin: There are Pearl Leaves everywhere here. Let's use them.

(Deliver a Pearl Leaf to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: Have you brought the Pearl Leaf?
(Submit Pearl Leaf x1)
You attract more Green Rabbits with the Pearl Leaves. While they're eating, the tailless Green Rabbits are driven away and dare not approach.
Green Rabbit with Tail (Translation): No tail! Ugly! Ugly!
 Why do they talk like that?
Xuanyin: Sorry, that's how I set it.
Xuanyin: Don't you think it's more interesting and understandable this way?
Xuanyin: From a researcher's standpoint, tail loss in Green Rabbits is considered a natural aspect of their metabolism. However, for them, the tail serves as a criterion to distinguish beauty from ugliness. Quite intriguing.
 Are rabbits also concerned about appearances?
Xuanyin: All animals want to "select high-quality mates," as it serves as a form of survival strategy.
Xuanyin: Green Rabbits feed on grass and their tail glands play an important role in metabolizing toxins. When toxins accumulate to a certain extent, it manifests as "tail loss."
Xuanyin: But I didn't anticipate that they would be so disheartened because of this reason...
 They're just rabbits.
Xuanyin: Well, when I was optimizing the module, I introduced human thinking logic to it this time.
Xuanyin: And! I, myself, am the sampler of human thinking logic. Don't you think the translation is more vivid?
Xuanyin uninstalls the Rabbit Language Translation Module from your Pangu Terminal and transmits the related data back to her.
Xuanyin: Farewell! I'll go back to write the report. See you next time!
