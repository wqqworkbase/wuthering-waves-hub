Steps[]

- Talk to Maqi to find out about today's game scenario.

- Complete today's scenario

- (Optional) Find out about "Unforgettable Dishes" from Flora&#32;&#91;sic&#93;.

- Give the Unforgettable Dish to Maqi
Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionMaqi is trying to contact you, and it appears to be related to the Pioneer Association's ambitious project, "Second Coming of Solaris"...

(Talk to Maqi)
 Today's scenario...
Maqi: Let me explain our job today.
Maqi: This chapter recounts how the protagonist met their destined companions for the first time.
Maqi: It goes: "The most reliable way to find you a reliable teammate is not through good vibes but a good fight."
Maqi: "And when you're concerned about mutual faith, ask for a bowl of hot soup when you suffer hunger and cold in the wilderness."
Maqi: During such a hard time, someone offered a bowl of hot soup to our protagonist.
Maqi: I would remember it for the rest of my life if I were the protagonist.
Maqi: What kind of food do you think would work best in such a scenario, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)?
 A bowl of hot congee.
Maqi: Well, I didn't know you would like that.
 A bowl of meat soup.
 Anything to fill the belly.
Maqi: After all, we don't really have many choices.
Maqi: Anyway, please pick a suitable dish as a reference for our dev team.
Maqi: If you happen to come across some samples, feel free to drop by.

(Optional - Talk to Maqi)
 Give me some clues to "Unforgettable Dishes".
Maqi: Well Panhua's Restaurant is definitely one of the must-haves in Jinzhou.
Maqi: If you need to learn about foods, I suggest you ask for Panhua's advice.

(Optional - Talk to Panhua)
 About the Dish in the novel...
Panhua: Though I've never read the book, the story certainly feels close to home!
Panhua: I remember back when I was young and my family was struggling to make ends meet, I could nibble on a plain flatbread that the kind old man of the village gave me to get me through the day.
Panhua: Perhaps it was these days that nurtured my love for cooking.
Panhua: What could be happier than to please your guests with all your cooking skills?
Panhua: Speaking of which, here is a specialty I made just now.
Panhua: It's called Jinzhou Stew, General Jiyan's favorite food of all time.
Panhua: It's also a taste from their childhood for many in Jinzhou.
 It certainly uses many ingredients.
 It looks delicious.
Panhua: Hehe, right? A stew of meat and vegetables always serves the best when you're hungry!
Panhua: Oh, don't forget your urgent errands. Let's continue our food conversation next time. I still have a few dishes to serve.
Panhua: You can drop by for a slow enjoyable meal when available. I'll cook for you!
(Obtain Jinzhou Stew  x1)

(Talk to Maqi)

(Submit Jinzhou Stew x1)
 I'm turning in "Unforgettable Dishes".
Maqi: It should be the ideal food to signify their first encounter.
Some time passed while Maqi was away to hand over the item...
Maqi: Thank you. That wraps up today's work!
 Do you find these guys familiar?
Maqi: No, I don't know them.
Maqi: Anyway, let's call it a day. See you tomorrow, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
(Quest Complete)
