Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou received a contact from the Ivory and Ebony Gatekeepers. Something must've happened with the Somnoire.

(Talk to Ivory Gatekeeper)
Ivory Gatekeeper: There you are! Right on time, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
 What happened?
Ebony Gatekeeper: ...
Ivory Gatekeeper: Don't worry. There's no immediate crisis that requires your attention... at least, not yet.
Ivory Gatekeeper: This time, it's an invitation we never saw coming.
The Ivory Gatekeeper hands you a finely crafted invitation. As you scan the unfamiliar handwriting, the words "Solaris Film Festival" stand out.
 What is this?
Ebony Gatekeeper: I don't know.
 Did you two organize this?
Ebony Gatekeeper: ...No, it wasn't us.
Ivory Gatekeeper: This is a first for us as well. The invitation undeniably carries the deep dream frequencies of the Somnoire, but… that's a dreamscape. Objects from a dream aren't supposed to manifest in reality.
 ...
Ivory Gatekeeper: We have two theories. One, the Somnoire has developed a consciousness of its own. Two, someone within the Somnoire possesses an ability powerful enough to shatter the boundary between dreams and reality, someone we never knew existed.
Ebony Gatekeeper: And now, they've sent an invitation. Maybe a path to the deeper layers of the Somnoire has finally opened.
 That sounds like trouble.
Ivory Gatekeeper: It does. While we can't determine the host's true intentions yet, one thing is certain: neither of us Gatekeepers can access the deeper layers. Only you can.
 Or… maybe it's just a new film festival?
Ivory Gatekeeper: You're right. There's no reason to dismiss that idea. The Somnoire is a place of endless possibilities. Maybe something new has come into existence, and it doesn't necessarily spell trouble.
Ivory Gatekeeper: But neither of us Gatekeepers can access the deeper layers. Only you can.
Ivory Gatekeeper: In other words, this invitation was meant for you alone.
 ...
Rover: Looks like I don't have much of a choice.
Ivory Gatekeeper: Our best hope is that the invitation comes with goodwill, and you are just attending a film festival within a dream... But no matter what happens, keep your guard up. We'll support you however we can.
Ebony Gatekeeper: If it turns out to be safe, enjoy the festival. We'll be waiting for your return.
(Quest Complete)
