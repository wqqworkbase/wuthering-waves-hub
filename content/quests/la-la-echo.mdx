Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionA musician in Ragunna City seems troubled by his Echo companion Melody...

(Approach the designated location)
Tommaso: Oh... my soulmate, my king of inspiration... what has happened to you...
Melody: *Buzz—buzz—whirr—clickety-clack—*

(Talk to Tommaso)
Tommaso: Oh... my soulmate, my king of inspiration... what has happened to you...
Melody: *Buzz—buzz—whirr—clickety-clack—*
 What seems to be wrong with this echo?
Tommaso: Hello, traveler from afar. My name is Tommaso, and I am a musician. This here is my Echo partner, the "Aria Mummer" Melody.
 Oh my gosh, it's starting to transform!
Tommaso: Transform? Melody can't do that, I'm afraid... Hello, traveler from afar. My name if Tommaso, and I am a musician. This here is my Echo partner, the "Aria Mummer" Melody.
Tommaso: Melody possesses the ability to capture every melody it hears and orchestrate its surrounding "Melody Ensemble" to replay them.
Tommaso: I have known Melody for many years, and it is thanks to its presence that I can savor those fleeting melodies, with an endless stream of inspiration flowing to me each day.
Tommaso: We often venture out together to gather inspirations, but lately, with preparations for the Carnevale, I've been caught up and had to leave Melody to handle it alone in the city.
Tommaso: Once I finally finished my work, I realized Melody had already ended up like this...
Melody: *Buzz—buzz buzz—whimper—*
Tommaso: It can no longer perform entire melodies. Even its voice shows signs of "distortion"... Melody... without you, how am I supposed to live?
 Maybe it's because it has listened to too many different things...
 All work and no play makes even the sharpest mind dull...
Tommaso: Sure... It seems likely... Ah, my friend, judging by your posture and attire, you must be a seasoned adventurer.
Tommaso: Have you heard or recorded any particularly memorable music during your adventure?
Tommaso: Could you share them with Melody? This might ease its "symptoms"...

(Talk to Tommaso)
Tommaso: Oh... oh! Yes! This is the sound Melody was meant to have! This... this is true music!
Tommaso: Thank you! Traveler from afar! This... "Melody Box" in your hands, where did you find it? Such wondrous music, captured on a tiny disc like this...
 Just a stroke of luck.
 Adapted from True Experiences.
 I am merely a porter in this world.
Tommaso: Hmm... So, what you're saying is, these are the things you've gathered through countless adventures in this vast world? Like a... response from the world to you?
Tommaso: I once heard a saying: All the music in this world is prewritten by the divine, scattered across the land. It is the duty of musicians to discover and record them.
Tommaso: It seems Melody and I should venture beyond Ragunna City and explore the wider world.
Tommaso: In the cacophony of the world's sounds, the lifelong challenge for every musician is to distill the purest melody... and "Aria Mummers' are no exception.
Tommaso: Perhaps within Rinascita, you may encounter other "Aria Mummers." They might be facing similar challenges.
Tommaso: I have always believed, alongside Melody, that great music has the power to touch others and inspire change.
Tommaso: During your adventure, if you happen to gather other "Melody Boxes," please lend a hand to the other "Aria Mummers" as well.
Tommaso: Please accept these as a token of my gratitude for your help!
(Quest Complete)
