Steps[]

- Decode the first Wish Tag with Patty

- Talk to Xiangli Yao to find out the meaning of the Wish Tag

- Follow the Wish Tag clues to find Xiaosheng

- Ask the researcher about the clues

- Talk to the eavesdropper

- Go to the target area

- Defeat the target Tacet Discords and collect Echo data

- Return to the laboratory to find and talk to Shifan
Gameplay Notes[]

- This quest is limited.

- Completing the quest awards the player the Rover's Journey trophy We're Listening.
