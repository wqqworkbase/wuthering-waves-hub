Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionWork with Yihu

(Talk to Yihu)
Yihu: Hi, you're here. Are you here to see me?
 I want to know how the last joke turned out.
Yihu: Oh, you reminded me.
Yihu: There's a follow-up to that joke!
 Tell me.
Yihu: Sorry, I can't right now. I have customers waiting for their tea.
 Let me help. That'll be faster.
Yihu: Thank you, but that's not good...
 It's okay.
Yihu: Well, I appreciate it. Let's serve the tea to the table over there.

(Serve the tea)
Teahouse Diner: Hey, newcomer, huh?
 I'm just helping out.
Teahouse Diner: Leave the tea here. Thank you.

(Talk to Yihu)
Yihu: Thank you for your help. Okay, let me continue.
Yihu: So, the story goes, the boss couldn't stand the ongoing oil theft.
Yihu: Looking at his fat cat lazily grooming itself, the boss went mad and kicked the cat out in frustration.
 So the cat's plan backfired, didn't it?
Yihu: You're right.
Yihu: So I thought the cat must be flustered.
Yihu: Soon after, the boss brought another cat back, all majestic. I thought, finally, the oil would no longer be stolen.
Yihu: Guess what happened?
 What happened?
Yihu: At midnight, I saw these two cats happily sneaking out together.
Yihu: Wondering why?
Yihu: The old cat shared with me that, being old and knowing the boss wouldn't keep him much longer, he had decided to sacrifice himself and let his son take over his place.
Yihu: Working as a family, these two cats cleverly manipulated the boss, enjoying a feast.
 What about that mouse?
Yihu: Probably eaten? When there's meat to feast on, who would care about that mouse? Plus, it's a good chance to display the son's skills to the boss.
Yihu: As the saying goes, "If you let the mice guard a warehouse, there'll be nothing left," and "If you let the cats guard a house, it'll fall apart."
(Quest Complete)
