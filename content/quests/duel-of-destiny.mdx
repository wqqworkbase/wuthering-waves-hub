Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionTwo legendary Gladiators are about to face their destined duel atop the tower. Sulpicia asks you to learn the story behind it…

(Talk to Sulpicia)
Sulpicia: I can tell you boast incredible skills just by looking at you, my friend! I wonder, have you noticed those two elite warriors atop the towers?
Sulpicia: People say they were once the supreme and legendary Gladiators of Septimont. Three years ago this very day, this very location, they agreed to meet each other in glorious battle... Now that they've both completed their trainings, they've returned to Septimont to fulfill their promise...
Sulpicia: Stories like this simply write themselves! I've got to learn more about them before they exchange blades!
Sulpicia: What kind of emotions do they bring into battle? Could it be a furious thirst for vengeance, a lonesome yearning for defeat, or an explosive mixture of admiration and hatred? I must find out!
Sulpicia: May I ask you to go and give them an interview? I'm sure they'd be willing to open up to a master such as yourself!
Sulpicia: Please. If you can do me this favor, I'll have adequate rewards ready for you once you return.

Find Artemus[]

(Talk to Artemus)
Artemus: Kaimastos... It's unbelievable to think your obsession has reached such heights.
You interview Artemus on his current thoughts.
Artemus: Are you going to put this in the newspaper? In that case...
Artemus: Echoes are the embodiment of a Gladiator's soul. For your twisted soul, Kaimastos, I shall set it right!
Artemus: Oh, could you add one extra line in there for me? Write: Do you love Peaks of Prestige? Then visit the Double Six Games of Septimont! Get a unique anniversary card with your first purchase, for free! Plus, receive additional discounts if you sign up for our membership, and earn a chance to grab the legendary Dark Wizard Echo set every tenth consecutive purchase!
Rover: Legendary Dark Wizard?
Artemus summons his Echo.
Rover: Isn't that just a Chop Chop?

Find Kaimastos[]

(Talk to Kaimastos)
Kaimastos: Artemus! My arch nemesis! At last, I've awaited this day far too long! I shall crush you with my own hands, just as I've dreamed of!
You interview Kaimastos on his current thoughts.
Kaimastos: My current thoughts? Hmm... How about you record the events that will occur here? Especially the moments of Artemus's defeat beneath my feet!
Kaimastos: Heed my summoning! My soul, my pride, my most powerful servant&#8212;White-Flames Blue Dragon!
Rover: Wait, is he about to summon the Dragon of Dirge?!
Kaimastos summons his Echo.
Kaimastos: Aaahahaha! Now tremble in despair, Artemus! I will shatter your dreams along with your honor!
Rover: And it's just a tiny little Drake...

Report back to Sulpicia[]

(Talk to Sulpicia)
Sulpicia: Oh, you're back! How'd it go? Tell me! What did the two legendary Gladiators have to say?
 This is probably going to disappoint...
 They weren't actually legendary gladiators per se...
You summarize the interview with the Peaks of Prestige players.
Sulpicia: Ahhh! So that's how it is... That is rather...
Rover: Looks like you'll need to find a different source of inspiration...
Sulpicia: That is rather brilliant!!
Rover: Huh?
Sulpicia: It's a splendid topic! I've already got a complete outline in mind! Thanks a million, and here's a reward for your time!
Sulpicia: Two young passionate warriors, born from completely opposite backgrounds, yet both equally burdened with the weight of destiny, as they rush forth on a collision course of growth, betting their souls in a dark duel with their pride at stake...
Sulpicia hands you the rewards, then proceeds to retreat into her own world.
(Quest Complete)
