Summary[]

You receive a message from Encore, saying that she has obtained something super amazing and fun, and that she's excited to share it with you. What could it be?
Turns out, the "super cool" thing is actually the Second Coming of Solaris, which has been in beta for a whole year. Encore asks for your help with her homework: developing a mysterious new feature for the game. Unable to resist her cuteness, you create an account and dive right into the world of Second Solaris.
Upon entering the Second Coming of Solaris, you're shocked to find yourself transformed into a Cube. Before you can even process what's happening, the anniversary cake&#8212;the game's control unit&#8212;gets devoured by a mysterious Cube named "Abbowser"!
After swallowing the control unit, Abbowser takes control of Second Solaris and holds the other Cubes hostage, forcing you into a classic "Hero vs. Demon Lord" game. Given the mysterious connection between Abbowser and Abby, you decide to play along.
In the second level of the castle, you encounter Camellya, who has taken over as the chief of the New Black Shores with the help of Abbowser. You agree to play hide-and-seek with her.
After having her fill of fun, Camellya ends this little game of hide-and-seek and prepares to return to the Cube World with you. Abbowser finally realizes it's been tricked by Camellya. But facing its own minions' betrayal, Abbowser has no choice but to make a strategic retreat.
In the fourth level of the castle, you find Pero, who has fused with Roccia. Hungry, she decides to go on a hunt for good food in Abbowser Castle.
After devouring the dishes, "Roccia" can't resist gobbling up Abbowser's cherished dessert too. Deprived of its favorite treat, Abbowser leaves in tears, tugging at the heartstrings of everyone present.
On another level, someone has swiped the chest Abbowser set. But thanks to a coin left behind, you have a pretty good idea of who the thief might be.
Just as you suspected, Brant is the "culprit" who stole the treasure. But when Abbowser accuses him, Brant coolly tricks Abbowser into waking a giant Chest Mimic. So, who's the real villain now?
You arrive at another level, where the Montelli family is still in charge due to Abbowser's poor management, and Carlotta tries to negotiate to gain ownership of its Echoes
.The negotiation between Abbowser and Carlotta is at a total stalemate. Your arrival tips the scales. With no minions or Echoes acting as backup, Abbowser is completely cooked and utterly defeated. Meanwhile, Carlotta seems to have found some clues leading to other Cubes in this level.
You find out bad Cubes have been confessing to the Primus, and a certain amazing recruit Z is currently working for Abbowser in the level.
When you arrive at the order of the D, you're surprised to find that the so-called Primus is none other than Phoebe. In her usual well-meaning way, she tried to fix some bad Cubes, which landed her in a bit of a pickle. Deciding to lend a hand, you manage to rescue Phoebe from her predicament.
To your surprise, the mysterious recruit Z is Zani, found inside a chest. I turns out she's working for Abbowser without pay because of the giant Chest Mimic she set up, which gave Abbowser a hard bite. Zani politely declines Carlotta's offer to help pay off her debt and promises to assist you after work every day.
Once again, Abbowser blocks your path, this time with the mighty Elite No. 2, Cantarella. But oops! She "accidentally" uses the wrong powder, making you even more powerful.
You finally meet the legendary and super mighty Elite No. 1, who turns out to be none other than your old acquaintance, Calcharo. In the end, you strike a deal; he hands over the newly captured Brant, and you hire him to help settle his debt with Abbowser.
You encounter Elite No. 3, Cartethyia, who appears to have a special connection with Abbowser. By the time you show up, they've already split over a design dispute. To ensure Abbowser doesn't make a dramatic return, you decide to hunt it down.
With the help of Elite No. 2, Cantarella, you finally put an end to Abbowser's mischief without any fuss. Now that all the Cubes are safe, it's time to fix the cake and get the ceremony started!

Glossary[]

- Second Coming of Solaris &#8212; A virtual simulation game developed by the Pioneer Association. Despite many setbacks during development, thanks to Rover's trust and support, the game is steadily evolving towards its ideal form.

- Cube &#8212; Officially known as "Remnant Cubes," these advanced artificial intelligences were created for the Second Coming of Solaris using Encore's abilities and Tethys's microprocessing support. Please note: Remnant Cubes are experimental simulations, automatically generated by the system. Their personalities and actions in the game do not reflect those of real individuals.

- Anniversary Cake &#8212; A large cake dedicated to the 1st anniversary of the launch of the Second Coming of Solaris Beta, as well as the control hub for its simulator. When asked about the hub's cake-shaped design, lead designer Encore replied, "What's a birthday without cake! Happy Anniversary!"

- Channel &#8212; A signal transmission pathway. During its simulation, the Second Coming of Solaris briefly linked with the Tethys System, creating a temporary channel between them.

- Sonoro Sphere &#8212; Sonoro Sphere is a space of dense Remnant Energy capable of preserving transdimensional information. In Huanglong culture, such ethereal realms&#8212;neither tangible nor illusory&#8212;are often referred to as "mirages."

- Ghost Hounds &#8212; A mercernary group in the New Federation led by Calcharo.
