Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionA girl called Dorothy appears next to a mysterious gate. She claims a sudden crisis is taking place inside.

(Talk to Dorothy)
Dorothy: Oh, it's you again! It's been a while, hasn't it? Hmm, no, should I say, "Nice to meet you?"
 I don't think we've met before.
Dorothy: Really? That's not too surprising though... Anything can happen here! Maybe I mixed up the time, mistaking what hasn't happened with what already has...
 You're the child from before.
Dorothy: That's right! I feel like we've met somewhere else before. But... when?
Dorothy: Hmm, I can't remember.
Dorothy: Oh, by the way, have you seen that large black cat?
 You mean... the Gatekeeper?
Dorothy: I don't know its name, but it's a super big black cat! Like bigger than a tiger!
 We fought once.
Dorothy: Huh... why? It's not a bad cat.
Dorothy: There are scary monsters beyond the gate. I've seen them before, but this time, they feel... different.
Dorothy: Something tells me they're from somewhere... "deeper."
 You mean, the depths of dreams?
Dorothy: I'm not sure. They're probably from somewhere I haven't been... or even know about.
Dorothy: They're tough. My friends and I couldn't handle them. But, thankfully, that black cat showed up out of nowhere.
Dorothy: It carried me on its back and brought me through the gate, but then… it turned around and went back.
Dorothy: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), could you help us? Please defeat those monsters and save my friends and that big black cat!
Dorothy: Even though most of my friends are still stuck on the other side, all of us who got out will help you like always!
 As always...?
 Has this happened before?
Dorothy: Maybe... at some point in the future? After all, time is a closed loop.
 (Remain silent)
 ...I've heard that before.
Dorothy: Anyway, I'll be rooting for you! Please drive those bad guys away!
(Quest Complete)
