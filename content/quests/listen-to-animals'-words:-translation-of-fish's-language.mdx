Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou meet Xuanyin again. This time, she is spying on the Tetra...

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: Hi, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). Come and take a look at these Tetra. Move gently.
 So it's the Fish Language Translation Module this time?
Xuanyin: Perfect guess! Strictly speaking, it can't be called a language module. After all, fish don't have vocal organs. Their ways of speaking are diverse.
Xuanyin: They "speak" via grinding throat teeth, vibrating muscles, and compressing swim bladders.
Xuanyin: As water is much denser than air, we need to use special techniques to collect the "sounds" emitted by them.
 I can sense subtle vibrations in the water.
Xuanyin: Wow, Resonators' perceptions are indeed more sensitive than us ordinary people.
Xuanyin: However, I've developed a technology that can help ordinary people grasp those subtle vibrations. Don't you think I'm a genius? Hehe.
 Only if the results are reliable and accurate.
Xuanyin: Well, you're right. Let's listen to what these Tetra are saying.
Xuanyin loads the Fish Language Translation Module onto your Pangu Terminal.
Tetra (Translation): Gurgle, gurgle, gurgle... Help...
Xuanyin: Oh no, the Tetras seem to be drowning and asking for help!
Xuanyin: Are they a mutated population that no longer breathes through gills but hasn't fully evolved their lungs? Is that why they are now struggling to breathe in the water?
Tetra (Translation): Gurgle...
Xuanyin: They're running out of air! (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), we need to rescue them as soon as possible!
Xuanyin: I'm not a good swimmer, so can you help me catch them? They will definitely make precious samples!
 Are you really a scientist?
Xuanyin: I worked overtime to improve the logic of this module. It's absolutely reliable!
Xuanyin: I know it may sound crazy, but you need to believe in my talent and invention!
Xuanyin jumps into the water and fishes out the Tetra.
 If you insist...
Xuanyin: I knew you were reliable!
(Catch the Tetra in the water)
You managed to catch every Tetra in the water.

(Talk to Xuanyin)
Xuanyin: How could this happen...
 They died.
Xuanyin: Sigh, this is a necessary sacrifice for closing in on the truth. Rest in peace. I will pay tribute to them too in the acknowledgments.
 Great, now you can remove my name from it.
Xuanyin: Hey, don't say that. I genuinely appreciate everyone who helped me, including animals.
Xuanyin: To cherish their remaining value, why don't you take it home? I heard it tastes fine...
Xuanyin: As for the translation module, I didn't expect so many malfunctions occuring this time. I need to check it out carefully.
Xuanyin: I was wondering why it didn't show any garbled text. Turns out it crashed a long time ago, haha. Alright, let me uninstall it while I'm at it.
Xuanyin: See you next time, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).
