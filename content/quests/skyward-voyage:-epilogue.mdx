Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionThe captain named David&#32;&#91;sic&#93; has finally taken the Gondola to the skies, but will this voyage go as planned?

(Talk to "Old Captain" Davide)
"Old Captain" Davide: So we meet again, my friend.
 Are you alright?
"Old Captain" Davide: Me? Couldn't be any better. The view above the clouds was exactly like what I dreamed.
"Old Captain" Davide: For a final voyage, it was everything I ever wanted, and deeply satisfying.
 Where is your Gondola?
"Old Captain" Davide: Oh, don't remind me. That Wingray had a mind of its own—it wouldn't change course or land.
"Old Captain" Davide: Eventually, hunger got the better of me, so I jumped into the sea, watching my partner and the Wingrays fade into the horizon...
 I'm sorry to hear that.
 What a bittersweet ending.
"Old Captain" Davide: That's alright. She seemed happy as could be. My partner loved the thrill of adventure as much as I do—we're two of a kind.
"Old Captain" Davide: She'll continue that grand, mysterious voyage, and I'm sure she'd find plenty of joy along the way.
"Old Captain" Davide: And, well, I did manage to find a little surprise.
 A surprise?
"Old Captain" Davide: It's that chest over there. I stumbled upon a floating platform in the clouds, and it was sitting right on top. No idea who left it there.
 You found the ruin?
"Old Captain" Davide: No, not really. Just a chest resting on a platform in the clouds. No idea how it got there.
"Old Captain" Davide: It could be the origin of that tale, even if it turned out, well, less impressive than I imagined.
"Old Captain" Davide: Anyway, as we agreed, everything in it is yours.
 What will you do now?
"Old Captain" Davide: Me? I'll go back to Ragunna and live the quiet life of a retired captain, telling my tales to anyone who'll listen.
"Old Captain" Davide: A captain who flew his Gondola to the sky... Well, who'd believe that?
"Old Captain" Davide: Farewell, my friend. May the Sentinel always bless you.
(Quest Complete)
