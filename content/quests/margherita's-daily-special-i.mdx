Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionA pair of cautious eyes from the corner of Margherita's restaurant seem to call for your attention. Drop by and take a look when you have the chance.

(Talk to the girl behind the pillar)
Shy girl: Ah! I'm so sorry! I didn't mean to sneak around... I'll leave right now.
 Why were you hiding behind the pillar?
Shy girl: I... I heard they were hiring, so I thought I'd take a peek. But Ms.Margherita seems really busy, and I didn't want to be a bother.
 Opportunity waits for no one.
Shy girl: You're right. I should go and&#8212;
Growl
 ...
 Are you hungry?
Shy girl: Eek! Sorry! I, um, haven't eaten anything today, and the food here smells amazing.
 It's about time for a meal anyway.
 You should really eat something.
Shy girl: I'd love to, but I don't have enough money...
 It will be my treat.
Shy girl: Really? Margherita makes the best food! My mum used to bring me here all the time.
Shy girl: But it doesn't feel quite right since we just met.
 Let's go see Margherita first.

(Talk to Margherita)
Margherita: Ah if it isn't Player! Welcome! What will be today?
 What would you like to eat?
Shy girl: Me? I'm fine with anything.
Margherita: Oh, you're Lelia from down by the Gondola Market, right? It's been ages since you stopped by! Nuvola Pasta, same as before?
Lelia: I didn't expect you to remember me. Actually, there's something I...
 (You give the girl a gentle nudge)
Lelia: Ms.Margherita, I'm here to apply for a job!
Margherita: Aha! So that's why you were being all squirrely! You are right on time &#8212; wer'e so busy right now that even poor Bakie is going nuts!
Lelia: I may not have a lot of experience, but if you hire me, I'm ready to dive in and give my absolute best!'
Margherita: You definitely can't do that on an empty belly! Well now, for you two special regulars...
Margherita: I suggest a "Thank-You Meal for Two" with Nuvola Pasta!
Margherita: This one's my treat. Now eat up!
(You and Lelia enjoy the delicious meal together. Shortly after, Lelia becomes the restaurant's new member.)
(Quest Complete)
