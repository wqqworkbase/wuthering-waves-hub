Summary[]

With Ishmael's guidance, you manage to find the old captain, Ahab at the dock. Ahab gladly lets you borrow his old Gondola, and you can go fishing at sea with it.
After you've recaptured the escaped fish, Ahab suggests you check out the market. To your surprise, Phoebe is there too. You find her in a sticky situation because of the locals' fraught history with the Order of the Deep. After you and Ahab help her out, Phoebe reveals she came here searching for Cetus the Tidebreaker. Ahab remarks that you'll run into the beast eventually just by going out fishing.

Steps[]

- Find the fisherman

- Find the Gondolier to prepare for the voyage to the Riccioli Islands

- Search for the old captain, "Ahab"

- Speak with Planck and get ready to set sail
