Descend into the depth of Somnoire, the depth of dreams. Here, chaos and noises reign. You watch the fabric of the whole place crumble before you. To the deeper dream, you go, and a faint whisper caresses your ear... In the realms of dreams, only the dream itself holds the answers we seek.

(Talk to Ivory Gatekeeper)
Ivory Gatekeeper: Meow... Ah, how come...
 Meow?

Ivory Gatekeeper: He's away from us now... Did he mention any info useful to you?

Ivory Gatekeeper: Looking for something? Indeed. In the realm of dreams, only the dream itself holds the answers we seek. He keeps saying that when he travels across the doors lately.
Ivory Gatekeeper: You must be absolutely careful while exploring the deep dream. My current power is...
 I'll be careful.
Ivory Gatekeeper: Right. I'll try my best to catch up with you.

Ebony Gatekeeper: Be careful of the obscure chunk. It's also causing repulsion from Somnoire. Is it the culprit behind the chaos?
Rover: Obscure chunk? Isn't it the Dreamless?
Ebony Gatekeeper: The Dreamless... Are we seeing the same thing? Is this yet another result of the dreams being mixed up?
Ebony Gatekeeper: Its face reflects the hidden depths of everyone's dreams. It could be a past terror or a long-held desire...

(Talk to Ebony Gatekeeper)
Ebony Gatekeeper: Can you feel it? Though everything looks sweet and inviting as always, something feels off.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Floating swim rings, some are even upside down... Which side is the real surface?
Ebony Gatekeeper: Water polo balls, metal beds, and some half-eaten dessert on the table... Let's see, one, two... six, seven. There are seven different fillings in it.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Conflicting directions, mismatched objects—all coexist in one space harmoniously. A strange experience, isn't it?
 Is it due to Somnoire's natural creativity?
Ebony Gatekeeper: Sure, fantasies and creativity are part of dreams, but Somnoire is different. It requires real memories and experiences.
 Is it caused by the chaos in Somnoire?
Ebony Gatekeeper: Yes. That's my assumption, too.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Remember the different sounds you heard? If we combine them with what we're seeing now, it seems this deep dream belongs to more than one dreamer.
Ebony Gatekeeper: They might come from different times and places, have different feelings, likes, and dislikes, and see things in various ways.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Their dreamscapes, however, are mixed together due to the anomalies in Somnoire, disrupting the usual laws and order of space.
Ebony Gatekeeper: I see you still don't quite get it. Let's move on then. In the realms of dreams, only the dream itself holds the answers we seek.

(Talk to Ebony Gatekeeper)
Ebony Gatekeeper: The disruption is getting stronger, just like when we last saw that strange thing.
Ebony Gatekeeper: The other side of the door could be way more dangerous. Do we proceed?
 In the realms of dreams, only the dream itself holds the answers we seek.
Ebony Gatekeeper: Hah, so you are starting to embrace this land of dreams. Then go push it.

(Talk to Ebony Gatekeeper)
Ebony Gatekeeper: We'll keep going through more doors and diving even deeper into the dream. Scared now?
 *Shake your head*
Ebony Gatekeeper: Good. Let's go. The culprit behind the chaos in this dream is somewhere on the other side.
Ebony Gatekeeper: In the realms of dreams, only the dream itself holds the answers we seek.
