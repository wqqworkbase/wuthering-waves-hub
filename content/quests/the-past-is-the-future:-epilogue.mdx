Dialogue[]

Shixia: Player, I have a gift for you. Please come find me when you have time. I'll be waiting for you under the tree behind the theater.

 Quest DescriptionHead to the back of theater and accept the gift Shixia has prepared for you.
(Meet Shixia behind the theater)
Shixia: Hey there. Here, this is a photo I took on the summit of West Mountain. It's a gift for you.
Shixia: I've said it before, but I truly appreciate you joining me on this mountain climb.
Shixia: Though it's a pity that we still don't know what my sister hid under this tree...
Mumin: How did you know there was something under this tree?
Mumin: I just found it the other day. It's a letter!
Shixia: What's in the letter? What's in the letter?
Mumin: Here. I have it on me.
Mumin: I can't understand the first half, but in the second half, it mentions crossing the waterfall and finding the tree behind it. It has to be about some hidden treasures!
Mumin: I wanted to go find the treasure, but I'm a little scared. Is there really a way behind the waterfall?
Shixia: Don't worry, girl. Just be confident and take a big step forward!

(Optional - Talk to Shixia again)
Shixia: Hmm... I'd better wait here for a while.
Shixia: If that girl is really going to walk through the waterfall alone, I'd better keep an eye on her.
Shixia: I did encourage her to give it a shot, after all.
Shixia: Back then, I couldn't climb the mountain safely without my sister's care and protection.
Shixia: And this girl is too young. I have to protect her, in case something happens.
Shixia: It's like I'm unknowingly standing in the shoes of my past sister...
