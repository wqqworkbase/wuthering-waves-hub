Steps[]

- Go to the match site

- Talk to Wenshu

- Learn about the participating teams

- Ask the players who completed the warm-up

- (optional) Talk to other players

- Talk to Jiyan

- Discuss strategy with team members

- Proceed to the handoff area

- Go to the handoff area to prepare for the match

- Complete the Gulpuff Championship

- Subdue Gulpuff

- Evacuate the audience

- Ask the audience assaulted by Gulpuff

- (optional) Comfort other viewers
