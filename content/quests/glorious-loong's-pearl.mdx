Glorious Loong's Pearl is a Exploration Quest that occurs in Mt. Firmament, Huanglong, [[{{{nation}}}]]. It is the first quest in the series: Mirrored Loong's Pearl.

Steps[]

- Eliminate the enemies and save the young woman

- Check on the young woman

- Feed the young woman some Milky Fish Soup

- Activate the Altar of Initiation

- Connect the Light Path with the Altar of Radiance

- Check on the Murals

- Complete Rite of Offering where light beam points

- Stand in the light and adjust the camera to align the Pearl with the Loong's mouth
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the "Feed the young woman some Milky Fish Soup" step will award the player Glazed Lamp.
