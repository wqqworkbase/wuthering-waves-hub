Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionA mysterious combat simulation program has gone viral on the Academy's Huaxu Hive. Researcher Lizhu, an active user, seems to have insights on this intriguing program...

(Talk to Lizhu)
Lizhu: Come on, Lizhu! The project deadline's coming up—you can't keep loafing around like this.
 Did something happen?
Lizhu: Oh, hi (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). You're here just in time.
 Slacking off again?
Lizhu: Nonsense! I've been collecting data all day, so I think I deserve a tiny little break.
Lizhu: Now... Have you heard of Pincer Maneuver Warriors?
 Pincer what?
 Sounds weird.
Lizhu: It's a simulation program that's gone viral on the Huaxu Hive. It simulates combat scenarios using data from digital entities and Tacet Discords.
Lizhu: You control two teams to perform a "pincer maneuver" and fight off endless waves of enemies in a limited time.
 Is this an ad?
 It's a bit awkward for an ad.
Lizhu: Anyway, that's what the anonymous uploader posted. I know, a lot of the game ads are kind of fake, but this one intrigued me. So I gave it a try after work.
Lizhu: Apparently, it's based on a tactical simulation project co-developed by the Academy and the Black Shores. They added some fun little features that make it surprisingly relaxing.
Lizhu: That's why I may have squeezed in A LITTLE free time from my research to play it. Can't blame me for this.
 So, you're slacking off again.
Lizhu: No, I'm not! Don't tell my mentor or I'll be in trouble.
Lizhu: Since you're here, I'm trusting you with my Huaxu Hive account so I can stay focused.
Lizhu: And if you're not too busy, why not try the program yourself? It's actually pretty fun! I promise!
 Alright, alright.
Lizhu: I knew I could count on you! Here, let me set up your terminal.
 If it's boring, I'm leaving a complaint on the feedback form.
Lizhu: No problem. Wait, how do you know there's a feedback form... Anyway, let me set up your terminal.
Lizhu takes your Terminal and operate on it for a while.
Lizhu: All set. Now you can log in with my account.
(Quest Complete)
