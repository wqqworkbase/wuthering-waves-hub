Dialogue[]

(Talk to Nannerl)
Nannerl: Drift away. Drift away. Take with you the world I convey...
 What's that you got there?
Nannerl: I'm making a message in a bottle. I plan to put my entire world inside of it.
 Your entire world?
 In that little thing?
Nannerl: This bottle here is more than enough to make my world blossom.
Nannerl: My younger brother and I had a passion for music since we were children. We'd practice singing and composing together every day. His talent shone bright, and he ended up joining the Order as a court musician.
Nannerl: But later, my brother confided in me his desire to chase his dreams. He boarded a Pilgrim's Sail seeking distant shores.
Nannerl: Since then, we've had no contact. When I heard the rumors about the Pilgrim's Sails, I thought...
Nannerl: It wasn't until the Carnevale, when the troupe's melodies washed over me, that I felt a sense of deep familiarity.
Nannerl: I just knew it was his voice, his music. He had become a great composer!
Nannerl: I know him better than anyone. He's always chased his dreams, and I'm so proud of him. It made me wonder...
Nannerl: It made me wonder about my own dreams long buried and a childhood lost in the tides. So, I wrote it all in this bottle.
 A bottle filled with your entire world.
Nannerl: Yes, but I have second thoughts. Should I really cast this bottle onto the sea and chase after my own dreams?
 You should go after your dreams.
Nannerl: Yes, you're right.
 I think you already know the answer.
Nannerl: I think I do want to give it to the sea.
Nannerl: Would you mind if we place it in the water together?
Nannerl: I hope it can carry my world far, far away, reaching wherever my brother is now. With that in mind, we should find a spot with a favorable wind.
 Very well.
 Let's find it together.
Nannerl: Thank you.

 Quest DescriptionIf you come across a message in a bottle, don't hesitate to check it out. Who knows, it might be one with precious dreams and hopes inside...
(Go to the beach)

(Put down the bottle)

Nannerl: Drift away. Drift away. Take with you the world I convey...
Nannerl: Along with my forgotten childhood and the dreams I abandoned.
Nannerl: Find your way to my brother, so that he may know of his sister's love.
Nannerl: And tell him, following his music, Nannerl has put her world into this bottle.
Nannerl: Now, she will pursue the dreams she once cast aside. She will continue to learn music and compose!
 Your dreams will come true.
 You're already on a path of your choosing.
Nannerl: Thank you.
Nannerl: I hope one day my brother will find this bottle.
Nannerl: Then he will realize how proud his sister is of him, and there will be a day when he will listen to Nannerl's own music.
(Quest Complete)
