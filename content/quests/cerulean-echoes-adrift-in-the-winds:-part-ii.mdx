Steps[]

- Head to KU-Key's location

- Head to the designated location via the portal

- Arrive at the designated location

- Clear the corrupted location

- Arrive at the designated location

- Interact with KU-Key to obtain clearance

- Eliminate the corrupted core

- Arrive at KU-Key's location

- Interact with KU-Key to obtain clearance

- Use KU-Key to shoot at the Data Wall and open a portal

- Eliminate the corrupted core

- Defeat the monsters

- Head to the designated location via the portal

- Arrive at Aetherfin's location

- Interact with the Aetherfin

- Complete the Aetherfin challenge

- Reach the designated location
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest awards the player the Footprints of the Shores I trophy And So Sally Can Wait.
