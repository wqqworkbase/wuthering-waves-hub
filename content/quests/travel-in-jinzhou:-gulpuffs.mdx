Steps[]

- Ask Shiyuan if he needs help

- Talk to Shiyuan for details on the challenge

- Follow the buoys back to the finish line
Gameplay Notes[]

- This is a hidden quest near the Resonance Nexus in Jinzhou.

- Completing this quest awards the Huanglong I trophy Swimming Out Till the Sea Turns Blue.
Dialogue[]

(Ask Shiyuan if he needs help)
Jumao: It's Rover! Hey Rover, I heard that Gulpuff feels ice-cold and yielding to the hand. Is that true?
Guyu: Watch this! I prepared this Gulpuff Observation Journal, with stickers on every page!
Sangyu: Woah! So cool. And you even have Researcher Baizhi's signature on the title page!!!
Shiyuan: (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;). You came right on time. I have a favor to ask.
 Who are you...?
Shiyuan: I am Shiyuan. I teach "Introduction to Tacet Discord Creatures" at Jinzhou School.
Shiyuan: Do you know "Echo Projector"?
 The machine that turns me into an Echo?
Shiyuan: Exactly! "Echo Projector" is part of this special outdoor lesson. We would ask for a Resonator to cooperate as a volunteer.
Shiyuan: A volunteer agreed to meet us here, but he has not shown up until now.
 So you're asking me to play Gulpuff?
 So you need another volunteer.
Shiyuan: Yes. We hope our students can observe and get to know TD more safely.
Shiyuan: Moreover, it seems Gulpuff is quite popular among our kids, so they kind of look forward to this lesson...
 All right. I'll help you.
Shiyuan: I have prepared compensation for the volunteer, so you won't work for anything.
Shiyuan: Come with me, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;).

(Talk to Shiyuan for details on the challenge)
Shiyuan: We will be studying the "Migration Patterns of Gulpuff" today.
Shiyuan: This is the downstream part of the river. And (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;) will play a Gulpuff swimming upstream. You will reach the destination with the guide of the buoys.
 I see.
Shiyuan: The children will observe and record the behavior patterns of Gulpuff on the bank, and compose a report after the lesson.
 Let's go!

(During the challenge)
Jumao: The Gulpuff jumped!
Sangyu: Exactly the way shown in the illustrations! Jumao, get the photograph Terminal, quick!
Guyu: Gulpuffs propel themselves with bubbles... Got it.

(At the finish line)
Sangyu: Woah. It feels so slippery.
Guyu: You were really in your element. Rover is so adept at playing Gulpuff.
Jumao: If we cook Rising Loong with Gulpuff... it would taste so delicious!
 The fabled Fried Echo?
Jumao: Don't take it so seriously, Mr. Gulpuff! We're just joking.
Sangyu: Hahaha, Mr. Gulpuff seems like a good name for Rover.
Guyu: Mr. Shiyuan! Could you take a photo of us and Mr. Gulpuff?
Shiyuan: (Shutter sound)*Click*, *click*.
You took a group photo with the school kids.
Shiyuan: Thank you for your help, (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)! The children all love you! Please take your compensation.
Shiyuan: May I turn to you again for similar sessions in the future?
 Of course, you may!
(Mission Complete)
