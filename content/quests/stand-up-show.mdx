Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionWork with Yihu

Yihu: Unfortunately, I don't have any new bits for you today.
 Doesn't matter. I just came to see you.
Yihu: Well, then you'd waste a trip. I can't allow that.
Yihu: How about this? You've seen my routine a few times now. Why don't you try to be the comic and I'll be the stooge?
 Can I really do it?
Yihu: Why not? It's harder to set up a joke than to tell the punch line.
Yihu: What do you think? Have you learned the punchlines?
 A cat working for a mouse. Now, isnt't that something?
Yihu: Exactly! The key is how you deliver it. You need to add a touch of absurdity in the way you pronounce "work for".
 A cat helping a mouse. Now, isnt't that something?
Yihu: Not bad, but a bit more practice could make it even better.
Yihu: There's another bit.
Yihu: Clouds cover the moon, people hide in shadows...
 ...don't make a sound until the cat meows thrice?
Yihu: You're such a natural.
 ...don't make a sound until the cat meows twice?
Yihu: Haha, it's okay. We can try it again next time.
