(Talk to Yhan after completing Simulation - Sealed Tube)
Yhan: Not bad. Far beyond my expectations.

(Talk to Jingzhu)
Jingzhu: Back so soon?

Jingzhu: I have no doubt that the world will soon be at your feet, youngsters!
Jingzhu: Haha, enough banter. Let's get down to the business first. The scythes of the Havoc Warrior await our sharpening!

(After upgrading Echo to Level 15)
Chixia: Whoa! It's even more impressive now! Check out those scythes&#8212;they're seriously intimidating!

Chixia: In-Instructor Yhan?

(Listen to Jingzhu's past)
Jingzhu: Ah, you still recognize this old face, Yhan.
Chixia: You two know each other?
Yhan: We do and for a long time.
Yhan: In the days of the Midnight Rangers, Mr. Jingzhu commanded my utmost respect.
Yhan: It's been a while since we last met. Mr. Jingzhu. You still look spirited... though your legs don't seem as nimble?
Jingzhu: Ahem, just a minor accident. You see, I'm getting on in years. A little slip while hiking. Nothing to worry about!
Jingzhu: I've been resting and should recover soon.
Yhan: Your injuries seem to have healed well, but don't underestimate the importance of rest.
Jingzhu: You're as talkative as ever, boy. Back in the day, we endured far worse and still fought valiantly in battles. Remember?
Yhan: But you have retired for a long time...
Jingzhu: Retired I may be, but the fire still burns within me! It's been quite some time since I retired, and opportunities to mingle with you youngsters are rare these days.
Jingzhu: And here stands Chief Instructor Yhan. You've been keeping well, I trust?
Yhan: I'm merely fulfilling my duty. And how fare your old comrades?
Jingzhu: Except for Captain and myself, they all... Ah, never mind. Let's focus on happier topics during this once-in-a-while reunion.
Jingzhu: The past is the past. When you reach my age, you'll realize that the present moment holds the most significance in life!
Jingzhu: Live in the moment and cherish each passing day. That's how we honor our comrades' sacrifices.
Yhan: Indeed, Mr. Jingzhu. The present demands our full attention.
Jingzhu: Exactly, then let's talk about the present. You know what I need now. Are you willing to lend a hand?
Yhan: You're as spirited as ever, Mr. Jingzhu.
Jingzhu: Haha, I'm truly flattered.
Yhan: These should be enough for your needs. Take them.
Jingzhu: Great, thanks.
Chixia: Instructor Yhan doesn't seem half as evil as the rumors make him out to be.

Chixia: I'm still surprised at the revelation that Grandpa Jingzhu was once a Midnight Ranger.
Jingzhu: Hmph, I'm used to keeping a low profile. Bide my time to impress Linghan.
Jingzhu: Make her fall in love with me with only one shot!
Jingzhu: With my natural charm and these finely honed scythes, I'm confident this time I'll win Linghan's heart and let her accept my Noctemint!
 Are you sure?
Jingzhu: Ahem, absolutely!
Chixia: What makes Grandpa Jingzhu so sure that having a powerful Echo will win Grandma Linghan's heart?
 Really?
Chixia: I'm with you on this one. What makes Grandpa Jingzhu so sure that having a powerful Echo will win Grandma Linghan's heart?
Jingzhu: Of course, it's not just the Echo I've prepared! Linghan once shared her criteria for an ideal partner.
Jingzhu: She said her ideal partner is a wise and valiant soldier wielding a handsome greatsword, accompanied by a majestic Echo, facing countless foes with unrivaled poise.
Jingzhu: You see? Apart from the majestic Echo, everything else fits me to a tee!
 ...
Chixia: ...
Jingzhu: Why the look? Do you lack faith in me? It's understandable, given the generation gap between us.
Jingzhu: But when it comes to matters of love, especially for us seniors, trust me. I've got this!
Jingzhu: Alright, all set and ready. Let's pay a visit to Linghan.

Chixia: Back here again, in this familiar spot.

Yingying: I even learned a new term! Late-life love!
Yingying: Anyway, Grandpa, you should go for it! I’ll go tell Grandma you’re here!
Yingying: GRANDMA!
Yingying: Grandpa Jingzhu is back with more Noctemints for you, Grandma!
Jingzhu: Oh my, oh my, kids these days are something else.

(Talk to Linghan)
Linghan: Noctemints?
Linghan: It's strange. Lately, about ten gentlemen have brought me Noctemints.
Jingzhu: It's twelve.
Jingzhu: And now I'm the thirteenth one... Hold on, those before were all me too...
Jingzhu: Remember what you told me last time? One can't judge an Echo solely from its appearance.
Jingzhu: That actually makes perfect sense! So I've honed these scythes even sharper!
Linghan: You can interpret it that way.
Jingzhu: What a relief to hear that! Alright, let me present to you what we've managed to achieve this time!
Re-release the enhanced Havoc Warrior
Jingzhu: What do you think, Linghan?
Linghan: Sorry, what was the reason for your visit again? Ah, to show me the Echo?
Yingying: NO! Grandma, he's here to give you Noctemints!
 Oh, girl...
Jingzhu: Yingying seems to be quite lively today, and that helped me a lot.
 Nice move!
Jingzhu: Yingying is certainly brimming with energy today, isn't she? That helped me a lot.
Jingzhu: I suppose I should seize this opportunity. Yes, that's right. I did come to bring you Noctemints. Would you like to spend the rest of your life with me?
Linghan: Oh dear, this is quite the headache. Thank you, but I'm not presently interested in seeking a partner. Even if I were, I'd prefer someone akin to my former lover...
Linghan: He was a wise and valiant soldier, and even his Echoes were impressive. Compared to him, everyone else falls short.
Linghan: So, feel free to ask me anything Echo-related, but let's keep everything else off the table.
Jingzhu: Alright, then... Sorry to bother you. I shall take my leave.
Jingzhu stayed put and watched Linghan go back inside and shut the door.

(Check on Jingzhu)
Chixia: It seems Grandpa Jingzhu was rejected again.
Jingzhu: ...
Chixia: Grandpa Jingzhu, are you okay? Please don't be sad... I-I'll do everything I can to cheer you up.
Jingzhu: Hmm? Sad? Not in the slightest! Even if I were, it'd be but a mere act.
Jingzhu: Thirteen failures down, but I'm not about to throw in the towel! I'll persist, be it the fourteenth, fifteenth, or beyond...
 Why are you suddenly fired up?
Jingzhu: For I've resolved to become the wise and valiant soldier that matches Linghan's ideals!
Jingzhu: I feel bad for rendering your efforts futile.
 It's okay.
Jingzhu: Thank you. You two are really kind-hearted!
 As long as you're not stingy about the payment.
Jingzhu: Don't worry. I'll ensure you receive adequate compensation.
Jingzhu: Well, this is a little embarrassing... Still, being happy is good for your health, right? At least I made someone happy. It's not too terrible.
 It was quite amusing to watch all of this unfold.
Jingzhu: Well, this is a little embarrassing... Still, being happy is good for your health, right? At least I made someone happy. It's not too terrible.
Jingzhu: Thirteen consecutive rejections... Perhaps it's time for some introspection on my approach...
Jingzhu: Perhaps, Linghan isn't used to such direct and impassioned displays of affection?
Chixia: That makes sense... Grandma Linghan does seem rather serious, so perhaps it's time to switch tactics?
Jingzhu: Hmm... What alternative approach should I try?
 Talk more about Echoes?
Chixia: That's a great idea! Start by engaging her in a topic that grabs her attention, and you might find yourself getting closer to her. Then, asking her to be your partner might just fall into place naturally!
Jingzhu: Indeed, it seems Echoes are the only thing that captures her interest.
Jingzhu: I'll have to think carefully about how to improve the Echo. Actually, my captain taught me a lot back in the day, but age has dulled my memory.
Jingzhu: Alright, let's call it a day. I need to go home and think it over. Here, this is your payment for today's efforts.
