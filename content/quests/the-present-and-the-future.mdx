Dialogue[]

Rover: (I wonder if the unrest here has spread to Ragunna. I'd better return and check it out.)

 Quest DescriptionYou return to Ragunna to see whether the aftermath of the Fabricatorium of the Deep reached the people there, but unexpectedly run into Zani in the city. Might as well talk to her to catch up with the situation.
(Head to the designated location)
Zani: No one's been affected. Yes, no worries. Leave the rest to me.
 Zani? Why are you here?
Zani: Ah, it's you. What a coincidence.
Zani: A short yet violent quake hit Ragunna not long ago. We still don't know the cause, but we're checking for casualties.
Zani: Fortunately, the damage seems minimal.
 (Tell her about the Fabricatorium of the Deep.)
Zani: That explains the commotion. I didn't expect the Order to have a research facility hidden deep underwater.
Zani: Sounds like a whole mess to clean up there.
Zani: I'll report everything to Ms. Carlotta. With the Common Echoes involved, the families, the Order... No, the entire city will need to unite.
Zani: Thank you for bringing this information to us.
 About the Journeying Paradise...
Zani: If what Fenrico said is true, the Order should make the right choice here.
Zani: But that's not something I need to worry about at my pay grade.
Zani: If the situation in Septimont is still unclear, you should go there first. Don't worry about us. Ragunna will be fine.
Black Shores Member: Finally! (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;), we've been trying to reach you, but we kept losing the connection.
Cartethyia: Are you alrigth?!
Rover: You couldn't reach me? Must've been due to the chaotic frequencies at the seabed. Relax, I'm fine.
Black Shores Member: That's a relief. You mentioned the seabed. Have you discovered anything?
 Yes, and I have something to say to Cartethyia.
Rover: You were right. The Threnodian's power at the seabed came from Mya.
Rover: It crystallized within a Gem, luring Fenrico to open a rift for the Dark Tide to seep through. It tried to escape back into the Tide to merge with the remaining Threnodian power to grow stronger.
Black Shores Member: You mean the Threnodian's Gem caused the Dark Tide? But we didn't detect any anomalous frequencies in Rinascita.
 That's the very anomaly we're investigating.
Cartethyia: That's strange. The Dark Tide at the seabed... It's different from what I've seen before. In Ragunna, it always come from the sky.
Cartethyia: Hmm... could you describe the Dark Tide you encountered in more detail?
 It was like the crack in a shattered crystal.
 It was an interior space where light and dark converged.
 Swarms of Tacet Discords emerged from it.
 Fenrico called it the Journeying Paradise.
Cartethyia: I see... I'll need some time to think it through.
Rover: Sure. I'm heading to Septimont to follow the clues Fenrico left behind.
Black Shores Member: Please take care of yourself. We'll keep you updated with any developments.
(Quest Complete)

Glossary[]

- Threnodian's Gem - Lingering power of the Threnodian Leviathan. After escaping from Mya's broken form, it concealed itself within the Ragunna gemstone on her helmet.
