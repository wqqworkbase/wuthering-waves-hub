Steps[]

- Inquire the anxious researcher about the situation

- Head to the location from which the distress signal was sent

- Defeat the guarding Exiles

- Investigate the cage

- Search for the key hidden by the Exiles

- Rescue Qiaomu

- Venture deeper into the contaminated zone with Qiaomu

- Proceed further into the contaminated zone

- Search for the missing researcher (use Sensor)

- Take the researcher out of the contaminated zone

- Head to the first contaminated zone

- Talk to Qiaomu to activate the device

- Obtain the first Offshoot Core sample

- Attack the device to activate drones

- Destroy the Offshoot Core

- Head to the second contaminated zone

- Keep moving further

- Defeat the contaminated Hoochiefs

- Head to the second contaminated zone

- Talk to Qiaomu to activate the device

- Obtain the second Offshoot Core sample

- Attack the device to activate drones

- Destroy the Offshoot Core

- Return to the campsite

- Assist Qiaomu and Senmin in making antibodies

- Talk to Senmin to make the antibody

- Pick up materials as instructed

- Catch up with Hoohoo

- Help Hoohoo

- Head to the third contaminated zone

- Talk to Qiaomu to activate the device

- Obtain the final Offshoot Core sample

- Attack the device to activate drones

- Destroy the Offshoot Core

- Head to the abandoned research institute

- Defeat the lingering Hoochiefs

- Open the gate to the research institute

- Activate the three Signals Console

- Explore the research institute

- Wait until tomorrow evening (17:00-19:00)

- Return to the campsite

- Head to the bottom of the central giant tree

- Climb to the top of the central giant tree

- Head to the bottom of the central giant tree

- Defeat the controlled Hoochiefs

- Return to your companions
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing this quest awards the Huanglong I trophy Atom: Jungle's Odyssey.
