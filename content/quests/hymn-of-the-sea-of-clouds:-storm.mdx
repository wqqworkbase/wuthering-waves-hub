Steps[]

- Enter the Forbidden Archive

- Proceed to the marked area

- Continue forward

- Touch the water crystal

- Defeat the Vitreum Dancers

- Defeat the Vitreum Dancer

- Continue forward

- Touch the water crystal

- Defeat the enemies

- Continue forward

- Touch the water crystal again

- Complete Tactical Hologram: Vitreum Dancer

- Continue forward

- Touch the water crystal again

- Challenge the Vitreum Dancer: Warden of Archive

- Chase the Vitreum Dancer: Warden of Archive

- Reach the last platform

- Defeat the Vitreum Dancer: Warden of Archive

- Retrieve the last record

- Leave the Major Forbidden Archive

- Return to the Aria Mummer

- Play the complete Nimbus Chorale
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the step "Retrieve the last record" will award the player  The Nimbus Chorale: Glory to Humanity.

- Completing this quest will award the player the Footprints in Rinascita I trophy Faith is Dead.
