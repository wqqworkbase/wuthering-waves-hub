Summary[]

You have fallen into the tangled data web of the Tethys System. To deter you from rescuing the Shorekeeper, who is on the verge of becoming its core, the Tethys System reveals three potential futures in its absence: and ice age, a cataclysmic disaster, and the total reconstruction of civilization. Once a tool for observing civilizations, this cold and calculation system now stands in open rebellion against you. The final showdown is at hand—who will prevail, you or Tethys?

The Shorekeeper and you return to reality, landing on the very beach where you first arrived in the Black Shores. She explains her deception regarding the Stellar Matrix and expresses her gratitude.
