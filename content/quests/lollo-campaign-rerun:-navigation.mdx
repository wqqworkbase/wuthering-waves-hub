Dialogue[]

 Quest DescriptionYou cross paths with Maji once more. Fresh from the Lollo Logistics Headquarters, he appears determined to relaunch the Lollo Campaign.

(Talk to Maji)
Maji: What's up, old friend? Long time no see. Looks like you didn't die from boredom while I was gone.
 Maggie!
 Well, if it isn't good old Maji...
Maji: Hahaha. All thanks to you, the "Lollo Entrepreneur Incubator Project" last time went down as smooth as butter. Even though it's not being adopted for the time being...
Maji: But visiting the headquarters and diving into some other projects was really an eye-opener for me, and so I've come up with a brand new idea.
 I'm listening.
Maji: A lot of the service pilots we prepped before operated on "independent tracks." I was thinking maybe we could consolidate them into a single "track."
Maji: To do so, we connect different services and functionalities to a certain main body, so that the former act as accessories that exist to perfect the latter.
Maji: Kinda like... the Utility module that you Resonators use. A single unit equipped with various tools for exploration.
Maji: So, are you interested in joining the trial run for the "Lollo Navigator," (&#8205;RoverThe player's chosen name for Rover&#8205;)?
 Ready when you are.
 We've been in this together for how long now?
Maji: Outstanding. I'll let you know as soon as I get a new update for the "Lollo Navigator."
(Quest Complete)
