Steps[]

- Shatter the crystal on the dragon bone

- Crystal on dragon's ridge

- Crystal on dragon's left wing

- Crystal on dragon's right wing

- Crystal on dragon's back

- Crystal on dragon's tail

- Talk to Jocaresa after completion
Gameplay Notes[]

- Completing the quest awards the hidden Exploration: Rinascita trophy Path of the Dragon.
Dialogue[]

(Talk to Jocaresa)
Jocaresa: Visitor from afar, come thou to bask in the power of my Master?
 Master?
Jocaresa: Behold! Their wings so great, they blot out the heavens! Even eons after death, Their majestic form looms still like the lofty peaks!
Jocaresa: This power. It's precisely why I call Them my Master.
 You have an odd way of speaking.
Jocaresa: Odd? Ahem... Well, it's quite clear that this is my way of showing respect for my Master... As written within the novels, you see.
Jocaresa: Wait, don't look at me like that! D-Do you not feel empowered under the presence of the mighty dragon?!
Rover: But I don't feel any frequency from it...
Jocaresa: You... Why won't you understand that size matters? Nobody these days appreciates the charisma of dragons!!
Jocaresa: Well, I digress... Though my Master is no longer with us, Their powers live on within Their remains...
Jocaresa: Locate and bring me the Remnant Energy from Their remains, and I will gladly share Their power with you.

 I didn't see anything...

 A dragon with burning wings ripping the earth apart?
Jocaresa: Ripping the earth apart... It sounds like a beast that brings nothing but destruction! Wait, it's just like that new TV series, isn't it?
 A giant hurling lightning bolts to slay a dragon?
Jocaresa: Hurling lightning bolts... Wait, isn't this from that classic foreign mythology?
Jocaresa: After all these years, there's barely any frequency left...
