Morphable Echo - Lingering Tunes is an Echo Item in Wuthering Waves. It contains one random Echo from the Lingering Tunes Sonata.
 This item doesn't show in the Backpack, instead it's used automatically upon obtainig and the random Echo is added to the Backpack's Echoes tab.
