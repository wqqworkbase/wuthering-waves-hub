The Lifer is an NPC that can be found in Oakheart Highcourt, Fagaceae Peninsula, Huanglong. It is a Chop Chop Echo trapped to play a board game eternally.

After defeating it for the first time, The Lifer can be fought again any time. Some of its buffs may be disabled by submitting a Stake of Imbalance at various Terminals around the maze. These buffs may be re-enabled at any time.
