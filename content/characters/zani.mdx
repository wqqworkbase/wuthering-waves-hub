Zani
Zani (Chinese: 赞妮) is a playable Spectro Mutant Resonator in Wuthering Waves. She is a staff member of the Montelli Family's Averardo Bank in Ragunna. As a serious and reliable Montelli employee, Zani follows a strict routine and manages tasks with ease. For years, she has clocked in on time without fail, finding as much enjoyment in her well-ordered life as in her carefully planned moments of leisure.
