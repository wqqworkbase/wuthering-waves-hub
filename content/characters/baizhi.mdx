Baizhi
Baizhi (Chinese: 白芷) is a playable Glacio Mutant Resonator in Wuthering Waves.

She is a researcher in Remnant Ecoacoustics at Huaxu Academy. Baizhi's once unfulfilled wish has now manifested as her loyal companion. The Remnant Creature You'tan is her source of healing powers, and a lifelong research focus.

Official Introduction[]

Baizhi, a researcher at Huaxu Academy, is known for her sharp intellect and precise methods.

Despite appearing aloof, she is approachable to those who seek knowledge.

Her insatiable thirst for understanding drives her to tirelessly study Remnant Energy.

She will stop at nothing until its mysteries are unraveled, even if it means dedicating her entire life to the pursuit.
