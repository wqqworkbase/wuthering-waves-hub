Jiyan 
Jiyan (Chinese: 忌炎) is a playable Aero Mutant Resonator in Wuthering Waves. 

He is the general of the Midnight Rangers selected by the sentinel of Jinzhou, Jué and is stationed in the same city.

Official Introduction[]

Jiyan is the General of Midnight Rangers stationed in Jinzhou, embracing strong sense of justice and resolution.

Born in a family devoted to medicine, Jiyan is both skilled and attentive.

Later, in a bid to fend off the encroaching Tacet Discords, he relinquished his role in medicine and took up arms as a soldier.
