Encore
Encore (Chinese: 安可) is a playable Fusion Congenital Resonator in Wuthering Waves. She is from the New Federation and a Consultant of the Black Shores. With her vivid imagination, her Wooly companions, Cosmos and Cloudy, and a penchant for crafting fairy tales, she is responsible for finding new clients for the Black Shores alongside her fellow Consultant and brother figure Aalto.

Official Introduction[]

Encore, a consultant from the Black Shores, is a quirky, lively, and spunky girl who loves to recount her friendships and adventures through fantasy and fairy tales.

She always has two Woolies with her, who are Encore's best friends and protectors.

With Cosmos and Cloudy, Encore travels all over the world!
