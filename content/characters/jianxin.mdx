Jianxin
Jianxin (Chinese: 鉴心) is a playable Aero Natural Resonator in Wuthering Waves. As a Taoist monk and successor to the art of FengyiquanFengyiquanA martial art embodying Taoist wisdom, where practitioners harmonize with nature's rhythms to develop balanced strength of body and mind. she has dedicated her life to mastering the ultimate martial art.
